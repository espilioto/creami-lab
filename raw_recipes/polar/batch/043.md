### [Cookies and Cream Ice Cream - Häagen Dazs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CW5cMUVDAPo)

Churned vanilla custard base with Oreo cream center melted in, finished with chopped and powdered Oreo biscuits — a recreation of Häagen Dazs Cookies & Cream.

**Ingredients:**

- **500g** whipping cream
- **500g** milk
- **180g** sugar
- **2** egg yolks
- **50g** Oreo vanilla cream (scraped from 1 pack of Double Stuf Oreos)
- Remaining Oreo biscuits from the same pack (chopped and powdered)
- **10g** vanilla extract
- **50g** SMP (skim milk powder)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, sugar, egg yolks, and SMP in a saucepan. Mix well.
2. Heat to 75–85°C, stirring frequently to prevent the eggs from catching.
3. Stir in the 50g of Oreo cream centers until fully melted and incorporated.
4. Remove from heat and transfer to a container. Add the whipping cream and vanilla extract. Stir to combine.
5. Refrigerate until completely chilled.
6. Churn in an ice cream machine until soft-serve consistency.
7. While churning, chop the leftover Oreo biscuits (without cream) into small pieces. Set a small portion aside and grind to a fine powder.
8. Once at soft-serve consistency, fold or machine-add the Oreo powder and cookie pieces. (If the machine is not powerful enough, stir in by hand after churning.)
9. Transfer to a tub and freeze.

**Notes:**

- The cream is scraped from a full pack of Double Stuf Oreos — this equals roughly 50g of filling; the remaining biscuit wafers are used as mix-ins.
- Nick powders 2–3 cookie halves for the dust and chops the rest into small pieces — powder tints the base darker than Häagen Dazs' white base.
- Häagen Dazs' commercial version uses only small cookie flecks with no powder, giving a noticeably whiter appearance.
- Nick's verdict: the original Häagen Dazs tasted more like plain vanilla with sparse cookie pieces; this homemade version is more heavily speckled.
- Recipe is for a standard batch (ice cream machine, not Ninja Creami).
