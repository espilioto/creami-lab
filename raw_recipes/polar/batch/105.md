### [Making Ben & Jerry's Dublin Mudslide at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyiWuqA3FDc)

Irish cream ice cream with coffee fudge swirls and double chocolate chip cookie pieces — a recreation of the retired (and revived) Ben & Jerry's flavour using Baileys Irish Cream Liqueur.

**Ingredients:**

Ice cream base:
- **340g** whole milk
- **455g** whipping cream (35% fat)
- **120g** sugar (sucrose)
- **45g** skimmed milk powder
- **50g** glucose syrup / golden syrup / corn syrup
- **100ml** Baileys Irish Cream Liqueur (note: 120ml used in video — described as too much)
- **1.3g** stabilizer (optional)

Coffee fudge swirl:
- **50g** sugar
- **50g** chocolate
- **15g** cocoa powder
- **50g** milk
- **45g** golden syrup (or corn syrup)
- **7g** coconut oil
- **10g** instant coffee
- pinch of salt

Mix-ins:
- store-bought double chocolate chip cookies (cut into ~1cm pieces), quantity to taste

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a saucepan. Add sugar and glucose syrup, then warm to 50–55°C.
2. At that temperature, whisk in the skimmed milk powder until fully dissolved. Add stabilizer if using.
3. Turn off heat and slowly stir in the whipping cream, then add the Baileys Irish Cream Liqueur. Stir well.
4. Pour into a container and chill in the fridge for 3–4 hours.
5. For the coffee fudge: combine milk and golden syrup in a pan. Turn on heat, add sugar and let it dissolve before adding cocoa powder and instant coffee.
6. Bring to a high rolling boil (this starts converting sugars to invert sugar). Once boiling hard, turn off heat and stir in the chocolate until smooth.
7. Transfer fudge to a container and set aside to cool.
8. Churn the chilled ice cream base in an ice cream machine.
9. When churning is almost complete, add the cookie pieces and let them incorporate.
10. Transfer churned ice cream to a tub, layering in generous amounts of coffee fudge swirl as you fill.
11. Seal and freeze for a few hours before serving.

**Notes:**

- 100ml Baileys is the recommended amount; the 120ml used in the video was noted as too much.
- Golden syrup is preferred over corn syrup in the fudge — it adds extra depth of flavour.
- Use a good-quality instant coffee; quality varies significantly and affects the fudge flavour.
- Cookies are store-bought double chocolate chip; Nick notes he plans to make homemade versions in a future video.
- The high rolling boil of the fudge begins converting sucrose to invert sugar, improving texture and shelf life.
- This is a traditional churned recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
