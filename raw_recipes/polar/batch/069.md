### [Ben & Jerry's Fossil Fuel Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wojgoyZk9TY)
Recreating Ben & Jerry's discontinued (2005–2010) sweet cream ice cream with fudge dinosaur shapes, a chocolate fudge swirl, and chocolate cookie pieces.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **750ml** milk
- **450ml** double cream (heavy cream)
- **300g** sugar
- **7.5g** Silk Gel (mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids) OR **45g** skimmed milk powder
- Vanilla essence or extract (to taste)

Fudge Dinosaur Shapes:
- **60g** milk chocolate (Belgian milk chocolate callets used in video)

Fudge Swirl:
- **125g** Dairy Milk chocolate
- **125g** butter
- **1** egg
- **30g** sugar
- **5g** glucose (optional)

Cookie Pieces:
- Chocolate cookies — store-bought chewy cookies (or homemade if preferred)

**Steps:**

1. Combine all base ingredients (milk, cream, sugar, Silk Gel/skimmed milk powder) in a pan and heat gently until the stabiliser is fully dissolved. Add vanilla essence or extract.
2. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, and chill for a minimum of 4 hours.
3. Melt 60g milk chocolate in the microwave. Pour into a dinosaur silicone mould, ensuring all cavities are filled. Freeze for about 1 hour, then unmould the shapes and keep them in a container in the freezer until needed.
4. For the fudge swirl: melt the chocolate and butter together in the microwave. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg and sugar together. Combine the two mixtures and whisk well. Transfer to a container, ready to swirl in during assembly.
5. Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine until done (approx. 12 minutes in a high-end machine; standard machines will take longer).
6. Transfer churned ice cream to a tub. Fold in cookie pieces (torn up), fudge swirl, and chocolate dinosaur shapes. Stir gently to distribute.
7. Freeze overnight (or at least a few hours) before serving.

**Notes:**
- The original B&J flavour was a plain sweet cream base — vanilla is an optional addition Nick adds for interest.
- Silk Gel (E471, mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids) is the stabiliser used; skimmed milk powder is given as a substitute.
- For the fudge dino shapes: if you want a softer, fudgier result rather than pure chocolate, add water at 15:1 ratio to the chocolate before melting, or add 0.5 tsp coconut oil before melting.
- Cookies were store-bought chewy chocolate cookies; use a homemade recipe if preferred.
- The glucose in the fudge swirl is optional.
- Machine used: Musso Stella 5030 (churns the full batch in ~12 minutes).
- This is a batch recipe (uses a traditional ice cream machine, not a Ninja Creami).
