### [Gelato Messina Chocolate Sorbet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ROFSwywUT_8)

Classic sorbet-style recipe using dextrose and maltodextrin for solids, with a stabilizer blend — heavy base that needs agitation before churning.

**Ingredients:**
- **650g** water
- **200g** sugar
- **60g** dextrose
- **25g** maltodextrin
- **5g** stabilizer (unspecified blend)
- **60g** cocoa powder (Valrhona used in video)

**Steps:**
1. Heat water to 40°C.
2. Add all dry ingredients (sugar, dextrose, maltodextrin, stabilizer, cocoa powder) to the 40°C water and stir to combine. Cocoa disperses better above 40°C.
3. Return to heat and bring up to 85°C, stirring until fully combined.
4. Base will be very thick (pudding-like) at this stage — whisk or blend before churning if needed.
5. Chill fully in the fridge, then churn in an ice cream machine.
6. Freeze for approx. 12 hours before scooping.

**Notes:**
- Base becomes extremely thick after heating — described as "pudding-like." Must be re-agitated (whisked or blended) before putting in the machine.
- 5g stabilizer per ~1kg batch is very high — host notes this is "insane" and attributes textural issues to over-stabilization.
- The maltodextrin is present to boost solids content and improve mouthfeel, compensating for the low-fat, water-heavy base.
- Outcome: dry, crumbly scoop with strange texture — broke apart rather than scooping cleanly. Flavor was good (Valrhona cocoa), but texture was a fail.

### [David Lebovitz Chocolate Sorbet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ROFSwywUT_8)

Famous two-stage water method with dark chocolate added after boiling — no stabilizer, rich rounded flavor, traditional scooping texture.

**Ingredients:**
- **555g** water (split: 375g + 180g)
- **200g** sugar
- **75g** cocoa powder (Valrhona used in video)
- **2g** salt (pink Himalayan)
- **170g** dark chocolate (60%)
- **3g** vanilla extract

**Steps:**
1. Combine 375g water with sugar, cocoa powder, and salt in a saucepan. Whisk together.
2. Bring to a boil and boil whilst whisking for 45 seconds.
3. Remove from heat and add the 170g dark chocolate. Stir until melted (less than 1 minute).
4. Add vanilla extract (3g) and the remaining 180g cold water to cool the mixture down.
5. Chill fully in the fridge, then churn in an ice cream machine.
6. Freeze for approx. 12 hours before scooping.

**Notes:**
- No stabilizer — relies on the chocolate itself for body and texture.
- The cold water addition at the end is a deliberate technique to rapidly cool the base; can also use cold cream in other recipes.
- Boiling the cocoa-water mixture pushes cocoa flavors forward (same principle as the host's chocolate fudge base).
- Outcome: won the showdown. Traditional glossy scoop, held together well, smaller ice crystals, more rounded flavor (cocoa + chocolate both present).
- Host verdict: "Hands down David Lebovitz chocolate sorbet is the winner."
- Both recipes work well in a Ninja Creami (sorbet program).
