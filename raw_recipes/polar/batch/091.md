### [KETO Orange Chocolate Chip Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fg5Zav7Xjww)
Keto orange ice cream (high fat, low carb) with milk chocolate chips — cold process, no heating required.

**Ingredients:**
- **264g** heavy cream
- **119g** water
- **55g** allulose
- **54g** inulin
- **5g** orange extract (Nielsen Massey brand used; or zest of half an orange)
- **5g** guar gum
- **25–50g** milk chocolate (Valrhona used; kept frozen then chopped into chips)

**Steps:**
1. Combine dry ingredients (allulose, inulin, guar gum) and mix together to break up the gum.
2. Add dry mix into wet ingredients (cream + water) in the Ninja Creami pint.
3. Add orange extract (if using zest instead, refrigerate the base for a few hours first to let orange oils infuse before freezing).
4. Stir to combine — guar gum will begin thickening the base slightly. Total base is ~500g, filling the pint to the line.
5. Seal and freeze minimum 24 hours.
6. Before spinning, chop the frozen milk chocolate into small pieces.
7. Spin on Ice Cream. While machine runs, prepare chocolate chips.
8. After spin, add chocolate chips and use Mix-In to incorporate.

**Notes:**
- Cold process recipe — guar gum works without heating. Alternatively, heat all ingredients to 80–85°C (180–185°F) with a hot-process stabilizer.
- If using orange zest instead of extract, rest base in the fridge several hours before freezing to allow orange oils to infuse; texture will be slightly firmer.
- For stronger orange flavor, increase extract slightly but reduce allulose to compensate.
- Keep chocolate in the freezer before chopping — makes it easier to cut cleanly.
- Macros (per 100g, excluding chocolate chips): Fat 17g, Carbs 4.18g, Sugar 1.8g, Fiber 0.7g, Protein 1.4g.
- Residual sugars come from lactose in the dairy (not added sugar).
