### [An Intro to Sous Vide Ice Cream + Boozy Maple Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mGVwBgd52Fk)

Maple liqueur gelato made using a sous vide for pasteurization, sponsored by Vevor chamber vacuum sealer.

**Ingredients:**

- **660g** 2% milk
- **120g** 33% whipping cream
- **40g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **126g** sugar
- **4.5g** Special Ingredients stabilizer blend (or 3.5g if using guar/xanthan alone)
- **100g** Wayne Gretzky Maple Canadian Cream liqueur (17% ABV)

**Steps:**

1. Set sous vide to 175°F (79°C) and allow water bath to heat up.
2. Combine milk and cream in a bowl or jug.
3. Add SMP to the liquid.
4. In a separate small bowl, mix sugar and stabilizer blend together dry to prevent clumping.
5. Add the sugar/stabilizer mix into the liquid and stir until dissolved.
6. Add 100g maple liqueur and stir to combine.
7. Transfer base to a vacuum bag. Add a sous vide weight to prevent floating. Vacuum seal.
8. Submerge sealed bag in the sous vide bath at 175°F for 40–50 minutes.
9. After cooking, transfer bag to an ice bath to cool rapidly.
10. Age in the fridge for at least 24 hours (longer is better — 3 days was used here).
11. Cut corner off the bag, pour base into ice cream machine, churn as normal.
12. Dish into container and freeze.

**Notes:**

- Gelato style: uses higher stabilizer levels than American ice cream due to lower fat content and more free water. For gelato, 0.2–0.4% stabilizer per kg of base is appropriate (vs. 0.1–0.2% for ice cream).
- Solids are deliberately low (~low 30s%) due to the alcohol's PAC contribution — this is as low as you want to go.
- Sugar substitution: allulose may work (reduce amount slightly). Xylitol is not recommended — it would require nearly 50% reduction and pushes solids too low (~24%), which is unacceptable.
- Alcohol: use any liqueur at approximately 14–20% ABV. Baileys is a suggested alternative. Do not exceed ~20% ABV or the freezing point will be too disrupted.
- Sous vide advantage: because the mix is sealed in a bag, no alcohol evaporates during cooking (unlike stovetop), so the PAC from alcohol stays fully intact and consistent in your recipe calculations.
- Vacuum sealer tip: do not overfill bags with liquid — the machine struggled with a full 1L; ~750–800g in a 10-inch bag worked reliably. Reduce vacuum time for liquids to minimize "boiling" during the vacuum phase.
- Place your sous vide container on a trivet or towel — sustained heat near 80°C can crack some quartz countertops.
- Stabilizer brand used: Special Ingredients (UK). If substituting with just guar or xanthan gum, reduce to 3.5g to avoid over-stabilization.
