### [Making Amazing VEGAN Häagen Dazs Strawberries & Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yZYA9L8bTFg)

Vegan/plant-based dupe of Häagen Dazs Strawberries & Cream using Carnation vegan condensed milk and Elmlea plant-based cream.

**Ingredients:**
- **500g** ripe strawberries
- **100g** sugar
- Splash of lemon juice
- **370g** Carnation vegan condensed milk
- **500g** Elmlea Plant Based cream (2 × 250ml tubs)
- **50g** glucose
- **40g** vegan milk (low-flavour type — rice milk or oat milk)

**Steps:**
1. De-stalk and chop strawberries. Add to a pan with the sugar and a splash of lemon juice. The sugar draws out the moisture; lemon juice stabilises the mixture and helps the strawberries hold their structure.
2. Heat gently until it just starts to bubble and takes on a jam/conserve texture. Remove from heat and let cool.
3. Once cooled, blend a portion of the strawberry mixture until completely smooth and fine.
4. In the pan, combine the blended strawberry base with the Carnation vegan condensed milk (it is quite stiff — warm gently to combine).
5. Add in the Elmlea Plant Based cream (both 250ml tubs) and the remaining strawberry sauce and pieces. Warm gently, stirring until nicely combined. Do not need to reach any specific temperature — just warm enough to fully combine.
6. Transfer to a container. Stir in another ~70g of the remaining strawberry mix to boost colour. Stir well.
7. Chill in the fridge for 3–4 hours until completely cold.
8. Churn in your ice cream machine to desired consistency, then transfer to a tub and freeze.

**Notes:**
- The key ingredient is the Carnation vegan condensed milk — Nick describes it as "a revelation" for vegan ice cream.
- Elmlea Plant Based cream is praised as the best cream substitute available; it comes in 250ml tubs.
- Vegan recipes using off-the-shelf condensed milk and cream tend to freeze harder than dairy equivalents — if texture is too firm, increase sugar slightly or rest in the fridge for 10–15 minutes before serving.
- Glucose (50g) and vegan milk (40g) are listed in the description ingredients but not explicitly called out by name in the video steps — include as part of the base.
- The homemade version turns out pinker/deeper in colour than the real Häagen Dazs due to no artificial colouring — that is expected and desirable.
- Strawberries are ~95% water and will always have some iciness unless soaked in glycerin or alcohol before use.
