### [Kraft Cheese Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rg-dhzZvggA)

Savory-sweet gelato made with Kraft cheese powder — an unusual flavor fusion popular in parts of Asia.

**Ingredients:**

- **436g** milk (2% fat)
- **110g** cream (33% fat / whipping cream)
- **75g** Kraft cheese powder
- **100g** sugar
- **30g** dextrose
- **1g** guar gum

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk and cream in a saucepan and gently warm on the hob.
2. Pre-mix 10g of the recipe sugar with the guar gum and set aside.
3. Add the remaining sugar, Kraft cheese powder, and dextrose to the warm milk/cream mixture. Whisk until fully dissolved.
4. Once everything is dissolved (around 52°C), turn off the heat. Add the guar gum + sugar blend, stir well for about 30 seconds.
5. Transfer to a container and refrigerate for 3–4 hours until thoroughly chilled.
6. Churn in an ice cream machine. Mix any foam back in before churning.
7. Transfer to a container and freeze.

**Notes:**

- Guar gum is a cold-mix stabilizer — no need to heat the base to a high temperature; ~52°C is sufficient to dissolve everything.
- Pre-mixing guar gum with a small amount of sugar before adding to the base helps prevent clumping.
- After chilling, the base may develop a thick foamy layer on top — stir back in before churning.
- The finished gelato is a light yellow color with a surprisingly creamy texture.
- Nick notes he personally dislikes Kraft cheese flavor, but the gelato genuinely tastes of Kraft cheese — suited for fans of the ingredient.
- Kraft cheese powder ingredients: dried whey, cheddar cheese (milk, salt, bacterial culture, modified milk ingredients, microbial enzymes, cream, calcium chloride, lipase, annatto), salt, butter, natural flavoring, paprika, turmeric, annatto extracts, citric acid, sodium phosphate. High in lactose-derived sugars (no added sugar).
