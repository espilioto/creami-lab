### [Delicious Tim Tam Ice Cream Ideas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=InG_HUJpYYY), Dimbulah Coffee, and Murray River Salted Caramel — each with its own swirl or fudge add-in. Batch recipe (3 pints).

---

**Recipe 1: Chewy Caramel Tim Tam**

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **400ml** milk
- **300ml** cream (49% fat)
- **100g** sugar
- **60g** dextrose
- **30g** dulce de leche powder (or 30g wet dulce de leche)
- **35g** skimmed milk powder (SMP)
- **1.75g** xanthan gum

**Ingredients — Tim Tam Fudge:**
- **3** Chewy Caramel Tim Tam biscuits, crushed/finely chopped
- **40g** sugar
- **50g** chocolate
- **10g** cocoa powder
- **60g** milk
- **10g** cream
- **20g** corn syrup (or golden syrup)

**Ingredients — Caramel Swirl:**
- Use the shared caramel sauce (see Recipe 3 below)

---

**Recipe 2: Dimbulah Coffee**

Coffee ice cream with chocolate and caramel notes, finished with a milk chocolate swirl.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **300ml** milk
- **300ml** cream (49% fat)
- **85g** sugar, slightly caramelised in the oven
- **30g** dextrose
- **30g** golden syrup
- **35g** skimmed milk powder (SMP)
- **7g** dark brown sugar
- **50g** Dimbulah ground coffee beans (soaked in warm milk for 30 minutes)

**Ingredients — Milk Chocolate Swirl:**
- **50g** sugar
- **50g** milk chocolate
- **5g** cocoa powder
- **50g** milk
- **15g** corn syrup (or golden syrup)
- **7g** coconut oil
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

---

**Recipe 3: Murray River Salted Caramel**

Salted caramel ice cream with a caramel swirl.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **335g** milk
- **340g** cream
- **85g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **1** egg yolk
- **60g** caramel sauce (from recipe below)
- **40g** skimmed milk powder (SMP)
- **5g** kosher salt (Nick used a touch too much — recipe adjusted down)

**Ingredients — Caramel Sauce (shared with Chewy Caramel swirl):**
- **200g** sugar
- **80g** butter
- **150g** cream
- Touch of vanilla extract

---

**Steps:**

**Ice Cream Bases (all three follow the same method):**

1. Add milk to a pan with most of the sugar; stir until dissolved.
2. Add skimmed milk powder and mix until lump-free.
3. Add remaining sugars and stabilizer (xanthan — for the Chewy Caramel base); heat to the temperature required for your stabilizer.
4. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, then add cream to rapidly bring the temperature down.
5. For the Coffee base: steep chopped Dimbulah beans in warm milk for 30 minutes, then strain through cheesecloth or a fine sieve before proceeding. Add golden syrup and dark brown sugar with the other sugars.
6. For the Salted Caramel base: add the pre-made caramel sauce and stir until fully dissolved before adding milk powder.
7. Refrigerate all bases until cold.

**Caramel Sauce:**

1. Add sugar to a pan; swirl gently over medium heat — do not stir. Keep swirling until fully melted and a deep caramel colour forms.
2. Turn off the heat and add butter carefully (it will bubble).
3. Stir until combined, then add cream (warm cream reduces splatter). Mix until smooth.
4. Store in a container; use as a swirl for both the Chewy Caramel and Murray River pints.

**Tim Tam Fudge:**

1. In a pan, combine milk, cream, sugar, and corn syrup; stir together.
2. Add cocoa powder; bring to a rolling boil, then turn off the heat.
3. Allow to calm for 1–2 minutes, then add chopped Tim Tams and stir through.
4. Store in a container until ready to use.

**Milk Chocolate Swirl (for Coffee base):**

1. Combine milk, sugar, corn syrup, coconut oil, and cocoa powder in a pan; stir together.
2. Bring to a rolling boil; turn off the heat.
3. Add chopped milk chocolate and stir until fully melted.
4. Store in a container until assembly.

**Assembly:**

1. Freeze all bases for at least 24 hours.
2. Churn/process each base according to your machine.
3. Layer or swirl add-ins as you fill each container:
   - Chewy Caramel: Tim Tam fudge swirl + caramel swirl
   - Coffee: milk chocolate swirl
   - Salted Caramel: caramel swirl

**Notes:**
- The Chewy Caramel was the fan-voted favourite from a Facebook poll — Nick made all three because he couldn't stop at one.
- Xanthan gum is used deliberately in the Chewy Caramel base to create a chewy texture mimicking the biscuit.
- Dulce de leche powder works best but wet dulce de leche (same weight) is a valid substitute — just stir it directly into the base.
- Corn syrup is not available in the UK; Nick uses golden syrup as a 1:1 substitute.
- For the caramel sauce, warm the cream before adding it to reduce violent splatter from the hot caramel.
- Boiling the fudge/swirl mixtures is essential — it changes the sugar structure so the swirl stays soft and gooey in the frozen ice cream rather than turning icy.
- Salt level in the Murray River base has been adjusted down from what Nick used in filming — the recipe as listed gives a strong but not overpowering salt punch.
- Tim Tams (Chewy Caramel flavour) cost around £8/pack in the UK — a premium ingredient worth seeking out for this recipe.
