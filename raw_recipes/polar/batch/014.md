### [Amazing Ninja Creami Baked Alaska](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZouDhyTZYFs)

Eggnog-flavored Ninja Creami ice cream layered on store-bought cake, covered in Swiss meringue, and baked at high heat until golden — a Christmas-themed showstopper dessert.

**Ingredients:**

*Ice Cream Base (single Ninja Creami pint):*
- **380g** milk
- **430g** whipping cream (33% fat)
- **55g** skim milk powder
- **90g** white sugar
- **40g** dark brown sugar
- **3g** nutmeg
- **3g** cinnamon
- **1g** guar gum
- **2 tsp** homemade vanilla bean paste (or vanilla extract)

*Meringue:*
- **3** egg whites
- **100g** white sugar
- **¼ tsp** cream of tartar
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract

*Assembly:*
- Store-bought marble cake (e.g. Safeway), 2 slices as the base

**Steps:**

1. Combine nutmeg, cinnamon, and guar gum with the sugars and stir together.
2. Add the spice/sugar/gum mix and skim milk powder to the milk. Microwave in two stages — 1 minute, stir, then up to 30 more seconds — until sugars are fully dissolved with no crystals remaining.
3. Add the whipping cream and vanilla bean paste. Stir to combine.
4. Pour into the Ninja Creami pint, freeze overnight (minimum 24 hours).
5. Process the frozen pint on the Ice Cream setting.
6. While the pint is spinning, preheat oven to 450°F (232°C) with the shelf on the lowest rack position.
7. Make the meringue: whip egg whites with a balloon whisk attachment. Mix cream of tartar into the 100g white sugar, then add the sugar mixture to the egg whites gradually, bit by bit, until fully dissolved and glossy. Add vanilla extract and whip to stiff, glossy peaks.
8. Line a baking pan with parchment paper. Place two slices of store-bought cake on the pan side by side, joined with a dab of meringue.
9. Scoop the spun Ninja Creami ice cream on top of the cake base (double-bowl style).
10. Pipe or spread meringue over the entire surface — top, sides, and all gaps — ensuring no ice cream is exposed. Use a piping bag with a round nozzle.
11. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for approximately 5 minutes, watching closely. Remove when the meringue is browned nicely.
12. Serve immediately.

**Notes:**
- Nick kept sugar slightly lower in the ice cream base specifically so it wouldn't go too soft during the brief oven blast — important for structural integrity in a Baked Alaska.
- The meringue acts as an insulating layer — it must completely seal the ice cream with no gaps or holes or the heat will melt it.
- Nick skipped the traditional step of re-molding the ice cream and freezing it a second time in a bowl shape, and it still worked.
- The Ice Cream setting is equivalent to the Gelato setting on this machine.
- Nick used Safeway marble cake as a convenient store-bought base — any pound cake or sponge cake works.
- This is a Christmas/holiday recipe — the cinnamon and nutmeg give an eggnog flavor profile.
