### [Salt & Straw Salted Malted Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRbNJEJ5hrg)

Exact recreation of Salt & Straw's salted malted chocolate chip cookie dough ice cream — churned base layered with malted cookie dough chunks and malted chocolate fudge.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **100g** sugar
- **2 Tbsp** milk powder
- **½ tsp** salt
- **¼ tsp** xanthan gum
- **320ml** whole milk
- **2 Tbsp** light corn syrup (glucose in UK)
- **1 tsp** molasses (black treacle in UK)
- **320ml** heavy (double) cream
- **2 tsp** vanilla extract

**Ingredients — Malted Cookie Dough:**
- **60g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **2 Tbsp** sugar
- **55g** brown sugar or muscovado sugar
- **1 tsp** salt
- **2 Tbsp** heavy (double) cream
- **1 tsp** molasses
- **1 Tbsp** corn syrup (golden syrup in UK)
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **60g** all-purpose flour (plain in UK), heat-treated (baked at 180°C / 350°F for 10 minutes)
- **2 Tbsp** malt powder
- **45g** semi-sweet chocolate chips

**Ingredients — Malted Fudge:**
- **1 Tbsp** cold water
- **2 tsp** malt powder
- **110g** corn syrup
- **120ml** heavy cream
- **1 Tbsp** unsalted butter
- **⅛ tsp** kosher salt
- **1 Tbsp** cocoa powder
- **85g** 80% dark chocolate, chopped

**Steps:**
1. **Ice cream base:** Whisk together sugar, milk powder, salt, and xanthan gum in a bowl until well combined (distributes the xanthan gum evenly).
2. In a pot over medium heat, combine the milk, corn syrup, and molasses; whisk until combined. Add the dry mixture and whisk until lump-free. Cook 2–3 minutes until sugar dissolves, then remove from heat.
3. Pour into a large container, add cream and vanilla. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours before churning. Churn 15–30 minutes depending on your machine.
4. **Malted cookie dough:** Cream together butter, both sugars, and salt with a spoon until combined. Add cream, molasses, corn syrup, and vanilla; mix. Add heat-treated flour and malt powder; mix until no lumps. Fold in chocolate chips.
5. Spread dough onto a parchment-lined tin and freeze for at least 1 hour until solid. Remove and chop into small cubes; return to freezer until needed.
6. **Malted fudge:** In a small pot, combine water and malt powder. Add corn syrup, heavy cream, butter, and salt. Heat until butter is fully melted and mixture just comes to a boil, then remove from heat. Add cocoa powder and chopped chocolate; whisk until smooth and chocolate is fully melted. Transfer to an airtight container and store in fridge.
7. **Assemble:** Layer churned ice cream, malted fudge, and cookie dough chunks into the storage tub — don't skimp on the mix-ins. Cover and freeze at least 3 hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- Recipe is a direct copy from Salt & Straw (published in their cookbook/online).
- Heat-treating the flour (180°C for 10 min) kills any raw flour bacteria — required for safe no-bake cookie dough.
- Malt powder appears in both the cookie dough and the fudge — key flavor element throughout.
- Churn time varies: Nick got 15–20 min, but can be up to 30 min depending on the machine.
- Freeze assembled tub at least 3 hours before eating for best texture.
