### [Lemon Meringue Pie Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9jLluMgclC4)
Updated remake of Nick's first-ever YouTube recipe: lemon ice cream base with buttery baked graham crumb cubes and a vanilla Swiss meringue swirl.

**Ingredients:**

Base:
- **592g** whole milk (3.25%)
- **582g** heavy cream (33–36% fat; if using UK double cream, reduce to 500g)
- **230g** sugar
- **24g** invert sugar / corn syrup / glucose syrup
- **65g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **6–10g** lemon extract (adjust to taste; Nick used ~10g)
- Zest of ½ lemon (steeped in base during cooking; Nick recommends leaving it in)
- Locust bean gum (amount not specified — mixed with the last 10g of sugar before adding)

Graham pie crust add-in:
- **200g** honey graham crumbs
- **100g** butter, melted

Swiss meringue swirl:
- **100g** egg whites (carton egg whites work fine)
- **100g** sugar (from the 230g total — 10g held back for this; Nick used 100g separately)
- **30g** corn syrup / glucose syrup
- **1.5g** cream of tartar (~¼ tsp; citric acid works as a substitute)
- 1–2 tsp vanilla extract (stirred in after whisking)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, 220g sugar, corn syrup, and SMP in a saucepan. Mix the locust bean gum with the reserved 10g sugar, then whisk into the base. Add lemon zest.
2. Heat base to 85°C (185°F), hold for ~5 minutes, then remove from heat.
3. Cool base to ~60°C, then stir in lemon extract (add off-heat to preserve volatile aromatics).
4. Transfer base to fridge overnight to mature and allow lemon oils from the zest to develop.
5. Make the graham crust: combine graham crumbs with melted butter (microwave to melt). Press into a foil-lined pan, flatten down. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 5–8 minutes. Cool completely to room temperature, then cube and refrigerate.
6. Make the Swiss meringue: combine egg whites, sugar, corn syrup, and cream of tartar in a heatproof bowl. Whisk together off heat, then place over a bain-marie (simmering water). Heat to 70–75°C, whisking continuously. Remove from heat and whisk with a hand mixer for 15–20 minutes until cool, pillowy, and marshmallow-like (~21°C). Stir in vanilla extract.
7. Churn the ice cream base in your machine.
8. Towards the end of the churn, add graham cracker cubes directly if your machine can handle it; otherwise fold in by hand after loading.
9. Layer into containers: ice cream, then vanilla Swiss meringue swirl, then more ice cream. Repeat until full. Top with remaining graham cracker cubes.
10. Freeze.

**Notes:**
- This is a batch recipe — yield is multiple pints (base alone ~1.5 kg before churn).
- Nick notes the base is ~14% fat — a rich, luxurious result.
- Two lemon sources (zest + extract) give a more natural, balanced lemon flavour than either alone. If using only zest, use a full lemon's worth and steep during cooking; if cold-process, infuse zest in the milk for at least 1 hour before mixing.
- Check lemons for wax coating (run fingernail over skin — wax comes off white); wash under warm water if present.
- Store-bought meringues turn to mush in ice cream — do not substitute. The Swiss meringue here stays stable due to the cooked egg whites and glucose syrup; the glucose gives it a marshmallow-like texture that holds in the frozen base.
- Lemon extract brand mentioned: Nielsen-Massey (available at Sainsbury's and Tesco in the UK).
