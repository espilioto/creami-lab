### [Creamy Vegan Pistachio Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mGG6_r29I8c)

Vegan pistachio ice cream using Elmlea plant-based double cream and oat cream, with homemade pistachio paste and a hint of almond extract. Traditional churned recipe.

**Ingredients:**
- **250ml** Elmlea plant-based double cream
- **250ml** oat cream
- **200g** water
- **180g** sugar
- **5g** corn syrup
- **80g** pistachio paste (homemade — see steps below)
- **2ml** almond extract (≈ ¼ tsp)
- **20g** chopped pistachios (to fold in at end of churn)

**Steps:**
1. Toast pistachios in the oven at 150°C for 6–8 minutes. Watch carefully — do not burn. Remove and let cool for 5–10 minutes.
2. Add 80g of the toasted pistachios to a small blender. Pulse first, then blend on high into a super smooth paste. This can take up to 15 minutes. If the pistachios won't come together, add a small amount of simple syrup (or water/oil) a little at a time and keep blending until smooth.
3. In a pan, combine the water, sugar, and corn syrup. Heat gently just to dissolve the sugars.
4. Add 250ml oat cream and 80g pistachio paste to the pan. Gently warm through. The mix will not be smooth at this stage — that's normal.
5. Remove from heat. Use an immersion blender (or a balloon whisk) to emulsify the mixture until smooth and homogeneous.
6. Once properly emulsified, add the 250ml Elmlea vegan cream and 2ml almond extract. Stir together well.
7. Refrigerate the base for several hours until fully chilled.
8. Churn in an ice cream machine. Right at the end of the churn (soft-serve stage), fold in the 20g chopped pistachios.
9. Transfer to a tub and freeze.

**Notes:**
- Elmlea plant-based double cream is Nick's top recommendation for vegan cream. If unavailable, try another plant-based double cream alternative and report back.
- You can add liquid (water, simple syrup, or oil) to nut paste while blending — ignore advice saying otherwise. Add slowly and keep blending until it comes together.
- Batch recipe (not single-pint) — yields approximately 700–750ml depending on churn overrun.
- Almond extract complements pistachio strongly — don't over-do it.
