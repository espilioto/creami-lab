### [Chasing the Perfect Turkish Ice Cream (Part 2)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZSZ02uH91U8)

Attempt at Turkish dondurma (stretchy ice cream) without salep or mastic, using locust bean gum and guar gum — a chef's internet recipe that Nick tested unmodified and found deeply disappointing.

**Ingredients:**
- **335g** whole milk
- **335g** heavy cream
- **100g** sugar
- **3g** locust bean gum (lo auasal / gallan gum) — G&G brand used
- **3g** guar gum — G&G brand, flavor-free version recommended
- **1 pinch** salt
- **5g** vanilla extract

**Steps:**
1. Pre-chill your ice cream machine bowl.
2. Combine milk and cream in a mixing bowl.
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together sugar, locust bean gum, guar gum, and salt until evenly mixed.
4. Add the dry mix to the liquid and whisk vigorously for 2 minutes.
5. Add vanilla extract (Nick forgot initially and added it after pouring into the machine — either step works).
6. Churn in a traditional ice cream machine until it reaches soft-serve consistency similar to the original salep-based version.
7. Transfer to a pre-chilled bowl and whip for a further 2 minutes to develop any stretch texture.
8. Decant into a freezer container and freeze for at least 2 days.

**Notes:**
- This is a **failed recipe** — Nick tested it as-is without adjustments and explicitly says not to waste time or money making it. It is icy, dry, and crumbles apart in shards rather than scooping cohesively.
- Root cause: locust bean gum requires heat activation to function properly; this recipe uses no heat, so the gum is wasted. The guar gum level is also insufficient to produce meaningful stretch on its own.

- Part 3 (Nick's own corrected dondurma recipe) is planned for a later video.
- This is a **traditional ice cream machine** recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
- The original recipe was sourced from a chef's online class; Nick intentionally withholds the source to avoid calling them out publicly.
