### [Oreo Space Dunk Mint Cookies 'n' Cream Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=maijkLNh2Ek)

Double-color (pink + blue) mint cookies and cream ice cream made with limited-edition Oreo Space Dunk cookies (cosmic cream with popping candy).

**Ingredients (per batch — recipe is made as 2 batches):**
- **750g** coffee cream (18% fat)
- **160g** sugar
- **30g** dextrose
- **50g** skimmed milk powder
- **2g** stabilizer blend (optional)
- Mint extract (to taste — added after heating)
- Pink gel food coloring (batch 1) / Blue gel food coloring (batch 2)
- Oreo Space Dunk cookies — cookie parts only, crushed to powder (mix-in)
- Marshmallow — for topping (optional, blowtorch-toasted)

**Steps:**
1. Combine cream and milk in one vessel; combine sugar, dextrose, and stabilizer blend in another; add skimmed milk powder to the dry mix.
2. Combine dry ingredients into the cream/milk mixture and stir well.
3. Heat on the hob, stirring, to 80–85°C (180°F) if using a stabilizer. If not, just warm enough to dissolve the sugars.
4. Remove from heat and allow to cool.
5. Add mint extract after the heating phase (or once fully cooled for strongest flavor). Add gel food coloring — pink to one batch, blue to the other.
6. Chill both bases in the fridge until cold.
7. Crush the cookie parts (no cream) of the Oreo Space Dunk cookies into a coarse powder.
8. Churn each base separately in your ice cream machine. Add crushed cookie pieces during or after churning.
9. Layer scoops of pink and blue ice cream in serving bowls for the double-color effect.
10. Top with marshmallow (store-bought or homemade) and blowtorch to toast.

**Notes:**
- This is a 2-batch recipe — both batches use identical bases, differentiated only by food coloring color.
- Use gel food coloring designed to work in fat if possible; water-based colorings will also work.
- Always add extracts after the heating phase to preserve flavor and alcohol; adding before heating loses both.
- The Space Dunk cookies are marketed as marshmallow-flavored but taste more like standard vanilla — the mint base and toasted marshmallow topping compensate.
- The popping candy in the cookies comes through noticeably in the finished ice cream.
- Use cookie parts only (scrape/discard the cream filling) for the mix-in to keep texture clean.
- Fat-based gel food colorings give more vivid results in a cream-heavy base.
