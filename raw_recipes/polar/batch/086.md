### [Häagen-Dazs Belgian Chocolate Ice Cream (Custard Base)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnXYz8YixeY)

Custard-base dupe of Häagen-Dazs Belgian Chocolate, churned in a traditional ice cream machine with a chocolate chunk add-in.

**Ingredients:**

- **250ml** milk @ 1.8% fat
- **200ml** cream @ 49% fat (double/heavy cream)
- **100g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **2** egg yolks (UK medium)
- **10g** cocoa powder @ 24% fat (Callebaut used in video; Seven Hills recommended for closer HD match)
- **40g** 70% chocolate (melted into base; can reduce to 25–30g)
- Extra 70% chocolate for add-in (amount to taste), frozen ahead of time

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a pan. Add sugar and dextrose; stir to combine.
2. Add egg yolks and begin heating over medium heat.
3. At around 50°C, whisk in the cocoa powder.
4. Continue heating to 77°C (ensures eggs are cooked; skip if using pasteurised eggs, though heating helps custard consistency).
5. Remove from heat. Add the 40g of 70% chocolate and stir well until fully melted.
6. Decant into a container, add the 200ml cream, and stir well.
7. Refrigerate for several hours until fully chilled.
8. Meanwhile, place the add-in chocolate in the freezer — frozen chocolate is much easier to chop finely.
9. Churn base in an ice cream machine for 15–30 minutes until soft-serve consistency.
10. While churning, chop the frozen add-in chocolate as finely as possible (a grater works well).
11. At the end of the churn, add the chopped chocolate into the machine and let it incorporate.
12. Transfer to a tub and freeze for several hours before scooping.

**Notes:**

- This is a traditional churned recipe (not a Ninja Creami recipe).
- Nick considers his earlier eggless version (https://youtu.be/WMqQvMxHbzE) a closer match to actual Häagen-Dazs — the custard base version is very good but not as accurate.
- The cocoa powder brand makes a significant difference: Seven Hills cocoa powder produced an almost identical flavour profile to Häagen-Dazs in the eggless version; Callebaut was used here and is noticeably different.
- Dextrose can be substituted with table sugar, though it will have a slightly different effect on scoop texture.
- Chop add-in chocolate as fine as possible for the best distribution; grating works well.
