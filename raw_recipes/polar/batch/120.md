### [Oreo Gelato (No-Cook / Cold Process)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhlhEbkB-rM)
Cold process cookies and cream gelato — no heating required, ready to churn in under 10 minutes.

**Ingredients:**
- **600g** 2% milk
- **150g** whipping cream (33%)
- **165g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **65g** skimmed milk powder
- **1.7–2.5g** xanthan gum (Nick used ~2.5g)
- **8 Oreos** (cookie only, no cream filling) — crushed to powder, mixed into base during churn
- Broken Oreo pieces — to taste, added as mix-ins

**Steps:**
1. Pre-mix the dry ingredients first: combine sugar, dextrose, and xanthan gum together in a small bowl. Mixing the stabilizer with the sugars prevents clumping and ensures even distribution.
2. Add skimmed milk powder to your main mixing bowl, then pour in the 600g milk and 150g cream.
3. Add the sugar/dextrose/xanthan gum mixture to the bowl.
4. Whisk thoroughly for 5–6 minutes until everything is fully dissolved — no graininess at the bottom. Test by spooning from the bottom of the bowl.
5. Refrigerate the base while preparing the Oreos (just a few minutes is fine).
6. Separate the Oreo cookies from their filling (discard or snack on the cream). Place the cookie wafers in a zip-lock bag, expel the air, and crush into a fine powder.
7. Optionally, reassemble some Oreos with double filling (two cream halves together) to use as whole mix-in pieces.
8. Churn the base in a pre-chilled ice cream machine. When the base starts to come together, add the Oreo powder. Continue churning for another 5–6 minutes until done.
9. Transfer to a tub. Press broken Oreo pieces into the gelato as mix-ins.
10. Serve immediately or freeze. Note: gelato is ideally served at -10°C to -12°C — standard home freezers (-18°C) will set it firmer, so allow a short temper before scooping.

**Notes:**
- Cold process works here because all ingredients (xanthan gum, skimmed milk powder, sugar, dextrose) hydrate and dissolve without heat.
- Crushing Oreo wafers to powder turns the gelato a grey-brown colour — not the most attractive but delivers strong cookie flavour throughout.
- Use only the Oreo wafer (no filling) for the powder component; the cream is too sweet and fatty to incorporate evenly.
- The "double-stuffed" trick: press two cream halves together on one wafer before crushing — makes chunkier, richer pieces if using broken Oreos as mix-ins.
- Nick notes scales are essential for xanthan gum — never measure stabilizers by spoon volume alone.
