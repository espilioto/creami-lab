### [Vegan Häagen Dazs Belgian Chocolate Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ar1GkLDvjVs)

Plant-based Belgian chocolate ice cream using Elmlea cream and Carnation plant-based condensed milk. Traditional churn recipe.

**Ingredients:**
- **300g** almond milk
- **500g** Elmlea plant-based cream
- **50g** dextrose
- **60g** 55% chocolate
- **50g** cocoa powder (good quality)
- **370g** plant-based condensed milk (Carnation — NOT coconut)
- **1.5g** stabilizer blend (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Combine almond milk, dextrose, and cocoa powder. Heat gently to dissolve cocoa.
2. Add chocolate, melt until smooth.
3. Stir in plant-based condensed milk and Elmlea cream.
4. If using stabilizer, heat to activation temp.
5. Chill completely (overnight preferred).
6. Churn in ice cream machine.
7. Transfer to tubs and freeze.

**Notes:**
- DO NOT use coconut condensed milk — Nick is emphatic it ruins the flavour.
- The Elmlea + Carnation plant-based condensed milk combo is what makes this work — described as "a revelation."
- No one would know this is vegan from taste alone.
