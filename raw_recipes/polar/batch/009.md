### [Amazing Almond Joy Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BCof8Vq1kD8)
Coconut ice cream base with desiccated coconut, dark chocolate stracciatella, and a crackle top — inspired by the Almond Joy / Bounty candy bar. Batch recipe made in a traditional churn, with a Ninja Creami comparison.

**Note: Batch recipe — large yield (~2000g+), scale down as needed. Also demonstrated in Ninja Creami.**

**Base ingredients:**
- **900g** coconut milk (~17% fat)
- **561g** coconut cream (~21% fat)
- **225g** plain sugar
- **112g** whipping cream (~33% fat)
- **50g** glucose syrup (GL glucose 40 DE) or corn syrup
- **56g** allulose + monk fruit extract blend (sweetener)
- **95g** skim milk powder
- **2g** cold process stabiliser (gum blend)

**Add-ins:**
- Desiccated coconut / shredded coconut (generous amount — key to texture)
- Toasted whole almonds (for the Almond Joy garnish on top)

**Crackle topping:**
- 80% dark chocolate (or milk chocolate — to taste)
- Small amount of coconut oil (deodorised or neutral) — just enough to slow the freeze slightly

**Steps:**
1. Combine coconut milk and coconut cream in a pan. Warm gently to 40–50°C to emulsify the fats together into a homogeneous liquid.
2. Warm the glucose syrup briefly in the microwave to loosen it (it is very thick/sticky), then add to the warm coconut mixture and stir in.
3. Add the allulose/monk fruit blend and plain sugar to the warm liquid — the residual heat helps dissolve the solids.
4. Add the whipping cream and stir to combine.
5. Add the skim milk powder. Stir well.
6. Sprinkle in 2g of cold process stabiliser (gum blend) over the powder on top, then stir everything together until fully dissolved and smooth.
7. Refrigerate for several hours (minimum 4h, or overnight) until thoroughly chilled before churning.
8. While the base chills: melt the dark chocolate with a small amount of coconut oil in the microwave until smooth. Set aside for use during churn and as crackle topping.
9. Toast whole almonds if desired and set aside.
10. Churn the cold base in a traditional ice cream machine. In the final minutes of churning, drizzle in the melted chocolate mixture — it will form stracciatella shards as it hits the cold ice cream.
11. Load generous amounts of desiccated coconut in with the churned ice cream and fold through.

**For Ninja Creami version:**
1. Fill the Creami pint with the prepared (unchurned) cold base — no add-ins yet.
2. Freeze solid (24h+).
3. Process on Ice Cream mode.
4. After processing, fold in desiccated coconut using a spoon, pressing down to close the top back over.
5. Top with melted chocolate crackle (chocolate + small amount of coconut oil).

**Serving (both versions):**
- Scoop ice cream into bowl.
- Top with a whole toasted almond.
- Pour over melted chocolate crackle and allow to set on top.

**Notes:**
- This recipe took Nick over 2.5 years to perfect — the fat balance and coconut texture are critical, not just a garnish.
- Coconut ice cream is texturally different to dairy-based ice cream; the dairy (whipping cream, skim milk powder) is essential to get the right consistency — pure coconut alone gives poor texture.
- Make sure to match the fat percentages of your coconut milk and cream. If your brand is lower fat, the texture will suffer.
- Glucose syrup (GL40 DE) is used specifically to avoid increasing sweetness while adding anti-freeze body. Corn syrup works as a substitute and is much easier to pour.
- Cold process stabiliser is used intentionally to avoid heating the base to high temperatures (which would cook off coconut aroma).
- For the Ninja Creami version, the desiccated coconut will be slightly firmer/harder (less time to absorb moisture from the dairy) — Nick considers this a different but acceptable result.
- The Ninja Creami blade decimates large chocolate shards — they become tiny fragments, not the large stracciatella pieces possible in a traditional churn. Still tastes excellent.
- Crackle top tip: more coconut oil in the chocolate = slower freeze on the ice cream. Chocolate alone (no oil) freezes very fast. Don't overdo the oil.
- Any neutral oil works for the crackle (coconut oil, olive oil, etc.). Since this is coconut ice cream, coconut flavour in the oil is irrelevant.
- Both Almond Joy (US) and Bounty (Europe) are essentially the same bar — coconut filling in chocolate. Almond Joy adds an almond on top. Nick used 80% dark chocolate; substitute milk chocolate if preferred.
