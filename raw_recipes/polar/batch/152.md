### [The Best Vegan Black Forest Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-y5XWXW7oxI)
Vegan chocolate ice cream base churned with homemade cherry agar jellies and chopped vegan biscuits, inspired by Australian Cadbury Dairy Milk Black Forest chocolate.

**Ingredients:**

Ice cream base:
- **250g** Oatly oat cream (single cream alternative)
- **250g** Elmlea Plant Based Cream
- **250g** Alpro Soy Based Cream
- **50g** coconut oil
- **100g** UHT soy milk
- **130g** sugar
- **60g** dextrose
- **40g** cocoa powder (Twinings/Whittard's brand mentioned)
- **2g** xanthan gum

Cherry jellies:
- **125g** cherry juice (from canned pitted black cherries) — or 125ml water + cherry extract + red food colouring
- **100g** sugar (may need a little more to taste)
- **3g** agar agar powder

Mix-ins:
- Vegan biscuits (shortbread-style, e.g. Sainsbury's Free From range), chopped — quantity to taste

**Steps:**

1. Pour UHT soy milk into a saucepan. Add sugar, dextrose, coconut oil, cocoa powder, and xanthan gum. Warm and combine until smooth and thick.
2. Pour base into the ice cream machine container and chill in the fridge for a few hours.
3. Meanwhile, drain the liquid from a can of pitted black cherries into a saucepan. Taste — add cherry extract and more sugar if desired.
4. Add agar agar powder to the cherry juice. Warm and stir until fully dissolved and emulsified.
5. Pour into small molds (a coffee bean mold works well for size). Leave to begin setting, then transfer to the fridge until fully gelled.
6. When the base has chilled and thickened, whisk it well to loosen before churning.
7. Churn the base in an ice cream machine as normal.
8. Once churned, layer the ice cream into tubs with the popped cherry jelly pieces and chopped vegan biscuits.
9. Freeze for 3–4 hours until hardened.

**Notes:**
- Nick notes this recipe has a high ingredient cost due to using all three plant-based creams simultaneously; in practice you can use just one (Elmlea preferred) as your standard vegan base and still get the same result.
- The base becomes very thick after chilling — whisk vigorously to loosen it before churning.
- Canned black cherry juice provides strong, natural cherry flavour; cherry extract can supplement if needed.
- Agar agar is fully vegan and sets firm enough to pop out of molds cleanly.
- Use any vegan shortbread-style biscuit; brand is unspecified (packaging discarded before filming).
- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
