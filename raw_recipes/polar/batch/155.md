### [The ULTIMATE Chocolate Ice Cream recipe works in Ninja Creami too](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u5hYG3yjZ0I)

Full-fat traditional churn base using premium dutched cocoa + milk chocolate, golden syrup for caramel depth, and a stabilizer blend — works in Ninja Creami with no adjustments.

**Ingredients:**

- **450g** 2% milk
- **550g** whipping cream (33–35% fat)
- **140g** sugar
- **44g** dextrose
- **10g** golden syrup (corn syrup works as substitute)
- **40g** skim milk powder (0.1% fat — not whole milk powder)
- **50g** cocoa powder (Ghirardelli dutched recommended; also: Cacao Barry, Valrhona, Guittard Rouge)
- **40g** milk chocolate (Valrhona Taïnori 33% cocoa solids used; or any high-quality milk chocolate)
- **2g** stabilizer blend (Nick's own "422" blend with emulsifier)
- **3g** lecithin — sunflower or soy (optional but recommended for best result)
- **2g** vanilla extract

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, and golden syrup in a saucepan.
2. Mix skim milk powder and sugar together, add to the dairy.
3. Combine stabilizer, dextrose, and lecithin (if using) together, then stir into the dairy mix.
4. Add cocoa powder and stir to incorporate (will look mottled — that's fine).
5. Add milk chocolate pieces (no need to chop; they melt as it heats).
6. Heat on the hob, stirring, to 85°C (185°F). Do not boil. Target 3–5% evaporation by weight — weigh before and after heating.
7. Remove from heat. Allow to cool to ~60°C (~140°F), then stir in vanilla extract (adding it while still too hot drives off the alcohol and the flavor).
8. Cool with lid on for 30 minutes at room temp, then refrigerate minimum 12 hours (overnight recommended — aging develops flavor).
9. Churn in ice cream machine. For Ninja Creami: freeze standard pint, use Ice Cream program.

**Notes:**

- This is a full-fat recipe — not low-cal. Nick explicitly states it's not low-fat.
- Golden syrup is the key flavor differentiator — its caramel notes build the "ultimate" profile. Available at Walmart, Safeway, any UK supermarket.
- Ghirardelli dutched cocoa gives a milk chocolate profile; combined with actual milk chocolate you get layered chocolate depth — two different milk chocolate flavor profiles in one base.
- High-quality cocoa is non-negotiable: Cacao Barry, Valrhona, Guittard Rouge all excellent. Cheap cocoa is the #1 reason commercial chocolate ice cream disappoints.
- Lecithin (sunflower or soy) acts as an emulsifier — same function as egg yolk lecithin. Chocolate already contains some lecithin, so it's optional, but adds texture quality.
- Vanilla goes in off the heat (~60°C) — above 80°C alcohol evaporates and takes the flavor with it.
- Aging (minimum 12h fridge) is called "super important" — flavors combine and intensify.
- Chocolate ice cream goes hard due to extra fats and the cocoa powder absorbing water; stabilizers and specific sugars (dextrose) counter this.
- Batch size: full churn batch — scale down to pint for Ninja Creami.
