### [Amazing Chocolate Ice Cream using Lindor Balls](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QBwWj3UE38)

Lindor milk chocolate balls melted into a Guittard cocoa powder base — silky, milk-chocolate-forward, no add-ins. Batch recipe (~1.5L, not single-pint).

**Ingredients:**
- **586g** milk (2%)
- **400g** heavy/double cream
- **40g** cocoa powder (Guittard recommended)
- **135g** Lindt Lindor balls, original milk chocolate (12 balls)
- **100g** sugar
- **151g** dextrose
- **55g** skimmed milk powder
- **1.3g** stabilizer (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Add milk to a pan over heat. Add sugar and dextrose, warming until dissolved.
2. Add cocoa powder and stir in well until fully incorporated with no lumps.
3. Add skimmed milk powder and stir until completely dissolved.
4. Turn off the heat. Add the Lindor balls and stir slowly until fully melted and the mixture looks silky smooth.
5. Transfer to a container. Add the cream and give a final mix.
6. Chill in the fridge for several hours until fully cold.
7. Once chilled, give one final stir, then churn in an ice cream machine for 12–15 minutes (longer for a large batch).
8. Transfer to a freezer container and freeze for 3–4 hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- Batch recipe — description amounts are for a 1–1.5L base. Scale up or down as needed.
- Chocolate ice cream freezes harder than most — the high dextrose load compensates for this and gives a better scoop from the freezer.
- Guittard cocoa powder gives a silkier, milk-chocolate-leaning flavour rather than a dark, punchy result. Nick rates it just behind Valrhona, but the style is different — pair it with Lindor for best results.
- Stabilizer is listed as optional — 1.3g is a small dose for this volume.
- No add-ins in this recipe; Lindor is fully melted into the base rather than used as a mix-in.
- Scoops directly from the freezer without waiting — described as "just the right density."
