### [Chunky Monkey Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sEjTGBYrgRY)
Ben & Jerry's Chunky Monkey dupe: banana ice cream base steeped with roasted bananas, loaded with chopped walnuts and chocolate banana shapes. Batch recipe using a traditional ice cream churn.

**Note: Batch recipe — uses a traditional ice cream churn, not a Ninja Creami.**

**Ingredients:**
- **500g** whole milk
- **300g** cream (heavy or whipping)
- **155g** sugar
- **20g** glucose syrup
- **40g** skimmed milk powder
- **2** egg yolks (free-range, organic or corn-fed — must be orange/deep yellow)
- **4** large ripe bananas (ideally very ripe; can roast to intensify flavour)
- **50g** walnuts, chopped
- **50g** chocolate banana shapes (or any chocolate fruit shapes from a silicone mold)

**Steps:**
1. If bananas are not very ripe, roast them in their skins at 175°C for 10–15 minutes to concentrate sugars and strengthen flavour. Remove from oven and allow to cool slightly.
2. Combine milk, cream, sugar, glucose, skimmed milk powder, and egg yolks in a saucepan. Heat, bringing just to the point of boiling, then hold for a couple of minutes. Remove from heat and transfer to a container.
3. Scoop the flesh from the roasted bananas and add it to the warm base. Steep for a minimum of 24 hours in the fridge.
4. After 24 hours, strain out the banana solids. This infuses pure banana flavour without pulp chunks or extra ice crystals.
5. Churn the strained base in an ice cream machine as normal.
6. While the base churns, make the chocolate shapes: melt chocolate in a microwave or bain-marie, fill a silicone mold (banana shapes or any fruit shapes), and freeze for ~10 minutes until hard. Pop out and store in the fridge until needed.
7. Chop walnuts to roughly 1cm pieces — smaller and they disappear, larger and they're overwhelming.
8. Once churned, layer ice cream into the tub with walnuts and chocolate shapes, stirring them in evenly. Fill the tub completely.
9. Freeze for a few hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- Egg yolks are added for colour and texture — use corn-fed or organic eggs with deep orange yolks. Heat base to 82°C if concerned about food safety with raw eggs.
- The steep-and-strain banana method (rather than blending banana into the base) is Nick's preferred technique: delivers true banana flavour with a clean, smooth texture and no ice crystals.
- Glucose syrup can be substituted with glucose powder, dextrose, corn syrup, or golden syrup — result is essentially the same.
- Chocolate shapes are optional; use whatever chocolate mix-ins you can source or make. Any silicone fruit mold works.
- Walnuts: buy pre-chopped or chop whole walnuts yourself to ~1cm.
