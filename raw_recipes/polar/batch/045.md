### [Creamy Pistachio Ice Cream using Monin Syrup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eypu322sstA)

Cooked custard base using Monin pistachio coffee syrup — a template for turning any flavored coffee syrup into ice cream.

**Ingredients:**

- **344g** 2% milk
- **317g** 33% whipping cream
- **61g** sugar
- **72g** invert sugar (corn syrup, golden syrup, or glucose syrup)
- **52g** skim milk powder
- **95g** Monin pistachio syrup
- **1** whole egg
- **1.5g** stabilizer (off-the-shelf; e.g. guar gum or similar — optional)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk and cream in a pan on the hob.
2. Mix stabilizer with sugar, then add both to the pan.
3. Add skim milk powder and stir in.
4. Add invert sugar.
5. Crack the whole egg directly into the pan and stir to combine.
6. Add Monin pistachio syrup.
7. Heat while stirring constantly until the mixture reaches 80°C — do not stop stirring or the egg will scramble.
8. Remove from heat and pour into a container. Check the pan bottom — no cooked egg bits should remain.
9. Refrigerate until fully chilled.
10. Churn in an ice cream machine (or use Ninja Creami). Load into containers.

**Notes:**

- The Monin syrup contributes a large portion of sugar (syrups are ~66–75% sugar), so the recipe is balanced around that — account for the syrup's sugar content when adapting to other flavors.
- Some Monin syrup flavors will cause the base to split/curdle — Nick mentions a separate video on how to handle that.
- The base works as a template for any coffee/flavored syrup flavor (hazelnut, Irish cream, raspberry, butterscotch, vanilla, etc.).
- Adding the whole egg directly to the cold/warm mix and heating slowly is Nick's preferred alternative to traditional tempering — works fine at home where you have time to stir constantly.
- Sugar-free Monin syrup versions can be used but still require balancing the recipe for PAC/sweetness.
- 1L of Monin syrup yields roughly 10 batches — economical per serve.
