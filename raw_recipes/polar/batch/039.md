### [Chocolate Orange Cheesecake Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=onCyCQJldH4)

A churned chocolate ice cream base layered with blended leftover chocolate orange cheesecake (mixed with crème de cacao and double cream) and digestive biscuit crumb. Inspired by a homemade Christmas cheesecake — presented as an improvised use of leftovers rather than a repeatable cheesecake-from-scratch recipe.

**Note: This is a large batch recipe (multiple pints). Scale down as needed.**

**Ice Cream Base Ingredients:**
- **550g** whole milk
- **400g** heavy/double cream
- **180g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **60g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **75g** cocoa powder (Nick uses Cacao Barry Brute)

**Cheesecake Core Ingredients:**
- Leftover chocolate orange cheesecake (baked, with chocolate cookie crumb base and orange zest filling)
- **30–50ml** crème de cacao (Mozart chocolate liqueur used; can substitute vodka or any spirit, or omit)
- Double cream, as needed to loosen

**Mix-In:**
- Digestive biscuits, broken into crumbs (US/Canada: graham crackers)

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a saucepan over heat. As it warms, whisk in the dextrose and sugar until dissolved.
2. When the milk reaches ~50°C, whisk in the cocoa powder until fully combined.
3. Add the SMP and continue stirring until fully incorporated (it takes time to dissolve).
4. Remove from heat and transfer to a container. Add the cold double cream — this brings the temperature down quickly.
5. Refrigerate overnight.
6. The next day, take frozen/cold leftover cheesecake and break into chunks. Add to a small blender with 30–50ml crème de cacao and a splash of double cream. Blend until very smooth, adding more cream if needed to loosen. Set aside.
7. Remove the chilled ice cream base from the fridge, give it a final stir to ensure it's fully emulsified.
8. Churn in an ice cream machine until done (Nick's batch took ~9 minutes).
9. Layer into containers: scoop of churned ice cream → dollop of blended cheesecake mixture → sprinkling of broken digestive biscuit crumb. Repeat until the tub is full.
10. Freeze overnight before serving.

**Notes:**

- This recipe uses leftover cheesecake as the core — Nick acknowledges you may not want to make an entire cheesecake just for this. The video is meant as inspiration for using up leftovers or adapting with your own cheesecake base.
- If repeating, Nick would reduce the milk and increase the alcohol (crème de cacao) in the cheesecake blend for better flavour and softer texture.
- Chocolate ice cream churns faster than vanilla-based recipes — Nick's batch set in ~9 minutes.
- Digestive biscuits are the UK equivalent of graham crackers. Use whichever is local to you.
- The blended cheesecake mix should be thick but pourable — add cream in small amounts to reach that consistency.
- Result was described as "pretty good but could be improved," mainly around the cheesecake core proportions and alcohol level.
