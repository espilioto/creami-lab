### [Making Salt & Straw - The Great Candycopia ice cream at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IInNcZLNGx8)

Bourbon butterscotch ice cream churned with Snickers, Heath Bars, Whoppers, and Reese's Peanut Butter Cups — a Halloween special based on the Salt & Straw recipe book.

**Ingredients:**

_Base (traditional churn — makes ~1 litre):_
- **90g** sugar
- **45g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **1.5g** xanthan gum
- **45g** light corn syrup (or golden syrup)
- **470ml** milk at 1.8% fat
- **470ml** heavy cream at 35% fat

_Butterscotch sauce:_
- **50g** light brown sugar
- **15g** granulated sugar
- **15g** corn syrup
- **30g** bourbon
- **60g** heavy cream
- **5g** butter
- Splash of vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

_Add-ins:_
- **2x** Snickers bars, chopped
- **2x** Heath bars, chopped
- **250g** bag of Whoppers
- **6x** Reese's Peanut Butter Cups, chopped

**Steps:**

1. In a pan, combine corn syrup and milk and stir well. Add sugar (pre-mixed with xanthan gum) and skim milk powder. Stir until sugar is dissolved.
2. Turn off heat and stir in the heavy cream. Chill the base for 4+ hours.
3. Make the butterscotch: combine brown sugar, granulated sugar, corn syrup, and bourbon in a pan. Stir until sugars look slightly wet, then heat without stirring until it reaches a gentle smoke point. Remove from heat.
4. Add heavy cream, butter, vanilla, and salt to the butterscotch. Stir well and hold at room temperature until the base is chilled.
5. Once the base is chilled, stir in the butterscotch sauce thoroughly (takes a few minutes of stirring to fully incorporate).
6. Churn in an ice cream machine until soft-serve consistency.
7. While churning, chop all add-ins into small pieces.
8. Remove churned ice cream from machine and fold in all add-ins. Freeze for a few hours before serving.

**Notes:**

- Recipe is from the official Salt & Straw recipe book (available at the links in the description).
- Nick notes: if making again, he'd go easier on the add-ins and would add the butterscotch to the base while still warm (easier to incorporate).
- Whoppers and Heath Bars are North American products and can be hard to source in the UK/Europe — plan ahead.
- This is a traditional churned recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
