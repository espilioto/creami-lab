### [The BEST Raspberry Sorbet recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BHJUvsw4dY)
Smooth, creamy raspberry sorbet stabilized with pectin — intense fruit flavour, no ice crystals.

**Ingredients:**
- **300g** water
- **500g** raspberries
- **220g** sugar
- **85g** dextrose
- **5g** pectin powder

**Steps:**
1. Mix the pectin powder together with the sugar (dry) before adding to liquid — pectin will not dissolve if added to water alone.
2. Combine the pectin/sugar mixture with the water and dextrose in a saucepan.
3. Bring to a boil, stirring, until the pectin is fully dissolved. The mix will thicken slightly.
4. Remove from heat and cool for 20–30 minutes.
5. Add the cooled syrup and the raw raspberries to a blender. Blend thoroughly for about a minute.
6. Strain through a fine mesh sieve to remove seeds.
7. Chill the strained mix in the fridge for 3–4 hours until completely cold.
8. Churn in an ice cream machine. The sorbet will lighten in colour as it churns. Remove after about 15–20 minutes (machine-dependent) once lightened and stiffened.
9. Transfer to a container and freeze.

**Notes:**
- Critical technique: always pre-mix pectin with sugar before adding to water — it clumps irreversibly if added directly to liquid.
- Raspberries are not cooked, which preserves pure, intense flavour and aroma.
- Sorbet is ready to pull from the machine once it visibly lightens in colour and stiffens up.
- Recipe is for a traditional churned ice cream machine, not a Ninja Creami — adapt freeze-and-process method accordingly.
