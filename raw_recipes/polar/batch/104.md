### [Making Ben & Jerry's Cherry Garcia in a Ninja Creami](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m_2Dj7o8rI8)

Three-part B&J Cherry Garcia clone: cherry ice cream base, chocolate fudge, and erythritol-soaked cherries as mix-ins — made in the Ninja Creami.

**Batch note:** Recipe as shown makes 1.5L (3 Ninja Creami pints). Ingredients below are for the full batch; divide by 3 for a single pint.

---

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base (full batch, ~1.5L):**
- **375g** 2% milk
- **600g** cream, 33% fat (whipping cream)
- **110g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **190g** sugar
- **30g** invert sugar (corn syrup)
- **200g** frozen cherries (blended into base)
- Ice cream stabilizer (off-the-shelf blend of stabilizers and emulsifiers, e.g. Special Ingredients brand) — mixed into sugar before adding liquids

**Ingredients — Erythritol-Soaked Cherries (mix-in):**
- **100g** frozen cherries (for mix-in, not blended into base)
- **150g** water
- **60g** erythritol/monk fruit blend (erythritol-only also works; xylitol works too)

**Ingredients — Chocolate Fudge:**
- **1 can** condensed milk (390g / 300ml)
- **350g** dark chocolate, 70% (e.g. Cacao Barry 70%) — do not use milk chocolate

---

**Steps:**

1. **Soak the cherries (mix-in):** Combine 100g frozen cherries, 150g water, and 60g erythritol/monk fruit blend in a small saucepan. Simmer on low for ~20 minutes. The heat allows the sugar syrup to infuse into the cherry flesh, preventing them from freezing rock-solid. Cool completely, then refrigerate (ideally overnight).

2. **Make the ice cream base:** Combine sugar and stabilizer in a large saucepan and mix together. Add skim milk powder and stir to distribute. Add corn syrup/invert sugar and the 200g frozen cherries. Pour in the milk and cream cold. Heat everything together from cold — do NOT add hot acidic cherry liquid to cold dairy, it will curdle. Bring up to 85°C (185°F) to activate stabilizer and fully break down the cherries. Do not exceed ~90°C. Once at temperature, turn off heat.

3. **Blend the base:** Transfer the hot mixture to a blender (carefully — it is super hot). Blend on low for ~20 seconds, then increase to medium/high in short bursts until smooth. This extracts all cherry flavor and produces a clean, smooth pink base. If no blender, mash cherries and pass through a fine-mesh sieve.

4. **Fill pints and chill:** Pour base into Ninja Creami pints (fills ~3). Refrigerate until fully chilled (several hours). Then freeze for at least 24 hours.

5. **Make the chocolate fudge:** Combine condensed milk and 350g dark chocolate in a saucepan. Warm gently over low heat, stirring, until chocolate is completely melted and mixture is smooth and silky. Do NOT overheat. Pour into a lined 12×8-inch baking tray. Leave at room temperature to set. Once set, cut into ~5mm pieces for mix-in. Stores in the freezer for months (over a year reported).

6. **Prepare cherries for mix-in:** Remove the soaked cherries from fridge. Drain and chop into quarters.

7. **Spin and add mix-ins:** Spin frozen pint on the Ice Cream setting. Use a spoon to make a channel down the center to the bottom. Add chopped fudge pieces and quartered soaked cherries into the channel. Run Mix-In program.

---

**Notes:**
- This is the North American version (fudge pieces), not the European version (chocolate pieces). Nick never tried the NA version before moving to Australia.
- Use dark chocolate (70%+) for the fudge — milk chocolate does not pair well with cherries.
- The re-spin decimates the mix-ins in the Ninja Creami; a traditional ice cream machine preserves them better. Cut fudge small (~5mm) as the machine cannot process large hard chunks.
- Soaking cherries in erythritol syrup infuses FPD (freezing point depression) into the fruit, preventing them from going icy/solid. This also avoids curdling the base from acidic cherry juice.
- Add cherry mixture cold to cold dairy, then heat together — prevents curdling from acid + heat differential.
- Erythritol/monk fruit combo recrystallizes within 24 hours in ice cream; use it only for the cherry soak, not the base. For the base, use regular sugar (or xylitol at reduced quantity; avoid allulose).
- Fudge keeps in the freezer for over a year; this batch makes a large amount — treat it as a long-term supply.
- The blended cherry base produces a naturally pink ice cream (the OG B&J color, not the modern bland brown).
