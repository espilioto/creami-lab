### [Luxury Keto Protein Chocolate Peanut Butter Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DKdKYNas7zE)

Keto, high-protein, zero-added-sugar chocolate ice cream churned in a traditional machine with layered peanut butter swirl.

**Ingredients:**

- **300g** whole milk
- **317g** whipping cream (33% fat; 35–36% is fine)
- **20g** cocoa powder (strong/dark recommended)
- **43g** chocolate protein powder (use whatever you have; chocolate-flavored preferred)
- **1g** guar gum
- **50–100g** peanut butter (for swirl mix-in)
- **1 tsp** refined coconut oil (for peanut butter swirl — refined so no coconut taste)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, and cocoa powder in a saucepan. Heat gently to 55–60°C, stirring, until cocoa is fully homogenized into the liquid. Do not boil.
2. Remove from heat. Allow to cool slightly to around 40°C.
3. Mix protein powder and guar gum together in a separate bowl, then whisk into the warm base. Adding protein at this lower temperature prevents overcooking (overcooked protein becomes thick and gummy).
4. Transfer base to a container and refrigerate until fully chilled.
5. Pre-chill ice cream machine bowl for 5–10 minutes. Pour in the cold base and churn until soft-serve consistency.
6. While churning, gently melt peanut butter with coconut oil in a microwave until fully liquid and homogenized.
7. Layer soft-serve into your storage tub in stages, drizzling the liquid peanut butter swirl between layers.
8. Freeze for 3–4 hours (or overnight) to harden off.
9. Scoop and serve; drizzle any remaining warm peanut butter over the top.

**Notes:**

- This recipe uses a traditional churning ice cream machine, not the Ninja Creami. If using a Ninja Creami, pour the cooled base directly into the pint and freeze 24h before processing — but expect higher calorie density per serving due to zero overrun vs. 10–30% overrun from a churning machine.
- Protein powder brand is not specified — Nick uses his own daily protein shake powder. For a chocolate recipe, chocolate-flavored protein is preferred but plain works since cocoa is added separately.
- Many flavored/pre-mixed protein powders contain their own gums and stabilizers — be careful stacking additional stabilizers on top, as the base can become too thick and freeze rock hard.
- Macros (as churned, no overrun): 15.8% fat, 0.3% sugar, 6% MSNF (milk solids non-fat), 38.6% total solids, 61.5% water, ~3g carbs/100g, ~6g protein/100g.
- Peanut butter quantity is flexible (50–100g) depending on how much swirl you want.
- Use refined coconut oil for the swirl so it stays neutral-flavored.
