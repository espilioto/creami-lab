### [Coffee & Donut Ice Cream DUO TUB — James Hoffmann / Dana Cree style](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EhFEbFXiRqU)

A duo tub with one half coffee ice cream and one half donut-infused ice cream, inspired by James Hoffmann's coffee-and-donut drink and Dana Cree's donut ice cream from "Hello, My Name Is Ice Cream." Made with a traditional ice cream machine (churned).

---

**Coffee Ice Cream Ingredients:**
- **400ml** whole milk
- **200ml** double (heavy) cream
- **130g** sugar
- **25g** skimmed milk powder
- **50g** ground coffee beans (coarse) OR 2–3 tsp instant coffee granules

**Donut Ice Cream Ingredients:**
- **400ml** whole milk (plus extra — need at least 400ml after steeping/straining)
- **200ml** double (heavy) cream
- **120g** sugar
- **25g** skimmed milk powder
- **6** Krispy Kreme Original Glazed donuts (4 will work at a push)

---

**Steps:**

*Coffee Ice Cream — Bean method:*
1. Combine milk, sugar, and 50g coarse ground coffee beans in a saucepan. Heat briefly, then turn off the heat and leave to steep for 30 minutes.
2. Strain through a cheesecloth. Refrigerate until ready to use.

*Coffee Ice Cream — Instant method (alternative):*
1. Warm a small portion of the milk in the microwave. Add the sugar and instant coffee granules and stir until fully dissolved.
2. Pour the warm mix back into the remaining cold milk to bring the temperature down. Refrigerate until ready to use.

*Donut Ice Cream:*
1. Steep the donuts in the milk in the fridge for 24 hours, letting the glaze and dough flavour fully infuse.
2. Strain well, squeezing all excess milk out of the donuts. Discard the spent dough. You need at least 400ml of donut-infused milk after straining.
3. Refrigerate until ready to churn.

*Assembly (Duo Tub):*
1. Churn the coffee ice cream first. When done, load it into one half of the tub. Lay the tub on its side, cover the open face with cling film, and freeze for approximately 30 minutes.
2. While the coffee half is firming up, mix the donut milk with sugar, milk powder, and cream until the powder is fully hydrated, then churn in the ice cream machine. Stop the churn slightly earlier than fully done so the mix is still soft enough to pour.
3. Remove the tub from the freezer, take off the cling film, and fill the empty half with the donut ice cream. Press it right down to the bottom to avoid air pockets.
4. Freeze overnight before serving.

---

**Notes:**
- This is a batch/churned recipe — requires a traditional ice cream machine, not a Ninja Creami.
- The duo tub technique works with any two flavour combinations — just churn one half, freeze it firm, then churn and add the second.
- Krispy Kreme Original Glazed are Nick's preferred donut; use whatever glazed donuts you can get.
- Stopping the donut churn a few minutes early makes it easier to press into the void left by the coffee half without overfilling.
- Inspired by Dana Cree's donut ice cream ("Hello, My Name Is Ice Cream") and James Hoffmann's coffee-and-donut drink concept.
