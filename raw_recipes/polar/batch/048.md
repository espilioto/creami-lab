### [Black Forest Frozen Yogurt – Tart Cherries, Chocolate & Creamy Greek Yogurt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYa7hXhbhds)

Churned Black Forest frozen yogurt with a Greek yogurt base, cherry syrup swirl, and grated 70% dark chocolate mixed in at the end of the churn.

**Ingredients:**

Base:
- **215g** whole milk (3.5% MF)
- **474g** Greek yogurt (5% MF) — full-fat strongly recommended; lower fat makes balancing very difficult
- **85g** whipping cream (33%+ MF)
- **138g** sugar
- **17g** invert sugar (corn syrup, glucose syrup, or golden syrup)
- **5g** vanilla extract (up to 10g acceptable)
- **3g** stabilizer (Nick uses his "442 blend"; any stabilizer works — cold-process types need only ~50°C, heat-activated need 85°C / 185°F)
- **60g** skim milk powder (SMP) — intentionally high to boost MSNF (milk solids-non-fat)

Cherry syrup:
- **~100g** Fabri cherry syrup (or homemade cherry syrup — see Notes)
- **~3g** xanthan gum (pre-mixed with a small amount of sugar to prevent clumping)

Mix-in:
- **~50g** 70% dark chocolate (Callebaut or any 70%), grated and/or roughly sliced into mixed-size pieces

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, yogurt, cream, sugar (reserve ~30g to premix stabilizer), invert sugar, stabilizer, and skim milk powder in a saucepan. Stir gently — the yogurt will make it prone to splashing.
2. Heat to 85°C (185°F) if using a heat-activated stabilizer. Cold-process stabilizer: heat only to ~50°C, enough to dissolve solids and combine everything.
3. Allow to cool for ~5 minutes off the heat — the base will thicken noticeably. Add vanilla extract once slightly cooled.
4. Transfer to a container (base may appear to separate in the fridge — this is normal for frozen yogurt). Refrigerate 3–4 hours until fully chilled.
5. Prepare cherry syrup: mix xanthan gum with a small pinch of sugar, whisk into ~100g cherry syrup. If it clumps, microwave 5–10 seconds and whisk again, or warm briefly on the hob. Refrigerate until needed.
6. Prepare chocolate: grate and roughly slice 70% dark chocolate into a mix of fine and larger pieces.
7. Once base is fully chilled, whisk it back together (it thickens in the fridge). Pour into ice cream churn machine and churn. Frozen yogurt churns faster with less overrun than standard ice cream due to high solids content.
8. Towards the end of the churn, add the dark chocolate pieces and incorporate fully.
9. Layer into serving containers: alternate scoops of frozen yogurt with small spoonfuls of cherry syrup until containers are full.
10. Freeze 3–4 hours to harden. Serve.

**Notes:**

- **Recipe was edited three times** after early versions went too hard from day 2 onward. The ingredient amounts above (published 31 May 2025) are the final corrected version and are described as "wonderful — doesn't go too hard."
- **Description also lists an alternate/earlier set of amounts** (240g milk, 385g yogurt, 85g cream, 185g sugar, 10g invert sugar, 10g vanilla, 3g stabilizer, 20g SMP) — use the VTT/final version above.
- Use 5% MF Greek yogurt. Zero to 3% MF yogurt makes balancing very difficult and is not recommended.
- Invert sugar (corn syrup, glucose syrup, or golden syrup) is key — it prevents the graininess and iciness that frozen yogurt is prone to and improves mouthfeel.
- Cherry syrup: Nick uses Fabri brand (recommended as best available). Homemade is easy — flavored sugar syrup thickened with xanthan gum.
- Xanthan gum is preferred over other stabilizers for the cherry syrup because cherries are highly acidic and some stabilizers fail in acidic environments.
- The base will look like it is separating in the fridge — this is completely normal for frozen yogurt; just whisk before churning.
- Works in a Ninja Creami or traditional churn-style machine. Overrun will be lower than ice cream due to high solids.
- Chocolate: mix of grated (fine) and sliced (larger) pieces gives textural contrast.
