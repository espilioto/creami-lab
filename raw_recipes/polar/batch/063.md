### [Festive Eggnog Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Yye5d6cKbc)

Boozy custard-based eggnog ice cream churned in a traditional machine — rich, spiced, and spiked with Old J Spiced Rum.

**Ingredients:**

- **350g** whole milk
- **350g** double/heavy cream
- **50g** brown sugar
- **120g** white sugar
- **60g** skimmed milk powder
- **5** egg yolks
- **5g** ground nutmeg (fresh preferred, dried fine)
- **70g** Old J Spiced Rum (or alcohol of choice — Jack Daniel's, Captain Morgan's, Baileys, etc.)
- **5g** vanilla paste or extract

**Steps:**

1. Add milk (whole milk), brown sugar, white sugar, skimmed milk powder, and egg yolks directly into a saucepan. Whisk together well.
2. Heat on the stove, stirring continuously, until the mixture reaches 82°C. This pasteurises the eggs and creates the custard base without a separate tempering step.
3. Remove from heat and transfer to a container.
4. Stir in the double/heavy cream.
5. Add the rum (or alcohol of choice) and nutmeg. Stir to combine.
6. Refrigerate until completely chilled.
7. Pre-chill your ice cream machine bowl well (high alcohol content means the mix resists freezing).
8. Churn until done — took 13 minutes in Nick's 5030 machine.
9. Transfer to a tub and freeze.

**Notes:**

- Traditional churned recipe — not a Ninja Creami recipe.
- The 70g of rum significantly depresses the freezing point; sugar was deliberately reduced to compensate. If you reduce alcohol, you can increase sugar slightly.
- Nick used notably orange-yolked eggs, which give the ice cream a golden colour — not a problem, just visual.
- Alcohol choice is flexible: whiskey, rum, Baileys, brandy all work. Stronger spirits give a more pronounced effect on texture and freeze point.
- Nick warns against using store-bought eggnog as a milk substitute — he found it gave a subpar flavour.
- Vanilla paste or extract both work; paste gives flecks.
