### [The Secret Recipe for Ben & Jerry's Coffee Coffee Buzz Buzz Buzz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wNNmVsDADlc)

Ben & Jerry's Coffee Coffee Buzz Buzz Buzz dupe — strong coffee ice cream base with espresso chocolate fudge chunks, made in a traditional churner.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **500g** milk
- **300g** cream
- **140g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose (or increase sugar to 180g total if omitting)
- **60g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **2** egg yolks
- **2g** stabiliser (optional)
- **50g** ground coffee beans OR **50g** whole coffee beans OR **40–50g** instant coffee powder

**Ingredients — Espresso Chocolate Fudge:**
- **400g** condensed milk (sweetened condensed milk)
- **200g** dark chocolate
- **20g** instant coffee powder OR **5g** coffee extract

**Steps:**

1. Make the fudge first: gently warm condensed milk in a pan, add dark chocolate and stir until completely melted. Add instant coffee powder and stir to combine thoroughly.
2. Pour fudge into a lined container and refrigerate for a few hours to harden. Once set, cut into small pieces (~0.5cm squares) while still cold/frozen — it gets very sticky at room temperature.
3. Make the coffee-infused milk: warm milk on the hob and add ground/whole/instant coffee. Keep on low heat for at least 1 hour to steep and infuse.
4. Strain the coffee from the milk (if using ground or whole beans). Return infused milk to the pan and add sugar, dextrose, egg yolks, and skim milk powder. If using stabiliser, pre-mix it into the sugars before adding.
5. Heat mixture until combined. Remove from heat, pour into a container, add cream (cools the base quickly), and refrigerate until fully chilled.
6. Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine. In the final minutes of churning, add the chopped espresso fudge pieces and let them incorporate.
7. Transfer to a tub and freeze for at least 4 hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- Batch recipe — makes multiple servings, not a single pint.
- Coffee method is flexible: ground beans give fastest infusion, whole beans take longer (smaller surface area) but produce the same final strength, instant coffee is the quickest option.
- Fudge is best chopped straight from the freezer — at room temperature it becomes very sticky and messy.
- Stabiliser is optional; pre-mixing it with the sugars helps even dispersion.
- Adding cream after the heat step (rather than heating it) brings the base temperature down quickly before chilling.
