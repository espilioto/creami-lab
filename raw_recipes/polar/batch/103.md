### [Making Ample Hills - Hopelessly Devoted to Moo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4dKqHEscREI)

Malted vanilla ice cream with chunks of Brooklyn Blackout chocolate cake and a chocolate pudding swirl — a recreation of the discontinued Ample Hills Creamery flavor.

**Ingredients — Brooklyn Blackout Cake:**
- **100g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **270g** granulated sugar
- **2** large eggs, room temperature
- **1/4 tsp** vanilla extract
- **70g** unsweetened cocoa powder
- **3/4 tsp** baking powder
- **3/4 tsp** baking soda
- pinch of salt
- **215g** all-purpose flour
- **165ml** whole milk

**Ingredients — Chocolate Pudding Frosting:**
- **600ml** water
- **540g** granulated sugar
- **1 tbsp** corn syrup
- **110g** unsweetened cocoa powder
- **80g** cornstarch
- **78g** unsalted butter, cold, cut into 1-inch cubes
- **1/2 tsp** vanilla extract

**Ingredients — Malted Vanilla Ice Cream:**
- **300ml** milk
- **300ml** cream (49% fat)
- **90g** granulated sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **40g** golden syrup
- **50g** Horlicks Malt Powder
- **1.1g** stabiliser
- **2 tsp** vanilla extract

**Steps:**
1. Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Butter and dust two 8-inch pans with cocoa powder.
2. Make the cake: cream butter and sugar, add eggs and vanilla, combine dry ingredients (cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, salt, flour), alternate adding dry mix and milk. Bake 25–30 minutes.
3. Make the pudding frosting: combine water, sugar, corn syrup, cocoa, and cornstarch in a saucepan; cook until thickened, then whisk in cold butter and vanilla. This doubles as the pudding swirl.
4. Make the ice cream base: combine milk, cream, sugar, dextrose, golden syrup, Horlicks, and stabiliser; churn in an ice cream machine.
5. Assemble: layer churned ice cream with crumbled blackout cake chunks and swirls of the chocolate pudding.

**Notes:**
- This is a multi-component batch recipe — significant prep time required. Nick notes you can skip baking and use a store-bought chocolate cake to simplify.
- The pudding frosting serves dual purpose: it frosts the cake AND is swirled into the ice cream.
- Horlicks Malt Powder is the key flavoring for the "malted vanilla" ice cream base.
- Cream is listed at 49% fat — a high-fat double/extra-thick cream.
- Stabiliser quantity is minimal (1.1g) — type not specified, likely a commercial blend.
