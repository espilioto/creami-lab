### [The Secret to Perfect Homemade Mango Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-dPxbFgWyk)

A low-fat mango gelato remake with improved color, flavor, and overrun using higher mango content and reduced fat (6%).

**Ingredients:**

- **400g** mango flesh (sliced/frozen mangoes)
- **215g** milk
- **170g** cream (33% whipping cream) + **50g** cream added just before churning
- **80g** sugar
- **71g** dextrose
- **65g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **4g** stabilizer blend (Special Ingredients brand recommended)

**Steps:**

1. Combine all dry ingredients (sugar, dextrose, SMP, stabilizer blend) together in a bowl and mix well so the stabilizer is fully dispersed — this prevents clumping when added to liquid.
2. Gently heat milk and cream (170g) in a pan. Pour in the dry ingredient mixture and whisk together.
3. Heat to 80–85°C, then stir off the heat for about 5 minutes to ensure the stabilizer is fully hydrated.
4. Blend mango flesh in a blender on low until smooth, then add the hot dairy base and blend again to combine.
5. Strain the mixture through a fine mesh sieve into a clean bowl to remove any lumps.
6. If the base is too viscous/thick, add the reserved 50g (up to 75ml) of cream to loosen it to a pourable consistency.
7. Pour into a container and freeze.

**Notes:**

- This is a remake of a previous mango gelato — the key improvement is dropping fat to ~6% and using a higher mango ratio, which delivers stronger color, better mango flavor, and improved overrun.
- Pre-mixing the stabilizer with the other dry powders before adding to liquid is critical — adding stabilizer directly to liquid causes clumping.
- The high fiber content from 400g of mango flesh contributes to good overrun and a slightly whipped, light texture.
- The extra 50g cream added after blending is a viscosity adjustment step, not part of the original base calculation — add it if the blended base is too thick to pour.
- Stabilizer brand used: Special Ingredients (no affiliation declared); requires heating to 80–85°C to activate.
