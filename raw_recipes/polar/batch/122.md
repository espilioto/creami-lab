### [Salt & Straw Maple Bacon Streusel Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fZezHjIDT8o)

Salt & Straw's maple ice cream with candied bacon and oat streusel — a sweet-and-savory combo made famous by Voodoo Donuts.

**Ingredients — Streusel:**
- **80g** quick-cook oats
- **40g** all-purpose flour, sifted
- **80g** dark brown sugar
- **0.5 tsp** salt
- **75g** cold butter

**Ingredients — Candied Bacon:**
- **50g** maple syrup
- **50g** brown sugar
- Bacon (quantity to taste)

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **315ml** whole milk (1.8% fat)
- **315ml** heavy cream (35% fat)
- **60g** sugar
- **30g** light corn syrup (or golden syrup)
- **100g** maple syrup (65% sugar)
- **30g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **1g** xanthan gum (or stabiliser of choice)

**Steps:**

1. **Candied bacon:** Coat bacon strips on both sides in the maple syrup and brown sugar. Place on a foil-lined baking tray, add extra sugar on top if desired. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes. Remove and cool quickly in the freezer. Once cold, chop into small pieces.
2. **Streusel:** Combine oats, sifted flour, dark brown sugar, salt, and cold butter. Mix until crumbly. Bake at 175°C for 12–15 minutes until golden. Remove from oven and let cool. Add the chopped candied bacon to the cooled streusel. Place the combined mix in the freezer until fully chilled.
3. **Ice cream base:** Add corn syrup and milk to a saucepan, stir to combine. Mix the 1g stabiliser into the 60g sugar, then add to the pan. Add maple syrup and skim milk powder. Heat to 65°C (or to the temperature required by your stabiliser). Remove from heat and stir in the heavy cream. Store covered in the fridge overnight.
4. **Churn:** Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine for 15–30 minutes depending on your machine.
5. **Pack:** Layer churned ice cream and streusel-bacon mixture into a tub. Freeze until set.

**Notes:**
- Salt & Straw use guar gum and carrageenan in their commercial tubs but specify xanthan gum in their home recipes. Any stabiliser works — keep the total amount the same (1g).
- Pre-mix the stabiliser into the dry sugar before adding to the pan to ensure even distribution and prevent clumping.
- Line the baking tray with foil when candying the bacon — burnt caramelised sugar is very difficult to clean.
- Cool the bacon in the freezer immediately after baking so it firms up and is easier to chop.
- Taste profile: subtle maple ice cream base, bacon flavour comes through as a finish, streusel adds a pleasant textural contrast. Nick notes this is a personal-taste flavour — not universally loved (he personally wasn't convinced) but technically well-executed.
- Streusel recipe is the same proven recipe used in the Jeni's Fluffernutter video.
