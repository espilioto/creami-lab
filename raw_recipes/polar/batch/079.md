### [Snowball 3 Ways — Advocaat Ice Cream (UK Christmas Drink)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q0-Fxmpru7s)

Three serving variations of the classic British Christmas Snowball cocktail (advocaat + lemonade), with a homemade advocaat ice cream as the centrepiece — dedicated advocaat ice cream recipe video was promised as a follow-up.

**Ingredients (Variation 1 — Classic Snowball):**
- **50ml** Warnick's advocaat
- Lemonade, to top up
- Lime wedge, to garnish

**Ingredients (Variation 2 — Ice Cream Float Snowball):**
- **50ml** Warnick's advocaat
- Lemonade, to top up
- 1 scoop advocaat ice cream (eggless base with a large quantity of advocaat + meringue pieces mixed in — full recipe promised in separate video)

**Ingredients (Variation 3 — Advocaat Affogato Snowball):**
- 1–2 scoops advocaat ice cream (with meringue pieces)
- 1 shot freshly brewed espresso, poured over the top
- Advocaat white chocolate sauce (made separately), drizzled on top

**Steps:**
1. **Classic Snowball:** Pour 50ml advocaat into a glass, top with lemonade, stir briefly, garnish with a lime wedge.
2. **Ice Cream Float:** Make the classic snowball as above, then add a scoop of advocaat ice cream on top — the carbonation from the lemonade creates a float effect.
3. **Affogato Snowball:** Place 1–2 scoops of advocaat ice cream in a martini glass, pour a shot of hot espresso over the top, then drizzle advocaat white chocolate sauce over everything.

**Notes:**
- This video is a Christmas drinks showcase, not a Ninja Creami recipe video. The dedicated advocaat ice cream recipe (Ninja Creami) was filmed separately and released after Christmas.
- Advocaat brand used: Warnick's (Dutch egg liqueur, widely available in the UK).
- The ice cream used is described as an eggless base with a large amount of advocaat and meringue pieces folded in — full recipe not given in this video.
- The advocaat white chocolate sauce was made fresh approximately 20 minutes before filming; no recipe given.
- Nick notes the classic Snowball is 50ml advocaat topped with lemonade — variations sourced from Warnick's official website.
- Two further hot-chocolate-based advocaat variations were teased for a follow-up video.
