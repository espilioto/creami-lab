### [Festive Homemade Christmas Pudding Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6YRvuE7crc)

Churned ice cream base with cinnamon, nutmeg, and cherry brandy, with broken Christmas pudding folded in at soft-serve stage.

**Ingredients:**

- **475g** whole milk
- **350g** cream (whipping or double/heavy)
- **180g** sugar
- **15g** golden syrup (or corn syrup / glucose)
- **47g** skimmed milk powder
- **1.2g** xanthan gum (stabilizer)
- **5g** ground cinnamon
- **5g** ground nutmeg
- **30g** cherry brandy (24% ABV — or any preferred alcohol)
- **100g** Christmas pudding (leftover or freshly cooked; theirs contained brandy, cider, rum, and orange)

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a pan and warm gently.
2. Mix stabilizer (xanthan gum) with a portion of the sugar to prevent clumping; add to the milk along with the remaining sugar.
3. Add golden syrup and stir to combine.
4. Separately mix cinnamon and nutmeg into the skimmed milk powder, then add to the milk and stir well.
5. Remove from heat and stir in the cherry brandy.
6. Transfer to a container, add the cream (this brings the temperature down quickly), then refrigerate for several hours until fully chilled.
7. Churn the base in an ice cream machine until soft-serve consistency.
8. Break Christmas pudding into small chunks; fold into the churned ice cream and churn for another minute or two to distribute.
9. Transfer to a container and freeze.

**Notes:**

- Cinnamon and nutmeg are ground spices and will not dissolve — they settle (typically to the top during chilling), so stir the base well before churning.
- Any alcohol can be substituted for the cherry brandy; ABV affects freezing point.
- Christmas pudding can be supermarket-bought or homemade. Small chunk size is recommended for even distribution.
- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
