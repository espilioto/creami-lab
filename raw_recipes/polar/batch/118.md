### [Jeni's Fluffernutter Pie Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d4ZGPeULuAA)

A duo-tub recreation of Jeni's Fluffernutter Pie: salty peanut butter ice cream and vanilla marshmallow ice cream, both loaded with a baked oat crumble. Makes ~1.3L total (two halves).

**Ingredients:**

Peanut Butter Ice Cream:
- **300ml** milk (1.8% fat)
- **150ml** cream (49% fat)
- **110g** sugar
- **1g** stabilizer — commercial blend (Locust Bean Gum, Fatty Acids, Guar Gum, Sodium Alginate, Agar Agar) — optional
- **1/2 tsp** kosher salt
- **65g** peanut butter (Skippy smooth)

Marshmallow Ice Cream:
- **300ml** milk (1.8% fat)
- **200ml** cream (49% fat)
- **75g** sugar
- **75g** large marshmallows (~3 large; must contain gelatin and ~30–35g sugar)

Oat Crumble (shared between both halves):
- **80g** quick-cook oats
- **40g** all-purpose flour
- **80g** dark brown sugar
- **0.5 tsp** salt
- **75g** butter

**Steps:**

1. **Peanut butter base:** Combine milk, cream, sugar, and stabilizer in a saucepan. Heat to 75–80°C, stirring to dissolve. Remove from heat, stir in peanut butter (Skippy) and salt until smooth. Transfer to a container, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until ready to churn.
2. **Marshmallow base:** Combine milk, cream, and sugar in a saucepan. Add marshmallows chopped (use scissors or a knife). Heat gently — marshmallows will resist melting, then dissolve suddenly; stir until fully incorporated. The gelatin in the marshmallows acts as stabilizer. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, stir in cream, refrigerate until ready to churn.
3. **Oat crumble:** Combine oats, flour, dark brown sugar, salt, and melted butter in a bowl. Mix thoroughly until no dry lumps remain. Spread flat on a baking tray. Bake at ~180°C for 15–20 minutes until golden (remove any over-browned edges). Cool completely, then cut/break into small pieces. Store in freezer until needed.
4. **Churn peanut butter base** in ice cream machine (15–30 min depending on machine). Add crumble pieces at the end of the churn. Fill one half of a storage tub (or a separate container), press cling film onto the surface, lid on, and place in freezer on its side for 20–30 min to firm up while you clean the machine.
5. **Churn marshmallow base** the same way, adding crumble at the end. Pack into the second half of the tub alongside the first flavor. Tap down firmly to eliminate voids, lid on, and freeze for 3–4 hours before serving.

**Notes:**

- This is a **batch recipe** yielding ~1.3L — two ice cream flavors packed as a duo tub.
- Use marshmallows that have a strong vanilla flavour; bland/flavorless marshmallows will produce a flat-tasting base. Adding a splash of extra vanilla extract is recommended.
- The marshmallow gelatin replaces the need for a commercial stabilizer in that half. The peanut butter half uses 1g commercial stabilizer (optional but recommended).
- Butter is used in the crumble instead of sunflower oil (either works — you just need a fat to bind the dry ingredients).
- Smooth peanut butter preferred; if using chunky, stir the base during churning as peanut chunks may sink to the bottom.
- Crumble can be mixed in during the last minutes of churn (if your machine is robust) or folded in by hand in the tub.
