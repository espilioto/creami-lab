### [Stunning Ninja Creami Espresso con Panna Gelato - DUAL LAYER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qm7MhtFsL9Q)

Italian-inspired dual-layer Ninja Creami Deluxe recipe: espresso gelato base topped with stabilized vanilla whipped cream, frozen together as a two-layer pint. Makes 2 pints.

**Ingredients — Espresso Gelato Base (2 pints):**
- **490g** milk (2% or whole)
- **245g** whipping cream (double/heavy cream)
- **115g** allulose
- **50g** skim milk powder
- **100g** espresso (brewed — moka pot, machine, or pods)
- **12g** stabilizer blend (Nick's own blend, heat-activated; or 3g guar gum / xanthan gum if using a cold-process stabilizer)
- **1 tbsp** instant coffee powder (optional, for extra intensity — add while base is still hot)

**Ingredients — Stabilized Whipped Cream Top Layer (enough for 2 pints + extra):**
- **500g** whipping cream (double/heavy cream)
- Vanilla sugar (sugar stored with used vanilla pods — or substitute vanilla extract)
- **~2g** guar gum (or xanthan gum; must be cold-process if using xanthan)

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cream, allulose, and espresso in a saucepan. Mix stabilizer blend with skim milk powder first (to prevent clumping), then add to the liquid.
2. Heat to 85°C / 180°F, stirring until fully hydrated and cooked. Remove from heat.
3. While still hot, stir in 1 tbsp instant coffee powder if using, until dissolved.
4. Split base evenly between two Ninja Creami Deluxe pints. Freeze until the top is firm but the middle is still partially liquid (a few hours — enough to support the cream layer).
5. Whip 500g cream with vanilla sugar and guar gum to roughly 50% air incorporation — target a thick, pourable "melted chocolate" consistency, not stiff peaks. Stop before over-whipping.
6. Gently spoon whipped cream on top of the semi-frozen gelato in each pint, filling right to the max-fill line. Do not let the cream sink into the base.
7. Freeze overnight (full 24h) until completely solid.
8. Spin on **Lite Ice Cream** setting (TOP only, Deluxe). The faster spin re-creams the whipped cream layer without destroying the gelato underneath.

**Notes:**
- This is a batch recipe yielding 2 Deluxe pints; base ingredients are shared across both.
- Lite Ice Cream spins faster than standard Ice Cream — critical for processing the high-fat cream top layer back to a creamy texture.
- Use instant coffee powder rather than adding more espresso to boost coffee flavor; espresso is mostly water and will dilute/destabilize the base.
- Nick uses a heat-activated custom stabilizer blend at 12g per double batch; for common cold-process stabilizers (guar or xanthan gum) use ~3g total.
- Pre-mix stabilizer into skim milk powder before adding to liquid to prevent clumping.
- Vanilla sugar (sugar infused with spent vanilla pods for ~1 month) is Nick's flavoring for the cream layer; plain vanilla extract works fine as a substitute.
- The two-layer result: spun cream on top, coffee gelato beneath — both distinct textures in one pint.
