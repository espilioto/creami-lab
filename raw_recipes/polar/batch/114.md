### [Making Häagen Dazs Irish Cream Brownie at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6dNRLkdAD8k)

Custard-based Irish cream ice cream with chocolate fudge swirl and brownie chunks — Häagen Dazs dupe. Batch recipe (~1.2L).

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **340ml** milk
- **455ml** whipping cream (35% fat)
- **120g** sugar
- **35g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **55g** golden syrup / corn syrup
- **3** large egg yolks
- **100ml** Baileys Irish Cream
- **0.85g** stabilizer

Fudge Swirl:
- **40g** brown sugar
- **70g** white sugar
- **25g** cocoa powder
- **57g** butter
- **60ml** milk
- **1.5 tbsp** corn syrup / golden syrup
- **0.5 tbsp** vanilla extract

Brownies:
- **110g** butter
- **150g** plain flour (all-purpose)
- **35g** cocoa powder
- **0.5 tsp** baking powder
- **0.5 tsp** salt
- **275g** Bournville chocolate
- **275g** sugar
- **3** eggs

**Steps:**

1. Mix milk, sugar (with stabilizer mixed in), egg yolks, and skim milk powder in a pan. Heat gently to 82°C to cook the eggs — no tempering needed with this method.
2. Remove from heat, transfer to a container. Add cream, vanilla bean paste, and Baileys. Stir briefly, then refrigerate until fully chilled.
3. For the fudge swirl: heat milk, sugars, and butter together until melted. Add cocoa powder, stir well, bring to a boil then remove from heat. Stir in vanilla extract. Store in a container in the fridge.
4. For the brownies: bake at 160°C for 22 minutes (see linked brownie video). Chop and keep in the freezer until assembly.
5. Once the base is fully chilled, churn in your ice cream machine (approx. 10–30 minutes depending on machine — a Lello 5030 took 12 minutes for the full 1.2L batch).
6. While churning, chop brownies and keep frozen.
7. Layer churned ice cream, fudge sauce, and brownie chunks into tubs. Freeze for a few hours before serving.

**Notes:**

- This is a batch recipe yielding ~1.2L — scale down as needed for smaller containers.
- Mixing stabilizer into the sugar before adding to the base ensures even dispersion without clumping.
- Adding Bailey's off-heat (not cooked) preserves the Irish cream flavour — the host specifically addresses the common complaint that you can't get enough flavour into the base.
- Brownies should stay frozen right up until the loading phase so they don't go soggy in the warm churned ice cream.
- Reference brownie video: https://youtu.be/uBvbcW-55TY
