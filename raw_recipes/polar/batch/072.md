### [S'mores Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3vd07LekYGc)

Churned s'mores ice cream with malt-flavoured base, homemade marshmallow fluff (torched in the tub), molded milk chocolate pieces, and buttery digestive biscuit crumb — a more intense version of Ben & Jerry's Gimme S'more.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **500g** milk (1.8% fat)
- **350g** double cream (50% fat)
- **140g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **60g** SMP (skimmed milk powder)
- **10g** malt extract (brewer's/beer-making grade)
- **5g** triple-fold vanilla extract (or 10g standard shop-bought)
- Proprietary stabilizer mix (mixed with some of the sugar before adding to milk)

**Ingredients — Digestive Biscuit Crumb (graham cracker sub):**
- Digestive biscuits (quantity to taste)
- Butter (melted, quantity to taste)

**Ingredients — Marshmallow Fluff:**
- **3** egg whites
- **150g** sugar
- **50g** golden syrup (or corn syrup — not high fructose)
- Pinch of salt
- **3–5g** vanilla extract

**Ingredients — Molded Chocolate Pieces:**
- Milk chocolate (quantity to taste)
- Small amount of deodorized coconut oil

**Steps:**
1. Warm the milk in a pan. Mix the stabilizer with a portion of the sugar first, then whisk into the warm milk to remove lumps. Add remaining sugar, dextrose, and malt extract; heat and stir until fully dissolved.
2. Add SMP and continue heating until fully homogenized.
3. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, stir in the cream and vanilla extract. Chill in the fridge overnight.
4. **Biscuit crumb:** Blitz digestive biscuits to a fine powder in a blender. Mix with melted butter, press onto a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake until set. Cool completely, then refrigerate. Cut into ~1 cm cubes before use.
5. **Marshmallow fluff:** Combine egg whites, sugar, and golden syrup in a heatproof bowl over a bain-marie (inch of simmering water). Heat to 75°C, then remove from the bain-marie. Whisk vigorously with a hand mixer until the mixture cools, thickens, and becomes glossy. Stir in vanilla extract. Set aside (keeps ~48 hours covered; watch for separation if refrigerated several days).
6. **Chocolate pieces:** Melt milk chocolate in the microwave. Stir in a small amount of deodorized coconut oil (softens set so pieces are less brittle in the ice cream). Pour into a mold (Nick used coffee-bean shaped). Refrigerate until set.
7. Pre-chill the ice cream machine bowl (~10 minutes). Churn the cold base until ribbons form on top.
8. In the final minutes of churning, add the chocolate pieces and biscuit crumb cubes; churn to incorporate.
9. Transfer to tub, layer marshmallow fluff over the top. Freeze until firm.
10. When serving, torch the marshmallow fluff on top until charred and caramelized before scooping.

**Notes:**
- Malt extract sourced from a homebrew/beer-making supplier — not widely available in supermarkets. Adds a warm, malty depth to the base.
- Digestive biscuits are the UK sub for graham crackers — not identical but the closest widely available alternative.
- Deodorized coconut oil removes the coconut flavour; adding it to melted chocolate keeps pieces slightly softer and easier to unmold. Nick noted he'd add a bit more next time for a ganache-like texture rather than fully hard pieces.
- Torching the marshmallow fluff directly in the tub (or on the serving bowl) does not melt the ice cream — only the surface is touched by the flame. This delivers the key s'mores "burnt marshmallow" note missing from the original B&J version.
- Marshmallow fluff keeps ~48 hours at room temperature (covered); may start to separate after a few days in the fridge depending on ingredients.
- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
