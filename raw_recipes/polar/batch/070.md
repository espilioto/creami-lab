### [How to Make Ben & Jerry's Gimme S'more Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GjUpocuBl8)

Homemade recreation of Ben & Jerry's Gimme S'more — toasted marshmallow custard base with a cookie swirl, graham cracker (digestive biscuit) swirl, and chocolate fudge flecks.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **90g** sugar
- **250ml** milk
- **300ml** double cream
- **3** large egg yolks
- **100g** white marshmallows (good quality)

Cookie Swirl:
- **75g** chocolate cookies (Nick used Maryland Cookies)
- **~2 tbsp** chocolate syrup (to suit)

Graham Cracker Swirl:
- **75g** graham crackers / digestive biscuits
- **1.5 tbsp** honey
- **1 tbsp** butter

Fudge:
- **1 can** condensed milk
- **100g** dark chocolate

**Steps:**

1. **Custard base:** Heat sugar and milk in a pan over low heat, stirring to dissolve sugar. Whisk egg yolks in a separate bowl. Slowly temper the hot milk into the yolks, adding a little at a time and whisking constantly to avoid scrambling. Once half the milk is incorporated, return the mixture to the pan and cook on low, stirring slowly, for 5–10 minutes until slightly thickened.
2. **Toast marshmallows:** Spread marshmallows on a baking tray and toast at 150–175°C for about 10 minutes. You want colour on the outside — start under the grill, then finish with a kitchen torch if needed. Keep marshmallows hot — do not let them cool or they become impossible to handle.
3. **Blend base:** Remove custard from heat. Immediately add the hot toasted marshmallows to a blender, pour in the custard, and blend on full power for 30–60 seconds until smooth and combined. The colour will change from the burnt marshmallow. Pour into a container, cover with cling film, and refrigerate for 24 hours.
4. **Graham cracker swirl:** Crush biscuits into a bowl. Melt butter and honey together on the hob, mix through crushed biscuits until a thick paste forms. Refrigerate until ready.
5. **Cookie swirl:** Crush cookies into a bowl and mix with chocolate syrup until you have a loose, scoopable mix. Refrigerate until ready.
6. **Fudge:** Melt dark chocolate and condensed milk together in a double boiler until thoroughly combined. Pour into a cling-film-lined container and let set at room temperature or in the fridge. Once hard, grate or chop into flecks. Store fudge flecks in the freezer so they stay separated.
7. **Churn:** Stir the chilled marshmallow custard well to loosen (it gels thick from the marshmallow gelatin), then combine with the double cream. Churn in an ice cream machine (took approx. 20 minutes).
8. **Layer and swirl:** Transfer churned ice cream to your container in layers, adding the cookie swirl, graham cracker swirl, and fudge flecks between each layer. Fold and swirl gently with a spatula. Freeze until firm.

**Notes:**

- Keep marshmallows hot when transferring to the blender — once they cool they stick to everything and become very hard to work with.
- Good quality marshmallows make a significant difference to the final flavour.
- Digestive biscuits are the UK substitute for graham crackers; adding honey to the swirl compensates for the honey/cinnamon notes in authentic graham crackers.
- Grating the fudge cold from the fridge and storing the flecks in the freezer prevents them from clumping back together before mix-in.
- Don't go easy with the add-ins — generous swirls are key to the s'more experience.
- The marshmallow gelatin causes the base to thicken noticeably after 24 hours in the fridge; stir well before combining with cream.
