### [Häagen Dazs Chocolate Midnight Cookies — Plant Based](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8whcn7hwzg)

Plant-based recreation of HD Chocolate Midnight Cookies using 10% plant-based half & half cream. Batch recipe (traditional churn, not single pint).

**Ingredients:**

*Chocolate Base:*
- **700g** plant-based half & half cream (10% fat)
- **190g** sugar
- **20g** dextrose
- **65g** cocoa powder
- **1g** stabilizer (optional)

*Chocolate Fudge:*
- **50g** sugar
- **50g** 80% chocolate
- **10g** cocoa powder
- **50g** plant-based milk
- **15g** corn syrup or golden syrup
- **15g** coconut oil
- **3g** vanilla extract

*Mix-in:*
- Oreo halves (cream removed)

**Steps:**
1. Combine plant-based cream, sugar, dextrose, and cocoa powder. Heat gently to dissolve.
2. If using stabilizer, heat to activation temp. Otherwise just warm to combine.
3. Chill base completely.
4. For fudge: combine all fudge ingredients in a pan, heat until melted and smooth. Chill.
5. Churn the base in ice cream machine.
6. Layer churned ice cream with fudge drizzle and Oreo halves into tubs.
7. Freeze overnight.

**Notes:**
- Nick uses Silk brand plant cream (coconut-based, 10% fat) in Canada; recommends Elmlea plant cream for UK.
- Coconut-based creams add an unwanted coconut undertone — Nick specifically dislikes this but works around it with strong cocoa.
- Stats: 8.8% fat, 19.9% sugar, 40% solids.
- Nick says no one could tell this from dairy — it's that good.
