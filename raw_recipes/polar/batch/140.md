### [Häagen Dazs Strawberry Cheesecake Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtnwc1K06o8)

High-fat strawberry cheesecake ice cream with cream cheese base, strawberry sauce swirl, and baked graham cracker crumb pieces. Batch recipe (traditional churn).

**Ingredients:**

*Strawberry Sauce:*
- **500g** ripe strawberries
- **100g** sugar
- **15g** lemon juice

*Cheesecake Base:*
- **250g** cream (33% fat)
- **125g** cream cheese (27% fat)
- **500g** milk (2% fat)
- **160g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **65g** skimmed milk powder

*Cheesecake Crumb:*
- **85g** butter
- **140g** biscuit crumb (graham cracker or digestive)
- **15g** sugar

**Steps:**
1. Cook strawberries with sugar and lemon juice until broken down. Strain through fine mesh sieve. Refrigerate overnight.
2. Gently heat milk, sugar, dextrose, and SMP. Add cream cheese and cream, stir until smooth. Heat to 80°C if using stabilizer. Chill overnight.
3. Mix butter into biscuit crumbs and sugar. Bake at 360°F for 10 min. Chill 24h in fridge.
4. Next day: churn the cheesecake base.
5. Layer churned ice cream with strawberry sauce and crumb pieces into tubs. Stir gently.
6. Freeze 6-8 hours.

**Notes:**
- High fat is intentional — cream cheese flavor needs it to come through properly.
- The strawberry sauce must be strained to remove seeds and pulp.
- All three components are made a day ahead — the recipe requires overnight rests.
