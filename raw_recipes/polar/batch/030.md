### [Biscoff Cookies n Cream Ice Cream - Speculoos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LV7Du-aFQ1s) ice cream base loaded with chopped Biscoff sandwich cookies mixed in after churning.

**Ingredients:**

- **500ml** whole milk
- **400ml** cream
- **160g** sugar
- **200g** Biscoff spread
- **1 pack** Biscoff sandwich cookies (Lotus Biscoff — cookie rounds with biscuit cream filling)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk and sugar in a saucepan. Heat gently, stirring, until the sugar is fully dissolved.
2. Add 200g Biscoff spread and whisk until fully incorporated into the milk-sugar base.
3. Remove from heat. Add the cream directly to the hot base — this drops the temperature quickly and reduces fridge time needed.
4. Transfer to a container and refrigerate for 1–2 hours until fully chilled.
5. Churn in an ice cream machine for approximately 10–30 minutes (time varies by machine) until done.
6. While churning, chop the Biscoff sandwich cookies into a mix of small pieces and larger chunks — avoid making them all uniformly fine.
7. Once churned, layer the ice cream into a tub, folding in generous amounts of chopped cookies throughout. Do not scrimp — use the whole pack.
8. Freeze for several hours until firm.

**Notes:**

- Nick notes this is a no-egg base — simpler than his usual custard method for this flavour.
- Adding cream to the hot base (rather than chilling the base first) is a quick-cool trick that cuts fridge time to 1–2 hours instead of longer.
- Biscoff sandwich cookies (cookie rounds with cream filling) are not available everywhere — substitute plain Lotus Biscoff biscuits if needed, though the sandwich version adds extra richness.
- Use the full pack of cookies and don't chop them too fine — having some larger chunks gives better texture contrast.
- The base is heavily Biscoff-flavoured, so pairing it with plenty of cookies makes the most of it.
