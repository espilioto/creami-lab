### [Making Salt & Straw Peppermint Patty Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giXf2hPTdq0)

Salt & Straw's cocoa peppermint base churned with a semi-liquid sweetened condensed milk peppermint fondant core piped into the center.

**Ingredients:**

*Salt & Straw Standard Ice Cream Base:*
- **110g** granulated sugar
- **30g** skim milk powder
- **1.2g** xanthan gum
- **30g** light corn syrup
- **332g** whole milk
- **332g** heavy cream

*Cocoa Addition (mixed into base):*
- **55g** granulated sugar
- **30g** unsweetened cocoa powder (not drinking chocolate)
- **45g** water
- **5g** peppermint extract (or 1 drop peppermint oil; Nick uses more than 5g personally)

*Peppermint Patty Core (batch — description lists half amounts for single pint):*
- **240g** sweetened condensed milk (approx. 2/3 of a standard can)
- **200g** powdered/icing sugar
- **30g** water (reduce to ~15g for a firmer core; add up to 45g for a looser, more pourable core)
- Peppermint extract — to taste
- **~0.6g** fine salt (pinch, to balance flavor)

**Steps:**

1. Whisk together sugar, skim milk powder, and xanthan gum in a small pot to combine dry ingredients.
2. In a separate saucepan, combine corn syrup and whole milk. Add the dry mixture and stir. Heat gently until sugar dissolves — do not superheat. Remove from heat.
3. Stir in heavy cream. Transfer to a container and refrigerate at least 6 hours, preferably 24 hours (better flavor).
4. For the cocoa addition: combine 55g sugar and 45g water in a small saucepan. Bring to a rapid boil, then immediately remove from heat. Quickly stir in 30g cocoa powder. Cool to room temperature (about 30 minutes, or over a cold water bath) before adding peppermint extract. Do not add extract while hot — volatile flavor compounds will burn off.
5. Once both components are fully cold, whisk the cocoa-mint mixture into the chilled base until fully incorporated (about 1 minute). If it seizes cold from the fridge, loosen by briefly stirring — avoid refrigerating the cocoa mix, as it becomes too stiff to blend.
6. Churn combined base as normal in an ice cream machine. Fill churning tubs without overfilling — leave room for the core.
7. Freeze churned base for 1–2 hours until firm enough to hold a carved core channel.
8. For the peppermint core: combine condensed milk and water in a small saucepan. Heat gently to 40–50°C — just enough to thin the condensed milk. Do not boil. Remove from heat.
9. Stir in 200g powdered sugar and peppermint extract until smooth. Add salt. The mixture should be pipeable or pourable. Adjust water for desired texture in the freezer.
10. Carve or scoop a channel down the center of the partially frozen ice cream. Pipe or pour the peppermint core into the channel. Return to freezer until fully set.

**Notes:**

- This recipe is a batch — the description's peppermint core amounts (120g condensed milk, 100g powdered sugar, 15g water, 5–10g extract, ~0.3g salt) are half the video batch quantities; use those for a single pint.
- The Salt & Straw method involves making a universal base and adding flavor components per recipe — the cocoa-peppermint addition is not part of the base itself.
- Do not refrigerate the cocoa addition overnight as the book suggests — it becomes too stiff to incorporate into the cold base cold. Keep it at room temperature until needed.
- Core consistency is adjustable: 30g water = semi-firm, scoopable core; 15g water = very firm; 45g water = soft, pourable, won't freeze solid.
- Peppermint oil (1 drop) is the recipe's spec, but peppermint extract requires more (5g+). Nick uses more than specified.
- The core must be added to a partially frozen (1–2 hr) base — too soft and the channel fills back in before the core sets.
