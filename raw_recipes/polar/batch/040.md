### [Chocolate Orange Cinnamon Rolls + Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uiKE7zYouXI)

Chocolate orange cinnamon buns with cream cheese frosting, served with or churned into a vanilla cinnamon ice cream. Batch recipe — bun dough yields multiple rolls.

---

#### Cinnamon Bun — Chocolate Dough

**Ingredients:**
- **60g** unsalted butter
- **220g** whole milk
- **11g** fast-action dried yeast (1 packet)
- **50g** caster sugar
- **1** medium egg, room temperature
- **1** medium egg yolk, room temperature
- **460g** strong white bread flour, plus more for dusting
- **50g** cocoa powder (Menier 100% Cocoa Powder)
- **5g** salt

**Steps:**
1. Melt the butter, then warm the milk to 50–60°C. Whisk in the yeast and leave a few minutes to hydrate.
2. Add flour, sugar, salt, cocoa powder, whole egg, and egg yolk to the stand mixer bowl with the dough hook. Pour in the butter, then the milk-yeast mixture.
3. Mix on medium speed for about 7 minutes until the dough comes away from the sides of the bowl and is smooth and springy.
4. Lightly oil a bowl, add the dough, cover, and leave to prove for 90 minutes to 2 hours until doubled in size.
5. Roll dough out to roughly 14 in × 9 in rectangle on a lightly floured surface.

---

#### Cinnamon Bun — Orange Cinnamon Sugar Filling

**Ingredients:**
- **60g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **120g** light brown sugar / muscovado sugar
- **20g** ground cinnamon
- **1** large orange, zest only

**Steps:**
1. Mix cinnamon into the brown sugar until evenly coated (cinnamon coats the molasses, making it less sticky and easier to spread).
2. Smear softened butter over the rolled-out dough, then sprinkle the cinnamon sugar evenly across the surface.
3. Grate the orange zest over the top.
4. Roll the dough tightly along the long edge into a cylinder. Trim the ends and cut into 1½-inch slices.
5. Place rolls in a baking pan. Leave to prove again for another hour.
6. Bake until cooked through. Cool before frosting.

**Notes:**
- If making a larger batch, freeze rolls after cooling but before frosting — they keep for months.

#### Cinnamon Bun — Chocolate Orange Cream Cheese Frosting

**Ingredients:**
- **50g** milk chocolate (Menier Swiss Milk Chocolate)
- **75g** full-fat cream cheese, room temperature
- **50g** icing sugar (powdered sugar)
- **1** large orange, juice only (use the orange zested for the filling)

**Steps:**
1. Warm the cream cheese in the microwave for about 20 seconds until malleable.
2. Beat together cream cheese and icing sugar until combined.
3. Melt the milk chocolate. Pour into the cream cheese mixture and stir until fully combined.
4. Add orange juice and mix through. Spread or drizzle over cooled cinnamon buns.

#### Vanilla Cinnamon Ice Cream (Ninja Creami / churned)

Condensed milk base — super simple.

**Ingredients:**
- **350g** whole milk
- **350g** whipping cream
- **300ml** sweetened condensed milk
- **1½ tsp** vanilla bean paste
- **½ tsp** ground cinnamon (adjust to taste)

**Steps:**
1. Whisk all ingredients together until completely combined.
2. **For Ninja Creami:** Pour into a pint tub, freeze solid (24h+), then spin on Ice Cream mode.
3. **For churned:** Pre-chill machine, pour in the base, churn until set.
4. To assemble: Chop cooled cinnamon buns into small cubes. Load chunks between layers of spun/churned ice cream. Do not add bun pieces before spinning — add after the spin is complete to keep them chunky.

**Notes:**
- Cinnamon bun pieces should be refrigerator-cold (not frozen) when added as mix-in after spinning.
- Do not use the Mix-In program on the Ninja Creami for the bun pieces — it will decimate them. Fold in manually after spinning.
- The vanilla bean paste boosts the cinnamon flavour.
- Nick uses a traditional ice cream machine for the video demo, but explicitly confirms the Ninja Creami method works: pour, freeze, spin as normal.
