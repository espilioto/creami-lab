### [Avengers Hunka Hulka Burning Fudge Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gb6HHMrvgCg)

A fictional Marvel Avengers ice cream brought to life: green marshmallow base with grated dark chocolate–condensed milk fudge flecks, a pourable chocolate fudge core, and a smashable green white chocolate crack top.

**Ingredients:**

*Ice Cream Base:*
- **400ml** milk
- **500ml** double cream
- **150g** sugar
- **100g** marshmallows (good quality, heavy on vanilla flavour), chopped
- Green food colouring

*Dark Chocolate Fudge (for flecks):*
- **400ml** condensed milk
- **340g** dark chocolate

*Fudge Core:*
- **125g** Dairy Milk chocolate
- **125g** butter
- **1** egg
- **30g** caster sugar
- **15g** glucose syrup

*Green Crack Top:*
- **75g** white chocolate
- **6ml** water (just over 1 tsp)
- Green food colouring

**Steps:**

1. Make the dark chocolate fudge: melt condensed milk and dark chocolate together in a bain-marie over low heat until fully combined. Pour into a mould and refrigerate until set. Once set, transfer a portion to the freezer so it's firm enough to grate.
2. Make the ice cream base: combine milk, cream, sugar, and chopped marshmallows in a pan. Heat slowly, stirring, until the marshmallows and sugar are fully dissolved and incorporated. Add green food colouring to desired hue. Chill completely in the fridge.
3. Make the fudge core mixture: melt Dairy Milk chocolate and butter together (microwave or bain-marie). In a separate bowl, whisk the egg and caster sugar, then combine with the melted chocolate mixture. Whisk together quickly. Add glucose syrup and mix in — this keeps the core from freezing too hard.
4. Churn the chilled base in your ice cream machine. Five minutes before the end of the churn, remove the frozen fudge block from the freezer and grate it. Add the grated fudge flecks into the machine to distribute throughout the ice cream.
5. Transfer the churned ice cream to a tub or container. Insert a plastic tube (sealed at the bottom) down through the centre to form a well for the core. Freeze for 10–15 minutes to firm up around the mould.
6. Remove the plastic tube. Pour the liquid fudge core mix into the cavity.
7. Make the crack top: gently melt white chocolate with the water to make a water ganache. Stir in green food colouring.
8. Pour the green chocolate ganache over the top of the ice cream. Freeze for at least 4 hours until the crack top is fully hardened.
9. Smash through the crack top to serve.

**Notes:**
- Use good-quality marshmallows with a strong vanilla flavour — cheaper marshmallows don't melt down properly and won't give the same result.
- The dark chocolate fudge melts very quickly at room temperature — work fast when grating it and return it to the freezer immediately if needed.
- Glucose syrup in the fudge core is key: it lowers the freezing point so the core stays soft and scoopable rather than rock-hard.
- The water ganache (white chocolate + a small amount of water) creates the crackable top — the small water addition is deliberate to adjust the set texture.
- Nick noted in hindsight he should have called it a "Smash Top" rather than a "Crack Top" — a nod to the Hulk.
- This is a creative interpretation; there is no official recipe for Hunka Hulka Burning Fudge — it's a fictional ice cream from the Avengers films.
