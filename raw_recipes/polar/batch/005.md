### [AMAZING Ninja Creami Pistachio Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyurIaOK6Eo)

Real pistachio gelato using shelled salted roasted pistachios blended into a paste with hot milk — no cream, just nut fat for gelato-level richness.

**Ingredients:**

- **75g** shelled salted roasted pistachios (start with ~150g in-shell; yield is ~50%)
- **358g** 2% milk
- **90g** sugar
- **5g** invert sugar (corn syrup, golden syrup, or glucose syrup)
- **20g** skim milk powder (zero fat / lowfat milk powder)
- **¼ tsp** guar gum
- **1 tsp** sugar (used to disperse the guar gum — taken from the 90g total)

**Steps:**

1. Shell the pistachios and weigh out 75g of nut meat.
2. Add the pistachios to a small blender (e.g. KitchenAid desktop blender) along with half a tablespoon of sugar from your measured amount.
3. Blitz briefly for a couple of minutes into a rough paste. Do not use a food processor — it will overheat and still won't get smooth.
4. Take roughly a quarter of the total milk (~100ml) and warm it in the microwave until just hot enough to touch.
5. Spoon the warm milk into the blender with the pistachio paste a little at a time. Blitz between additions until the mixture loosens into a thin, smooth pistachio liquid.
6. Mix the guar gum with 1 scant teaspoon of sugar (taken from the measured amount) and blend dry to eliminate lumps.
7. In the Ninja Creami pint, combine the remaining milk, skim milk powder, remaining sugar, and the invert sugar. Microwave briefly to warm and dissolve the sugar and milk powder.
8. Stir the guar gum / sugar blend into the warm milk mixture until fully incorporated.
9. Add the pistachio paste liquid and stir everything together well, scraping it all out of the blender.
10. Pour the base into the Ninja Creami pint, filling to the max line.
11. Freeze for at least 24 hours (Nick froze for a couple of days).
12. During the first few hours of freezing, periodically stir the base (whisk, spoon, or fork) to prevent the nut solids from sinking and settling at the bottom.
13. Spin on the Gelato setting.
14. Serve immediately.

**Notes:**

- 150g of in-shell salted pistachios yields approximately 75g of usable nut meat — expect roughly 50% shell waste.
- Buy pistachios from a cheaper outlet (dollar store, etc.) since you'll be processing them — brand quality doesn't matter here.
- Don't worry about bits of papery purple skin on some nuts; it gets decimated during the spin.
- The fat from 75g of pistachios is what gives this recipe its gelato-level fat content — no cream needed. High-fat nuts like walnuts could work similarly.
- Nick chose not to use a hot-processed stabilizer — the guar gum is added cold/warm. He notes a stabilizer (even just guar gum) really helps texture.
- Pistachio is a light, delicate flavor that benefits from a thick, dense, low-overrun texture — exactly what the Ninja Creami delivers. Gelato setting is appropriate here.
- No-cook method: everything is done with a microwave. No stove required.
