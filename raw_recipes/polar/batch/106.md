### [Making Ben & Jerry's Marshmallow Sky ice cream at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PApVMCFSApc)

Homemade dupe of a B&J scoop-shop-only flavour: blue spirulina vanilla ice cream with chocolate chip cookie dough, double chocolate chip cookie dough, and marshmallow swirls.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **400ml** milk (2% fat)
- **300ml** cream (49% fat / double or heavy cream)
- **65g** sugar
- **65g** dextrose
- **25g** skimmed milk powder
- **75g** marshmallows (gelatin-based — important)
- **2-3 tsp** vanilla extract
- **1/2 tsp** blue spirulina mixed with **1 tsp** water (added cold)

Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough:
- **60g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **45g** sugar
- **40g** brown sugar
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **2 tbsp** heavy/double cream
- **2 tbsp** golden syrup (or corn syrup)
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **70g** plain (all-purpose) flour, heat-treated
- **45g** semi-sweet chocolate, chopped

Double Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough:
- **60g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **45g** sugar
- **40g** brown sugar
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **2 tbsp** heavy/double cream
- **2 tbsp** golden syrup (or corn syrup)
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **50g** plain (all-purpose) flour, heat-treated
- **20g** cocoa powder
- **45g** semi-sweet chocolate, chopped

Marshmallow Swirl:
- **2** egg whites
- **100g** sugar
- **1/4 tsp** cream of tartar

**Steps:**

1. Heat-treat the flour for both cookie doughs: spread on a tray and bake at 180°C for 10 minutes. Let cool 15 minutes, then sieve (use a metal sieve, not plastic).
2. **Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough:** Beat together butter, sugar, brown sugar, and salt. Add golden syrup, vanilla extract, and cream. Mix in the 70g heat-treated flour, then fold in chopped chocolate. Place in a container and freeze.
3. **Double Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough:** Make exactly as above, but use 50g flour + 20g cocoa powder instead of 70g flour. Fold in chopped chocolate. Freeze.
4. **Ice cream base:** Add milk, sugar, and dextrose to a pan over medium heat. Chop the marshmallows and melt them into the hot milk mixture. Remove from heat, pour into a container, and stir in the cream. Once cooled enough, stir in vanilla extract and skimmed milk powder. Mix spirulina powder with 1 tsp water, add to base and stir. Refrigerate until fully cold.
5. **Marshmallow swirl:** Combine egg whites, sugar, and cream of tartar in a heatproof bowl. Heat over a bain-marie, stirring constantly, until mixture reaches 70°C. Remove from heat and beat with a hand mixer on maximum speed for ~5 minutes until thick and glossy. Refrigerate until needed; bring out slightly before assembly to soften.
6. Chop frozen cookie doughs into small cubes; return to freezer.
7. Churn the cold ice cream base in an ice cream machine (Nick uses a Lello 5030). Once churned, layer ice cream, cookie dough cubes (both kinds), and marshmallow swirl into the tub. Freeze for several hours before serving.

**Notes:**

- This is a **traditional churned recipe** (not Ninja Creami). Uses an ice cream machine to churn before packing into a tub.
- Cookie doughs are adapted from Salt & Straw's recipe — noted to be "really good."
- B&J's original uses pectin instead of actual marshmallow to create the marshmallow texture. Nick uses real gelatin-based marshmallows melted into the base.
- The marshmallow swirl is technically a Swiss meringue — it behaves like a marshmallow swirl when frozen.
- Blue spirulina is critical to the visual identity of this flavour; must be added cold to preserve the blue colour.
- If dextrose is unavailable, substitute with an equal weight of sugar (use 130g total sugar instead of 65g sugar + 65g dextrose).
- Batch size is larger than a standard single pint — the churner used is a Lello 5030 (1.5L capacity).
