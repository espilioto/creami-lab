### [AMAZING Ninja Creami Piña Colada Ice Cream - Home made glacé cherries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PhGy_aFeFM8)

Sugar-free dairy-free coconut ice cream base with a boozy rum-pineapple swirl and homemade glacé (candied) cherries. Batch recipe (~750g, fills one Ninja Creami Deluxe pint).

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **165g** coconut cream
- **439g** coconut milk
- **20g** water (to top up to volume)
- **37g** inulin
- **12g** erythritol (do not increase — inhibits freezing)
- **90g** allulose (or 55g xylitol)
- **2.5g** xanthan gum

**Ingredients — Pineapple Rum Sauce:**
- **540g** canned pineapple chunks in juice (1 × 540ml can — use both fruit and juice)
- **75g** rum (any rum you have on hand)
- **3g** xanthan gum

**Ingredients — Homemade Glacé Cherries (batch):**
- **400g** fresh sweet cherries (weight includes pits and stalks — before pitting)
- **200g** sugar
- Small splash of water (to help dissolve sugar)
- Invert sugar or corn syrup (optional — glucose syrup, golden syrup, or corn syrup all work)

**Steps — Pineapple Rum Sauce:**
1. Empty the entire 540ml can of pineapple chunks and juice into a small saucepan.
2. Heat on the hob over medium heat, bring to a boil and reduce down.
3. Remove from heat once reduced. Let cool for approximately 2 hours until almost at room temperature.
4. Once cooled, sprinkle in 3g xanthan gum (use xanthan — not guar, which doesn't work well in acidic foods).
5. Blitz with a hand blender or small blender. Leave some pineapple chunks for texture or strain through a fine mesh sieve for a smooth sauce — your choice.
6. Stir in 75g rum. Cool completely in the fridge overnight before using.

**Steps — Homemade Glacé Cherries:**
1. Pit 400g fresh sweet cherries (the 400g is the pre-pitting weight).
2. Place pitted cherries into a pan with a small splash of water and 200g sugar. Add invert sugar / glucose syrup / golden syrup if using.
3. Heat on the hob until the mixture reaches 235°F (113°C) — soft ball stage. This is when the sugar becomes invert sugar.
4. Remove from heat and let cool completely to room temperature. The cherries will look translucent when done.
5. Transfer to a jar or container and store in the fridge. They will keep for months due to the high sugar / invert sugar content.

**Steps — Ice Cream Base:**
1. Combine coconut cream, coconut milk, water, inulin, erythritol, allulose (or xylitol), and xanthan gum in a saucepan.
2. Heat on the hob for 5 minutes, stirring, until the mixture reaches 40–50°C. This is just enough to melt the coconut fat solids and disperse the xanthan gum (xanthan is a cold-process stabiliser — no need to boil).
3. Pour into a Ninja Creami Deluxe pint — exactly 750g.
4. Freeze overnight (minimum 24 hours).

**Steps — Spin and Serve:**
1. Process the frozen pint on Ice Cream.
2. Create a well in the centre and add a generous pour of the rum-pineapple sauce. Process on Mix-In.
3. Top with homemade glacé cherries to serve. Optionally garnish with a fresh pineapple wedge.

**Notes:**
- Batch recipe: the glacé cherries make more than needed for one pint — they keep for months in the fridge and can be used in baking, cakes, and future ice cream batches.
- If using xylitol instead of allulose, reduce from 90g to 55g.
- The erythritol amount (12g) is intentionally small — its role here is solely to adjust the freezing point, not sweetness. Do not increase it or the base will struggle to freeze.
- Nick notes that coconut ice cream with a small amount of dairy added is better than fully plant-based due to what milk solids contribute — but this plant-based version performs very well.
- Cherry recipe sourced from Bigger Bolder Baking (Gemma, Irish chef). The invert sugar prevents recrystallisation so the cherries stay shelf-stable for months.
- Nick had to develop the cherry recipe three times before getting it right — this version is the one that worked.
- The glacé cherries are called "candied cherries" in the Americas and "glacé cherries" in Europe.
