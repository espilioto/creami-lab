### [Making Cinnamon Snickerdoodle ice cream by Salt & Straw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3fZjgE0tN7Q)

Salt & Straw's cinnamon ice cream with homemade snickerdoodle cookies folded in, churned in a traditional ice cream maker.

---

**Ingredients — Snickerdoodle Cookies:**
- **100g** sugar
- **57g** butter
- **1** small egg
- **110g** flour
- **2.5g** salt
- **4g** cinnamon
- **5g** baking powder (UK: baking powder; US original: baking soda + cream of tartar)

**Ingredients — Cinnamon Ice Cream Base:**
- **100g** sugar
- **30g** skimmed milk powder
- **1g** xanthan gum
- **30g** light corn syrup / glucose syrup / golden syrup
- **322g** whole milk
- **326g** heavy cream
- **20g** ground cinnamon (Red Ape Cinnamon in the original)
- Pinch kosher salt
- Pinch ancho chile powder

---

**Steps:**

1. **Cookies — cream:** Beat butter and sugar together (stand mixer with paddle, or hand mixer) until light and airy.
2. Add egg and whisk until well combined.
3. Add flour, salt, and baking powder (see note). Mix until incorporated. If dough is too wet, add up to 30g more flour.
4. Refrigerate dough for a couple of hours.
5. Roll dough into small balls, coat in a cinnamon-sugar mix, place on a baking tray.
6. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20 minutes, rotating the tray halfway through. Cool completely and set aside.
7. **Ice cream base — dry mix:** Combine xanthan gum, sugar, and skimmed milk powder in a bowl.
8. Add corn syrup (or glucose syrup) and milk to a saucepan and warm gently.
9. Add the dry ingredient mix, stirring until sugar and milk powder are fully dissolved. Remove from heat.
10. Transfer to a container, add heavy cream, stir to combine. Chill in the fridge for 2–3 hours.
11. Remove base from fridge; stir in ground cinnamon and ancho chile powder. Return to fridge for 10–15 minutes to allow spices to hydrate. (Note: spices will clump rather than fully dissolve — consider blending instead.)
12. Pre-chill ice cream maker bowl for 10–15 minutes, then churn the base. Recipe is low in sugar so it churns quickly — approximately 8 minutes.
13. In the last couple of minutes of churning, add crumbled/chopped snickerdoodle cookies (roughly 0.5–1 cm pieces). If the machine is not strong enough to incorporate them, fold in by hand after transferring to the tub.
14. Transfer to tub, freeze overnight.

---

**Notes:**
- Recipe is from the Salt & Straw ice cream cookbook. Available via saltandstraw.com and major booksellers.
- UK/US leavening swap: US recipe uses baking soda + cream of tartar; in the UK, baking powder already contains the acid so cream of tartar is not needed.
- Red Ape Cinnamon is specified in the original but not available in the UK — any good-quality ground cinnamon works.
- The chile powder amount is not listed in the book; Salt & Straw confirmed it is "just a pinch" of ancho chile powder.
- Cinnamon tends to clump when stirred into the cold base; blending it in (rather than stirring) would give a smoother result.
- The base is relatively low in sugar, which causes it to churn and firm up faster than typical recipes (~8 min in a Cuisinart 5030).
- Batch recipe: uses a traditional churning ice cream maker, not a Ninja Creami.
