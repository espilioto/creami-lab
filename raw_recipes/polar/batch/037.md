### [Chicken & Waffle Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=boeFYmuu8FU)

Maple syrup ice cream with chicken-fat-fried Liege waffle pieces — a savory-sweet combo inspired by Salt & Straw and Made By Marcus, but done Nick's way.

**Ingredients:**

Ice cream base:
- **400g** milk (1.8–2% fat)
- **500g** double/heavy cream
- **30g** light brown sugar
- **150g** maple syrup (real maple syrup — not maple-type syrup)
- **67g** SMP (skimmed milk powder)
- **100g** dextrose
- **1.5g** stabilizer (guar gum + locust bean gum, pre-mixed into the sugar)

Waffle pieces:
- Liege waffles (quantity to taste), chopped into cubes
- Chicken fat / schmaltz (rendered — enough to coat and fry the waffle pieces)
- Small amount of sugar (for final caramelization toss)

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a saucepan. Pre-mix the stabilizer (guar gum + locust bean gum) into the light brown sugar to prevent clumping, then add the sugar mix to the pan.
2. Add dextrose and maple syrup. Slowly bring up to temperature, stirring to dissolve the sugars — no eggs in this recipe, so no specific target temperature, just dissolve everything well.
3. Add the skimmed milk powder (SMP) and stir until fully dissolved into the base.
4. Remove from heat and transfer to a container. Add double cream, give a final stir, and refrigerate for a few hours.
5. Chop Liege waffles into small cube-sized pieces.
6. Melt a generous amount of chicken schmaltz (rendered chicken fat) in a frying pan. Add waffle pieces and fry for ~5 minutes, allowing the fat to soak into the starch. Add more fat as needed.
7. Drain off excess fat, then continue cooking for another minute or so to crisp up the cubes.
8. Sprinkle a small amount of sugar over the waffle pieces and toss in the pan for ~30 seconds to caramelize. Remove from heat and cool completely.
9. Churn the ice cream base in your ice cream machine. In the last few minutes of churning, add the cooled chicken-fried waffle pieces and mix through until combined.
10. Tub up and freeze.

**Notes:**

- Dextrose is used instead of sucrose for a softer, more scoopable texture — less hardness from the freezer.
- Pre-mixing gums into sugar before adding to liquid prevents clumping.
- Double cream is added off the heat (after the base is removed from the stove), which is Nick's standard method.
- Chicken schmaltz is just rendered chicken fat — a jarred product. The fat is deliberately soaked into the waffle starch, not just used as a fry medium.
- The chicken element is subtle and in the background — by design. It's not an aggressive chicken flavor; it's savory depth from the fat-fried waffle.
- The sugar toss at the end gives a sweet-bite caramelized crunch to the waffle pieces that contrasts the maple ice cream.
- Use real maple syrup — maple-type syrup tastes poor and may require switching flavors altogether.
- Nick notes the waffle pieces retain good crunch even after churning and freezing.
- Serve with Liege waffles on the side and maple syrup drizzle as topping to complete the classic chicken-and-waffle trio.
