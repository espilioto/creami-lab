### [Authentic Japanese Matcha Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gOmZtb6VYAk)

Ultra-low-fat gelato (6% fat) made with ceremonial-grade matcha, kept cold-processed to preserve the vivid green color and bold umami flavor.

**Ingredients:**

- **30g** organic ceremonial-grade matcha powder (any grade works; ceremonial preferred)
- **500g** 2% milk (Nick noted the corrected batch uses more milk — see Notes)
- **125g** 33% whipping cream
- **30g** skim milk powder
- **120g** sugar (110g added to base; 10g reserved to mix with stabilizer)
- **40g** dextrose
- Guar gum (amount unspecified — mixed with the reserved 10g sugar for cold-process dispersion)

**Steps:**

1. Combine the 500g milk, 125g cream, 40g dextrose, 110g sugar, skim milk powder, and 30g matcha powder in a saucepan.
2. Mix the reserved 10g sugar with the guar gum, then add to the pan.
3. Gently warm on the hob to ~55–60°C (steam point only — do not overheat, to avoid cooked dairy flavor overpowering the matcha).
4. Remove from heat. Transfer to a container and let cool.
5. Once cool, strain through a fine sieve to remove any matcha lumps or impurities.
6. Refrigerate for at least a few hours; overnight or 24h is ideal to let the matcha flavor bloom into the dairy.
7. Churn in an ice cream machine until the desired consistency is reached.

**Notes:**

- Nick filmed two batches. The first used the recipe as listed above and produced almost no overrun — the high fiber content of matcha made the mix too viscous. He redid it with more milk to reduce viscosity and achieve better overrun. The description does not list the corrected amounts; use more milk (exact amount not specified on-screen) if overrun is poor.
- Dextrose is used instead of more sugar to reduce sweetness, letting the matcha flavor dominate, while also helping lower the freezing point.
- Guar gum is a cold-process stabilizer — no need to heat above 60°C.
- Matcha fiber (insoluble) boosts overrun potential but also increases viscosity; balance is key.
- The cream smooths out matcha's natural bitterness; if omitting cream, increase stabilizer and consider adding another body builder.
- Strain after chilling, not before — this avoids pushing large particles through the sieve.
- Serving tip: the flavor is very intense — a small quenelle portion is sufficient.
- This is a traditional churned gelato recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
