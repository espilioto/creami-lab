### [Nick's Darkest Chocolate Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=99Oh_jGLVp4)

A triple-source chocolate base (physical chocolate + high-quality cocoa + dark chocolate sauce) inspired by the original Jeni's Darkest Chocolate recipe before it was simplified for retail.

**Ingredients:**

- **250ml** whole milk
- **300ml** double cream
- **120g** sugar
- **15g** dextrose
- **50g** 70% dark chocolate (chips or chopped)
- **3 tsp** high-quality cocoa powder
- **1 tsp** 100% Dutch-process cocoa powder (optional — color only)
- **10g** skimmed milk powder
- **0.5 tsp** instant coffee / espresso powder
- **0.25 tsp** kosher salt
- Monin Dark Chocolate Sauce (or homemade — see Notes), to layer

**Steps:**

1. Add milk, cream, and sugar to a saucepan and gently heat.
2. Once warmed, add the chopped/chip chocolate and stir until fully melted and combined.
3. Add cocoa powder (both regular and 100% Dutch if using), instant coffee, and salt. Stir well.
4. Add skimmed milk powder and combine thoroughly. Turn off the heat.
5. Chill the mix in the fridge for a few hours.
6. Churn in your ice cream machine until done.
7. If your machine allows, add additional chopped 70% chocolate before the end of churning; otherwise fold it in after batching.
8. Layer in dark chocolate sauce (Monin or homemade) as you pack the container.
9. Freeze for at least 4–6 hours before scooping.

**Notes:**

- Nick renamed this from "Jeni's Darkest Chocolate" to his own version — the original Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams book recipe used cream cheese and physical chocolate; the current retail product uses only cocoa powder.
- The 100% Dutch-process cocoa powder is purely for deeper color, not flavor — skip if not available.
- The dark chocolate sauce is a key layered element; Nick uses Monin brand. A homemade version (from description): melt 200g 70% chocolate, heat 175ml milk + 2 tbsp double cream + 30g caster sugar, whisk together onto chocolate, bubble briefly, whisk in 30g diced butter, pass through sieve.
- Extra chopped 70% chocolate can be added as a mix-in at end of churn or folded in post-batch.
