### [McDonald's Shamrock Shake & McFlurry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wWkmo72umjw)

Homemade copycat of the McDonald's Shamrock Shake milkshake and Shamrock Oreo McFlurry — both made from the same vanilla-mint ice cream base churned in an ice cream machine.

**Ingredients:**

- **500ml** double (heavy) cream
- **250ml** milk
- **150g** sugar
- **2–3 tbsp** fat-free (1%) milk powder (skim milk powder)
- **2 tbsp** vanilla extract
- **1/16 tsp** peppermint extract
- Green food colouring, to taste
- Crushed Oreos (for McFlurry mix-in and decoration)
- Whipped cream (from cream whipper, for Shamrock Shake topping)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk and sugar in a bowl and whisk until sugar fully dissolves.
2. Add skim milk powder, stir in, then refrigerate for 20 minutes.
3. Remove from fridge, stir again, then add the cream and vanilla extract.
4. Add the peppermint extract (just 1/16 tsp — it's a very subtle hint, not mint ice cream).
5. Add green food colouring to desired shade and give a final stir.
6. Pour the base into an ice cream machine and churn 15–25 minutes (depending on machine size) until soft-serve consistency.
7. **For the McFlurry:** ~5 minutes before churning finishes, add approximately 75% of the crushed Oreos into the machine; reserve the rest for decoration. Once done, scoop into a glass/cup and top with remaining crushed Oreos.
8. **For the Shamrock Shake:** Transfer the churned ice cream to a container and freeze for several hours. When ready to serve, blend the frozen ice cream with a splash of milk until milkshake consistency, pour into a glass, and top with whipped cream from a cream whipper.

**Notes:**

- This is the same base for both products — make one batch and split it between the two.
- Key flavour insight: McDonald's Shamrock Shake is **vanilla ice cream with a touch of mint** (not mint ice cream). Using actual mint ice cream gives the wrong flavour profile.
- Peppermint extract is very potent — 1/16 tsp is intentional; don't increase it.
- If making the milkshake from frozen ice cream, add extra green food colouring and a touch more peppermint to the glass before blending — some colour and flavour intensity is lost when blending with milk.
- McFlurry requires soft-serve texture, so an ice cream machine is strongly recommended; a no-churn base works but won't replicate the soft-serve result.
- Recipe makes enough for both a milkshake and a McFlurry from a single batch.
