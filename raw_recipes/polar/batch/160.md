### [ULTIMATE Butter Pecan Ice Cream — Musso & Creami](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o2ArE5TzUuY)

Custard-based butter pecan ice cream made with browned butter, dark brown sugar, and vanilla bean paste, churned in a Musso machine — with a bonus Ninja Creami variation using pecan pie as a mix-in.

**Batch size:** ~1.6–1.7L after churn overrun (enough for 2 Ninja Creami Deluxe pints or 2⅔ standard pints). A portion (~400g) is reserved and frozen in a standard Creami tub for the pecan pie variation.

**Ingredients:**

Ice cream base:
- **500g** milk (2% or full fat)
- **544g** whipping cream (33%; 36% also fine)
- **168g** dark brown sugar (or white sugar + molasses combined)
- **56g** dextrose (or corn syrup)
- **67g** skim milk powder
- **10g** browned butter (reserved from the pecan step below)
- **125g** egg yolks (~6 eggs)
- **25–30g** vanilla bean paste (or vanilla extract)

Butter-toasted pecans (add-in):
- **150g** pecans (broken in half)
- **50g** salted butter (good quality, grass-fed preferred)

Optional Ninja Creami variation:
- 1 small pecan pie (store-bought or homemade), cut into pieces — pressed into a divot in the frozen Creami tub before the add-in spin

**Steps:**

1. **Brown the butter:** Cut 50g butter into small cubes and melt in a pan over medium heat. Swirl constantly for 5–8 minutes until foam subsides, the butter turns transparent, and dark brown milk solids appear with a toasted hazelnut aroma. Remove from heat immediately. Reserve 10g of the browned butter in a small container for the ice cream base.
2. **Toast the pecans:** In a separate dry pan over high heat, toast 150g of pecans (broken in half) for a couple of minutes until fragrant. Remove from heat and add to the pan with the remaining browned butter. Stir to coat. Store at room temperature for up to 24 hours to let flavors develop.
3. **Make the ice cream base:** Combine milk, cream, dark brown sugar, dextrose, skim milk powder, and the reserved 10g browned butter in the pan used for pecans. Whisk together. Add egg yolks and whisk until fully incorporated. Stir in vanilla bean paste.
4. **Cook the custard:** Heat over medium, stirring constantly, until the base reaches 70–75°C. Do not exceed this temperature.
5. **Chill:** Cool the base and refrigerate for at least 12 hours, ideally 24 hours, to allow flavors from the brown butter, vanilla bean paste, and dark brown sugar/molasses to develop fully.
6. **Churn:** Process in a Musso (or other ice cream machine) until churned. Near the end of the churn, briefly warm the pecans if the butter has hardened (15 seconds in microwave), stir to re-coat, and add all pecans plus remaining browned butter solids into the churning machine. Reserve a few pecans for decoration.
7. **Freeze:** Dish out into containers and freeze.
8. **Ninja Creami variation:** Pour ~400g of the unchurned base into a standard Creami tub and freeze (24+ hours). Spin on Ice Cream. Make a divot in the center, press in cut pieces of pecan pie, cover back over, and run the add-in program.

**Notes:**

- The "unknown" butter pecan flavor comes from the combination of dark brown sugar (molasses notes), browned butter (Maillard/hazelnut notes), and egg yolks deepening the vanilla base — not butter extract.
- Browned butter (French: *beurre noisette*) must be watched closely — it goes from perfect to burnt quickly. Pull it the moment foam subsides and toasted spots appear.
- Break pecans in half rather than leaving whole; open cut ends absorb the butter better and the flavor penetrates deeper.
- No stabilizer used in this recipe; Nick notes a stabilizer would make it "100% perfect" by countering the egg whites and improving structure.
- The pecan pie Creami variation is outstanding — the Creami add-in spin shatters the frozen pie filling into chewy caramelized nuggets throughout the ice cream.
- Dark brown sugar gives the finished ice cream a light caramel color.
