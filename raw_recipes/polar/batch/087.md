### [Jack Sparrow Rum Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hmX9T1e5-hM)

A Pirates of the Caribbean-inspired boozy ice cream featuring three rums at different ABVs: Lambs vanilla rum in the base, Sparrow's rum in a wet caramel sauce, and Pusser's Gunpowder Proof in dark chocolate truffles.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **356g** heavy/double cream
- **451g** milk (2% or semi-skimmed)
- **84g** skimmed milk powder
- **80g** egg yolk (approx. 3 medium yolks)
- **180g** sugar
- **50g** Lambs Vanilla Rum (30% ABV / 60 proof)
- **2g** stabiliser blend

**Ingredients — Sparrow's Rum Caramel:**
- **225g** sugar
- **60g** water
- **71g** butter (room temperature)
- **125g** cream
- **80g** Sparrow's Rum (40% ABV / 80 proof)

**Ingredients — Gunpowder Rum Chocolate Truffles:**
- **100g** double cream
- **25g** salted butter, cubed
- **300g** milk chocolate, finely chopped
- **50ml** Pusser's Gunpowder Proof Rum (54.5% ABV / 109 proof)
- **2–3 tbsp** cocoa powder (for coating)

**Steps:**
1. **Ice cream base:** Combine milk, egg yolks, sugar, and skimmed milk powder in a saucepan. Heat to 70°C to cook the egg yolks. Turn off the heat, then stir in the Lambs Vanilla Rum. Transfer to a container and chill in the fridge.
2. **Truffles:** Gently warm the double cream in the microwave (or hob). Pour over the finely chopped milk chocolate and cubed butter; stir until fully melted. Stir in the Pusser's Gunpowder Rum. Pour into a lined 8x8 square tin and refrigerate until solid.
3. Once the truffle slab is chilled, scoop portions and roll between gloved hands into balls. Drop into cocoa powder and roll until fully coated. Store in the fridge until assembly.
4. **Caramel:** Combine sugar and water in a pan; dissolve the sugar and gently bring to heat. Cook to desired amber colour, then turn off the heat (it continues cooking). Add room-temperature butter and cream; mix until combined. Stir in the Sparrow's Rum (80g). Keep at room temperature until assembly.
5. **Churn:** Process the chilled base in an ice cream machine. Note: high alcohol content means it will take longer to churn — reducing the sugar compensates for this.
6. **Assembly:** Transfer churned ice cream to a tub, layering in caramel and halved truffles throughout. Seal and freeze for at least 6 hours.

**Notes:**
- Sugar in the base is intentionally reduced because the Lambs rum contributes sweetness and lowers the freezing point — this also prevents the base from being too soft after churning.
- Rum ABV has a significant impact: Lambs (30%) is mild in the base; Sparrow's (40%) intensifies the caramel; Pusser's Gunpowder (54.5%) makes the truffles fiercely potent.
- The ice cream alone is moderate in alcohol; the caramel and truffles make this extreme — serve them "on the side" for guests who want to control the intensity.
- Any rum can be substituted, but match the ABV carefully — swapping to a higher-proof spirit will keep the ice cream softer/less frozen.
- Three-part recipe: base + caramel + truffles are all made separately before final assembly.
