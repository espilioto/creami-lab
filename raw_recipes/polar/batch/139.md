### [PERFECT Ninja Creami Mango Sorbet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SDXxHKvncoo)
Simple 4-ingredient mango sorbet using fresh ripe mangos, designed to work in both the Ninja Creami and traditional ice cream machines.

**Ingredients:**
- **314g** ripe mango flesh
- **420g** cold water
- **210g** sugar (200g added to blender, 10g reserved for stabilizer)
- **3.5g** guar gum
- **52g** corn syrup (or glucose syrup / golden syrup / dextrose)

**Steps:**
1. Add mango flesh and cold water to a blender.
2. Add 200g of sugar to the blender.
3. In a small bowl, mix the reserved 10g of sugar with 3.5g of guar gum until fully combined (no clumps). This prevents the stabilizer from clumping when it hits the liquid.
4. Add the sugar/guar gum mixture to the blender.
5. Add 52g of corn syrup to the blender.
6. Blend on low speed for ~30 seconds until ultra-smooth.
7. Pour into Ninja Creami pint(s) and freeze for minimum 24 hours.
8. Spin on Sorbet program.

**Notes:**
- Corn syrup/glucose syrup lowers the serving temperature from -10°C to -16°C, making it scoopable straight from the freezer without waiting.
- Always pre-mix stabilizer with a dry powdered solid (sugar, cocoa, milk powder, etc.) before adding to liquid — prevents clumping and ensures full dispersion.
- The Ninja Creami's high shear produces a smoother, denser sorbet than a traditional churn machine; both taste identical but the Creami texture wins.
- Recipe yields more than one Creami pint — Nick kept the excess for another video / used in a traditional ice cream machine for comparison.
- Do not just blend plain frozen fruit and expect good results — the sugar balance is essential to prevent an icy block.
