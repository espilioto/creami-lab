### [Ice Cream Cookie Sandwiches (ASMR)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ytdo46ZSq1o)

Double chocolate and red velvet cookies sandwiching a boozy white chocolate ice cream, churned on a Musso 5030.

---

**Cookies (makes both double chocolate and red velvet — shared base, then divided):**

- **150g** butter, softened
- **200g** sugar
- **1** egg
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **200g** flour
- **1 tsp** baking soda

Double Chocolate half:
- **25g** cocoa powder
- **100g** milk chocolate chips

Red Velvet half:
- **8g** cocoa powder
- Red food colouring
- White chocolate chips (amount to taste)

**White Chocolate Ice Cream:**

- **435ml** milk
- **450ml** cream (40% fat)
- **70g** sugar
- **60g** dextrose
- **40g** golden syrup
- **35g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **50g** white chocolate
- **30ml** Mozart White Chocolate Liqueur

---

**Steps:**

**Cookies:**
1. Preheat oven to 175°C fan.
2. Cream butter and sugar until smooth. Beat in egg, then stir in vanilla.
3. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt; stir into creamed mixture until just blended.
4. Divide the dough in two. Add 25g cocoa + milk chocolate chips to one half (double chocolate). Add red food colouring + 8g cocoa + white chocolate chips to the other (red velvet).
5. Refrigerate both halves for 2–3 hours.
6. Portion into 2" rounds on a lined baking sheet.
7. Bake 12–15 minutes at 175°C fan. Cool completely (allow ~2 hours).

**Ice Cream:**
1. Warm the milk, sugars (sugar, dextrose, golden syrup), and SMP gently.
2. Add white chocolate and stir until melted. Remove from heat.
3. Add cream, then chill in fridge overnight (at least 4 hours).
4. Before churning, blend with an immersion blender if the white chocolate has semi-solidified.
5. Churn in machine (Musso 5030 shown). Freeze for ~2 hours until firm enough to scoop.

**Assembly:**
1. Scoop ice cream onto flat side of one cookie. Press second cookie on top.
2. Serve immediately or wrap and freeze.

---

**Notes:**

- Batch recipe — yields multiple sandwiches; no exact count given.
- White chocolate commonly semi-solidifies after overnight chilling — immersion blender or whisk before churning is essential.
- The Mozart White Chocolate Liqueur adds a noticeable boozy note; Nick describes flavour as "VERY nice white chocolate with alcohol bonus."
- Cookies are intentionally slightly gooey in the centre for the best sandwich texture.
- Machine used: Musso 5030 (Stella or Pola). This is a traditional batch ice cream churner, not a Ninja Creami.
