### [Coffee Coffee Buzz Buzz Buzz (Insanely Caffeinated Coffee Fudge Ice Cream)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s-vhOxo64yo)

Ninja Creami coffee ice cream inspired by the Ben & Jerry's flavor — ultra-caffeinated espresso base with a dense dark chocolate coffee fudge mix-in. Ninja Creami Deluxe 750ml batch.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **190g** whole milk
- **255g** cream
- **120g** espresso (or strong coffee — see notes)
- **130g** sugar
- **10g** corn syrup (or glucose / golden syrup)
- **45g** skim milk powder
- **1.5g** stabilizer (guar gum or similar — used to prevent icing; described as slightly more than usual due to coffee's tendency to ice)

**Ingredients — Coffee Fudge:**
- **400g** sweetened condensed milk (1 full can)
- **200g** dark chocolate (Lindt used in video)
- **20g** instant coffee powder (or 10g coffee extract)

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk and cream in a jug.
2. In a separate container, mix sugar, skim milk powder, and stabilizer together dry.
3. Brew 120g espresso (or make very strong instant coffee — 4–5 teaspoons in 120ml water).
4. Add the dry sugar mix into the milk/cream jug. Add the corn syrup. Pour in the hot espresso. Stir until sugars dissolve.
5. Pour the base into the Creami pint. Skim off any large air bubbles from the surface. Freeze overnight (minimum 24h).
6. **Make the fudge:** Gently heat the sweetened condensed milk in a pan until warm enough to melt the chocolate. Add the dark chocolate and instant coffee powder. Stir until a smooth, glossy, homogeneous fudge forms — do not fully dissolve all coffee granules (intentional texture).
7. Pour fudge into a lined baking pan/sheet. Cool to room temperature, then freeze until firm (overnight alongside the base).
8. Spin the frozen base on the Ice Cream setting.
9. While the base is spinning, remove the fudge from the freezer/fridge and chop into small cubes.
10. Add fudge cubes to the spun ice cream and run the Mix-In setting.

**Notes:**
- Batch size is for the Ninja Creami Deluxe 750ml pint — scale down for the standard 24oz pint.
- Espresso alternatives: pod machine on espresso setting, moka pot, or any strong brew method. Avoid weak coffee — the high water content from coffee can cause iciness, hence the slightly elevated stabilizer amount.
- Liquid coffee cannot be used in the fudge — only powdered/instant coffee works there. Leaving some granules undissolved is intentional for texture.
- The fudge is dense and sticky; the Mix-In setting handled it without needing to hand-fold.
- High caffeine content — consume responsibly.
