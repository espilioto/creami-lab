### [Rosemary Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3VmJ6zjqsRQ)
Plant-based rosemary-infused ice cream made with cashew milk, plant cream, and Carnation plant-based condensed milk.

**Ingredients:**
- **700g** cashew milk (store-bought)
- **500g** plant cream (~31% fat, e.g. Elmlea plant-based)
- **370g** plant-based condensed milk (Carnation plant-based)
- **100g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **1.5g** stabilizer (optional but recommended)
- Handful of fresh rosemary, leaves stripped from stalks and roughly bashed/chopped

**Steps:**
1. Strip rosemary leaves from stalks, discarding woody parts. Bash and roughly chop the leaves to release the oils.
2. Combine cashew milk, sugar, dextrose, and stabilizer in a pot. Heat while stirring to dissolve.
3. At around 50°C add the rosemary and steep on low heat for about 5 minutes. Stir to ensure stabilizer reaches 65–75°C, then remove from heat.
4. Add the plant cream to the mixture and whisk to combine.
5. Transfer to a container and refrigerate for 24 hours to steep and chill.
6. After 24 hours, strain out all rosemary pieces.
7. Pour the base into an ice cream machine and churn (approx. 20 minutes).
8. Transfer to pint tubs and freeze until fully set.

**Notes:**
- Nick notes the finished batch froze rock solid and went slightly icy — likely due to insufficient total sugar/PAC rather than too much. Consider increasing dextrose or adding more condensed milk in future versions.
- Also came out slightly gummy — attributed to stacked stabilizers: stabilizer in the plant cream, stabilizers in the plant-based condensed milk, plus the added 1.5g stabilizer. If skipping the added stabilizer, note that both the cream and condensed milk already contribute stabilizers.
- Strong rosemary aroma; the flavor is assertive. Nick describes it as an acquired taste — similar to the honey lavender he made previously.
- Different rosemary varieties may yield different intensity; use what is available.
- This is a churned (traditional ice cream machine) recipe, not a no-churn or Ninja Creami recipe.
