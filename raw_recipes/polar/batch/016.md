### [Amazing Snickers Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NHNbBdaC1Dc)
Deconstructed Snickers candy bar in ice cream form: nougat base, peanut caramel swirl, and milk chocolate stracciatella. Traditional churned recipe.

**Note: Batch recipe — uses a traditional ice cream churn, not a Ninja Creami.**

---

**Ingredients:**

*Nougat base:*
- **550g** milk (2% or whole)
- **550g** cream (33%; use 450g if using 50% double cream)
- **66g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **110g** nougat scraped from Snickers bars (approx. 8 x 52g bars)
- **220g** sugar
- **1.2g** stabilizer blend (optional)

*Milk chocolate stracciatella:*
- **100g** milk chocolate
- **7g** refined coconut oil (refined = no coconut flavour)

*Peanut caramel swirl:*
- **150g** salted peanuts (pulsed fine in food processor)
- **250g** sugar
- **100g** water
- **25g** butter
- **250g** cream (33%)
- **50g** invert sugar (glucose syrup, corn syrup, or golden syrup)

---

**Steps:**

1. Slice open Snickers bars with a knife and scrape out the nougat layer only — discard the chocolate coating. Collect 110g of nougat.
2. Combine milk, cream, SMP, sugar, and optional stabilizer in a saucepan. Whisk together, then add the nougat. Heat on the hob to 85°C (185°F), stirring until the nougat dissolves fully into the base.
3. Transfer base to a container and refrigerate overnight to age and develop nougat flavour.
4. For the caramel: combine sugar, water, and invert sugar in a saucepan. Cook over medium-high heat without stirring until the mixture turns a deep golden brown colour.
5. Remove caramel from heat and carefully add the butter (it will spit), then slowly pour in the cream, stirring continuously. If the mixture seizes, return briefly to heat until smooth.
6. Stir the crushed peanuts into the finished caramel. Pour into a container and leave to cool to room temperature.
7. Melt milk chocolate and refined coconut oil together in a microwave until fully liquid. Set aside.
8. Churn the aged base in your ice cream machine according to the manufacturer's instructions.
9. In the final minute of churning, stream in the melted chocolate to create a stracciatella effect.
10. Layer scoops of churned ice cream into a container, alternating with spoonfuls of peanut caramel swirl as you build up the layers.
11. Freeze for several hours until firm before serving.

---

**Notes:**
- Use refined (not cold-pressed) coconut oil for the stracciatella — refined has no coconut flavour.
- The invert sugar (glucose/corn/golden syrup) in the caramel is essential — it prevents the caramel from setting rock-hard in the freezer. More cream also keeps the swirl softer.
- Nougat from the Snickers bars acts as a natural stabilizer for the base; the additional 1.2g stabilizer blend is optional but recommended.
- Peanuts should be pulsed to a cornmeal-like texture — fine but not a paste — so they distribute evenly through the caramel.
- Age the base overnight in the fridge to maximise nougat flavour infusion.
- The caramel swirl is layered in during assembly, not churned in; this preserves distinct pockets of caramel.
