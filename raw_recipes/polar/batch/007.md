### [AMAZING White Chocolate Blondie Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V0EDfRAC6KA) loaded with homemade white chocolate blondies and a Werther's Soft caramel sauce swirl. Inspired by Gelato Messina and Scientifically Sweet.

> **Batch recipe** — 1.5L / ~1500g base. Yields 3x standard Ninja Creami pints, 2x Ninja Creami Deluxe pints, or 1x 1L churn + 1x standard pint.

---

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **680g** milk
- **440g** 33% whipping cream
- **100g** dextrose
- **55g** skim milk powder
- **110g** white chocolate (high quality — Nick uses mostly Valrhona + some white chocolate chips)
- **~11g** vanilla bean paste (~3/4 tablespoon)
- **1.8g** stabilizer (Nick's own blend)

**Ingredients — White Chocolate Blondies** (Scientifically Sweet recipe):
- **85g** white chocolate (high quality — Nick uses Valrhona)
- **100g** butter
- **135g** white sugar
- **55g** light brown sugar (Nick used demerara)
- **2** eggs, room temperature
- **2.5g** pink salt (~1/2 tsp)
- **7ml** vanilla bean paste (~1.5 tsp; Nick used ~3 tsp because he likes vanilla)
- **160g** all-purpose flour
- White chocolate chips (extra, folded in at end — amount to taste)

**Ingredients — Werther's Caramel Sauce:**
- **20** Werther's Soft caramels (unwrapped)
- Splash of cream (enough to loosen into a pourable sauce)

---

**Steps — Blondies (make first):**
1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Line an 8x8 pan with baking parchment.
2. Melt butter and white chocolate together. Microwave in 30-second blasts, stirring between each, until smooth. Be careful not to burn the white chocolate — stir it into the hot butter rather than microwaving hard.
3. In a large bowl, add white sugar, brown sugar, eggs, and salt. Mix by hand until pale and thick.
4. Add vanilla bean paste and mix through.
5. Pour in the melted butter and white chocolate mixture. Stir to combine.
6. Fold in the flour until just incorporated — do not whisk or overmix.
7. Fold in extra white chocolate chips.
8. Pour into the lined pan. Bake 25–30 minutes until golden around the edges with a smooth top.
9. Remove from oven and cool completely. Cut into pieces. Keep some for mix-in; vacuum-seal and freeze extras.

**Steps — Ice Cream Base:**
1. Combine milk powder, dextrose, and stabilizer in a pot. Whisk dry.
2. Add milk and cream. Add white chocolate (broken into pieces).
3. Heat on the hob, stirring, until the white chocolate melts fully and the base reaches 85°C (185°F). Remove from heat.
4. Add vanilla bean paste and stir through.
5. Note: white chocolate can cause cocoa butter to solidify on top as it cools — if this happens, re-incorporate with a whisk or immersion blender.
6. Chill in the fridge until cold (rapid chill first, then finish in fridge).
7. Churn in ice cream machine until thick and aerated.

**Steps — Caramel Sauce:**
1. Unwrap all 20 Werther's Soft caramels into a small jug.
2. Add a splash of cream.
3. Microwave in 30-second blasts (then shorter 20–25 second blasts) until melted. Stir together until smooth.
4. Refrigerate until ready to use (stays pourable for hours; does not set hard).

**Assembly:**
1. During the last few minutes of churning, add a few center-cut blondie pieces (softer pieces without hard edges) into the churning ice cream so they break up and distribute.
2. When loading into containers, drizzle in the Werther's caramel sauce between layers as a swirl.
3. Freeze until hardened.

---

**Notes:**
- Use high-quality white chocolate (Valrhona, Guittard, etc.) — it makes a significant difference to the final flavour.
- White chocolate ice cream is notoriously cold/flat when made with lower-quality chocolate. The stabilizer helps with this.
- Nick uses 1.8g stabilizer here because white chocolate benefits from slightly more stabilizer than other flavours.
- Heating the base to 85°C with skim milk powder mimics commercial pasteurisation and reduces water content, giving a more luxurious result.
- Vanilla bean paste is preferred over vanilla extract here to avoid alcohol in the base.
- The outer edges of the blondies stay crispy even when frozen in the ice cream; the centre pieces stay chewy and decadent.
- Werther's Soft caramel sauce is easy: cream + caramels + microwave. Stays perfectly pourable after hours in the fridge.
- This is a churned ice cream recipe, not a Ninja Creami no-churn recipe.
