### [Rum Raisin Ice Cream CLASSIC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3sngyfXi0I)

Classic churned rum raisin ice cream with rum-soaked raisins stirred in at the end of churn.

**Ingredients:**
- **550g** milk (1.8% fat)
- **400g** cream (50% fat)
- **130g** sugar
- **55g** dextrose
- **60g** skimmed milk powder
- **50ml** strong rum
- **2g** stabilizer (optional)
- Raisins (quantity to taste — Nick used Cape raisins from South Africa)

**Steps:**
1. Place raisins and rum in a pan, warm gently and simmer for 5–10 minutes to open the pores of the raisins and allow the rum to permeate. Transfer to a container and leave overnight.
2. Combine milk, cream, sugar, and stabilizer pre-mixed with dextrose in a pan. Heat slowly, bringing up to 60–70°C.
3. At temperature, whisk in the skimmed milk powder until fully incorporated.
4. Remove from heat, pour base into a container, allow to cool slightly, then refrigerate until completely chilled.
5. The following day, strain the raisins from any remaining rum. Stir that remaining rum into the cold ice cream base (do not add rum to a hot base — this preserves the alcohol).
6. Churn the base in an ice cream machine. At the end of the churn, add the soaked raisins (as many or as few as desired).
7. Transfer to tubs and freeze until hardened.

**Notes:**
- Add rum to the chilled base, not the hot base — adding it hot drives off the alcohol.
- The alcohol in the rum keeps the ice cream from freezing completely solid, so be mindful of total sugar when balancing the recipe.
- Raisins soaked in alcohol stay completely soft in the frozen ice cream, giving a burst of rum flavour with each bite.
- Optional: add a small amount of vanilla to the base to bring forward complementary flavours.
- Nick used Cape raisins (from South Africa) — large, juicy, and excellent texture contrast in the finished ice cream.
- This is a batch recipe yielding multiple tubs; scale ingredients proportionally for a single pint.
