### [Fig & Honey Ice Cream - Thor inspired](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYNRtZtj3cE)
A fun Marvel/Thor-themed video producing a two-swirl churned ice cream: blue fig and honey, decorated with rose-gold white chocolate shards. No exact measurements given — the narration is entirely in-character Asgardian roleplay.

**Ingredients:**

*Fig ice cream base:*
- Fresh figs (quantity unspecified — cooked down to a jam)
- Sugar (quantity unspecified)
- Duck egg yolk (1 per base — described as "golden duck egg yolk")
- Whole milk (quantity unspecified — "finest milk")
- Double cream (quantity unspecified — "finest cream")

*Honey ice cream base:*
- Honey (quantity unspecified — described as fructose-rich, so use ~half the quantity you'd use for regular sugar)
- Whole milk (quantity unspecified)
- Double cream (quantity unspecified)
- Blue food coloring (for the "pastel dragon honey" color effect — turns green once mixed into the base)

*Rose-gold white chocolate shards:*
- White chocolate (quantity unspecified)
- Red food coloring
- Gold lustre/shimmer powder (for brushed decoration)

**Steps:**

1. Cook fresh figs in a pot over medium heat, stirring until broken down into a jam. Simmer for ~30 minutes until thick.
2. Remove from heat and strain through a sieve into a mixing container to remove seeds/skins. Retain all the liquid fig jam.
3. Add sugar and duck egg yolk to the strained fig jam. Mix well — the yolk adds richness and a golden color.
4. Add milk and double cream. Stir to combine. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.
5. For the honey base: combine honey, milk, and double cream. Add blue food coloring. Stir to combine — note it will turn green once fully mixed. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.
6. For the chocolate shards: melt white chocolate over a bain-marie (bowl over warm water). Add red food coloring and stir in. Spread thinly over a silicone baking sheet or non-stick surface.
7. Brush melted chocolate with gold lustre powder before it sets. Allow to harden (or refrigerate to speed up). Break/cut into shards once set.
8. Churn the fig base first in an ice cream machine. Transfer to the serving container and keep in the freezer while churning the honey base.
9. Once the honey base is churned, layer/swirl both ice creams together in the container.
10. Press chocolate shards into the top of the ice cream and scatter a few on the surface for decoration. Freeze until firm before serving.

**Notes:**
- No quantities are given in this video — the entire narration is in-character as a Thor/Asgard-themed skit. Use your standard ice cream ratios and adjust to taste.
- Honey is fructose-heavy and very sweet. Nick advises using roughly half the amount you would normally use for sugar, then adjusting upward if needed. Don't substitute all sugar with honey.
- The "blue" color in the fig base comes from the natural blue/purple pigment inside ripe fresh figs — it bleeds through when cooked.
- The honey base turns green (not blue) once mixed with milk/cream due to the blue food coloring interacting with the yellow cream.
- Duck egg yolks are richer and larger than hen eggs — they add extra emulsification and a deeper golden color to the fig base.
- This is a traditional churned ice cream (not a Ninja Creami recipe). The video is part of a Marvel-themed series alongside "Stark Raving Hazelnuts" and "Hunka Hulka Burning Fudge."
- White chocolate shards are primarily decorative — press them into the tub before final freeze so they stay put.
