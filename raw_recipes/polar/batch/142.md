### [Rebel Keto Coffee Chip Ice Cream - SUGAR FREE and KETO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JjJizfl8L7A)

A high-fat, sugar-free keto coffee chip ice cream inspired by Rebel Ice Cream, churned to ~42% overrun for a dense, luxury texture across ~2.7 pints.

**Ingredients:**

Base:
- **400g** heavy cream (33% fat)
- **100g** water
- **130g** allulose
- **30g** inulin powder
- **20g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **90g** egg yolks (approx. 3–4 yolks)
- **15g** instant coffee powder
- **2g** tara gum (or 2g standard stabilizer mix)

Straciatella chocolate chip:
- **100g** dark chocolate (72% — mixed with some milk chocolate to soften bitterness; Nick used Balcolade milk chocolate from Bulk Barn alongside a 72% bar from Safeways)
- **10g** olive oil (deodorized coconut oil also works — use the flavourless kind)

**Steps:**

1. Combine cream, water, coffee powder, allulose, inulin, skim milk powder, and egg yolks in a saucepan. Stir to dissolve the dry ingredients and break up the yolks — no special tempering needed, egg yolks go in cold.
2. Add 2g tara gum (or stabilizer mix) and stir in. Heat on the hob, stirring continuously, until steaming — target 65–80°C. Hold at steaming point for a couple of minutes to fully activate the stabilizer.
3. Remove from heat, pour into a container, and refrigerate until thoroughly chilled (overnight or several hours).
4. Chill the ice cream machine bowl for ~10 minutes. Whisk the cold base to agitate, then churn. Monitor temperature — when the mix reaches around -10 to -11°C, prepare the chocolate drizzle.
5. Chop the dark and milk chocolate into rough pieces, place in a microwave-safe bowl with the olive oil. Melt in 10–15 second bursts in the microwave, stirring between each burst. Remove while a few lumps remain — residual heat finishes the melt. Stir until smooth and pourable.
6. When the churned ice cream reaches approximately -12°C (machine is nearly done), drizzle the melted chocolate in a thin stream into the running machine — the cold environment instantly sets it into thin shards (straciatella technique).
7. Continue churning until done. Pack into containers. Target overrun: ~42%. Total yield: ~2.7 pints / ~1280g churned weight.

**Notes:**

- Batch recipe: yields approximately 2.7 pints (~897g unchurned base + chocolate). Scale down to a single pint if needed.
- Total net carbs: ~18.6g per pint at 42% overrun. Unchurned base is ~50.3g net carbs across the full batch.
- Nick deliberately targets ~30–42% overrun (not the 95–100% overrun of commercial keto brands like Rebel) — the result is far denser and more luxurious, comparable to Häagen-Dazs texture.
- Tara gum (Peruvian carob gum) is the stabilizer used in Rebel's original formula; 2g of a normal stabilizer blend is offered as a substitute.
- For the chocolate, Nick mixes a 72% dark bar with an unknown-% milk chocolate (Balcolade from Bulk Barn) to soften the bitterness. Any blend of dark + milk chocolate works.
- Use deodorized (flavour-neutral) coconut oil if substituting olive oil — the aromatic kind will add coconut flavour to the chocolate chips.
- When melting chocolate in the microwave, pull it out while a few lumps remain and stir to finish — prevents overheating and seizing.
- Drizzle chocolate at -10 to -12°C machine temperature for best straciatella shards.
