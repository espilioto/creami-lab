### [Clive's Luxury Chocolate Hazelnut Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sqwAyAP4fZE)

Diabetic-friendly chocolate hazelnut gelato using allulose and stevia, churned in an ice cream machine with a 1 kg base yielding ~1.3 L at 30% overrun.

**Ingredients (allulose version — primary):**
- **540g** unsweetened cashew milk (any brand, check for no added sugar)
- **200g** 33% cream (30–36% fat)
- **160g** allulose
- **50g** inulin
- **75g** unsweetened cocoa powder (strong/dark — not drinking chocolate; Nick uses Extra Brute / Cacao Barry Brute)
- **1g** salt
- **5g** ice cream stabilizer (Nick's own heated blend; 4–5g range)
- **1g** liquid stevia (added after cooking, not heated)

**Add-ins (churned in at end):**
- **~50g** 100% chocolate (chopped fine; no added sugar — use 100% cacao bar, not 85%/90% which still has sugar)
- **~handful** hazelnuts, roasted and chopped (raw or pre-roasted; Nick toasts 10 min at ~120°C / 250°F and rubs off skins)

**Xylitol version (described as "not quite excellent but still good"):**
- **535g** cashew milk
- **210g** 33% cream
- **140g** xylitol
- **55g** inulin
- **75g** cocoa powder
- **1g** salt
- **5g** ice cream stabilizer
- **1g** liquid stevia

**Steps:**
1. Combine cashew milk, cream, allulose, inulin, cocoa powder, salt, and stabilizer in a saucepan.
2. Heat to 80–85°C, stirring continuously, and hold for 2–3 minutes to activate the stabilizer.
3. Pour into container. Let cool, then stir in liquid stevia (do not cook the stevia — heat increases bitterness).
4. Refrigerate overnight to develop flavors (ice bath + immediate churn is acceptable but overnight is preferred).
5. Toast hazelnuts in oven ~10 minutes at ~120°C (250°F); rub off skins, let cool, then chop roughly.
6. Chop 100% chocolate bar into fine pieces.
7. Churn cold base in ice cream machine until nearly done.
8. Add chopped chocolate and chopped toasted hazelnuts in the last moments of the churn.
9. Transfer to containers (~1.3 L yield from this 1 kg base, ~30% overrun). Freeze.

**Notes:**
- Allulose version is significantly better than xylitol — xylitol dose is capped lower, which reduces solids and hurts texture.
- Stevia must go in after cooking; heating stevia amplifies bitterness.
- Use 100% cacao chocolate for the add-in — even 85%–95% bars contain added sugar.
- If pre-roasted hazelnuts taste weak, re-toast them; it makes a notable difference.
- The amount of 100% chocolate add-in is approximate (~50g for the 1 kg base); keep it restrained — it is intensely bitter.
- Nick mentions citrus fiber as an upcoming substitute for inulin in plant-based recipes if it becomes available.
- This is a traditional churned gelato recipe (not a Ninja Creami recipe).
