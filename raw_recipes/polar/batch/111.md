### [Making Dondurma Turkish Stretchy Ice Cream Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O7xOlzCmi28)

Traditional Turkish dondurma made with authentic salep orchid root powder and mastic resin, churned then stand-mixer beaten for its signature stretchiness.

**Ingredients:**
- **600g** goat's milk
- **200g** whipping cream
- **150g** sugar
- **15g** salep orchid root powder (true orchid root — NOT pre-made salep drink mix)
- **3g** mastic resin (ground)

**Steps:**
1. Grind mastic resin with a small amount of sugar (helps prevent sticking) and mix together with all remaining dry ingredients.
2. Combine goat's milk, cream, sugar, ground mastic, and salep powder in a bowl. Whisk for 2 minutes until sugar is fully dissolved — run a spoon along the bottom and sides to check for undissolved sugar.
3. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour.
4. Churn in an ice cream machine until the mix thickens and the machine tone changes — stop slightly earlier than you would for a normal ice cream (it should hold its shape but not be fully frozen).
5. Transfer the partly-churned ice cream immediately into a pre-chilled stand mixer bowl (bowl and beater both pre-frozen). Beat for 2 minutes on the stand mixer.
6. Transfer to a container and freeze.
7. Before serving, remove from freezer and let temper for ~15 minutes until the ice cream just starts to become malleable (~-10°C). Work it by hand for about 5 minutes to develop stretchiness — this mimics the traditional pounding/stretching done with paddles by street vendors.

**Notes:**
- Salep must be true orchid root powder sourced from Turkey — not the pre-mixed salep drink sold in shops, which is a starch pudding base and will not produce stretchiness.
- Mastic resin is available in Mediterranean/Greek grocery stores. Grinding it with sugar prevents it from clumping and sticking.
- Stretchiness is activated at the right serving temperature (~-10°C); straight from the freezer it will be too firm.
- Flavor is mildly rooty with notes of cinnamon and a hint of vanilla from the salep. Additions like vanilla extract or pistachio are recommended.
- This is Part 1 (traditional ingredients). Part 2 covers a modernist version without salep or mastic.
- Equipment needed: hand whisk, stand mixer with pre-chilled metal bowl and beater, ice cream machine.
