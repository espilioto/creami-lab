### [Ferrero Raffaello Ice Cream with Raspberry White Chocolate Ganache Swirl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nl9XgEgL4x0).

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **90g** Raffaello chocolate bar (from a 190g bar)
- **387g** whole milk (split: ~200g for melting chocolate, ~187g added after)
- **140g** heavy cream / whipping cream (33%+)
- **97g** sugar
- **11g** corn syrup or glucose syrup
- **20g** skim milk powder
- **3g** coconut flavoring (glycerin-based; Nick uses Frontier Co-op)
- **1g** salt
- Guar gum (a pinch, mixed with sugar — amount not specified)

Raspberry White Chocolate Ganache (separate video — full recipe at https://youtu.be/VmFSDXQJE9s):
- **300g** white chocolate couverture
- **130g** 35% cream
- **40g** glucose syrup
- **40g** unsalted butter (soft, room temperature)
- **20–25g** dehydrated raspberry powder
- **15g** honey
- **1g** citric acid
- **0.5g** salt

**Steps:**

1. Measure ~200g of milk into a jug. Break up the Raffaello bar and add it to the milk.
2. Microwave in 30-second blasts (about 3 rounds / 90 seconds total) until the chocolate is soft enough to stir.
3. Stir until the chocolate is fully melted into the warm milk.
4. Add the remaining ~187g milk and the 140g cream. Stir to combine.
5. In a separate small bowl, mix the sugar with the guar gum, then stir that into the base.
6. Add the skim milk powder and stir until dissolved (residual heat is sufficient; will fully dissolve in about 30 minutes even cold).
7. Add the coconut flavoring (3g) and salt (1g). Stir to combine.
8. Add the corn syrup / glucose syrup and stir through.
9. Pour into a Ninja Creami Deluxe pint tub. Freeze overnight (minimum).
10. Spin on the Ice Cream setting.
11. Dig a tunnel down the center of the spun ice cream with a spoon.
12. Spoon or pipe the raspberry white chocolate ganache into the tunnel.
13. Swirl the ganache through the ice cream by hand using a spoon — do NOT use the Mix-In setting.

**Notes:**

- The coconut flavoring is a glycerin-based extract (not a water-based extract); Nick uses Frontier Co-op brand.
- The coconut extract boosts the coconut flavor already present in the Raffaello coating.
- The recipe makes approximately 750g / 750ml — sized for the Ninja Creami Deluxe. For the standard (non-Deluxe) Ninja Creami, remove one third of all ingredients.
- Do not use the Mix-In setting to incorporate the ganache — it will completely blend it into the base, eliminating the swirl. Hand-swirl only.
- If you don't want to make the full ganache, substitute: fresh raspberries boiled down with sugar, thickened with guar gum, xanthan, or tapioca starch (not corn starch — it makes the sauce semi-opaque). Pipe into the tunnel and hand-swirl.
- Skim milk powder dissolves at fridge temperature; it will fully dissolve in about 30 minutes even in cold liquid.
- Guar gum shelf life: Nick notes his was over 3 years old and still effective.
