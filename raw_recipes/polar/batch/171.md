### [Will it Ice Cream? — Cup of Coffee in an Ice Cream Maker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dX69YdFLevY)

"Will it Ice Cream?" novelty experiment: a regular cup of coffee (with cream) churned in a cheap ice cream machine — no recipe, no freezing, just a fun test.

**Ingredients:**

- **1 cup** brewed coffee (made with Nescafe Azera x Grind collaboration instant)
- **Splash** of cream (added to the coffee)

**Steps:**

1. Brew a cup of coffee using Nescafe Azera (Grind collaboration). Add a splash of cream.
2. Let the coffee cool down completely (about 1 hour).
3. Pour the cold coffee into the ice cream machine bowl and churn for ~30 minutes.
4. Check result.

**Notes:**

- The answer to "Will it Ice Cream?" is **no** — a plain cup of coffee with cream does not turn into ice cream by churning alone.
- Nick explicitly states there is no recipe here; this is a pure curiosity experiment.
- Machine used: cheapest ice cream machine found on Amazon (traditional churn-style, not a Ninja Creami).
- The base was not frozen beforehand — just cooled to room temperature, then churned directly.
- Cream was added specifically to try to get a creamier result, though Nick noted it's not his usual approach.
