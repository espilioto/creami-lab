### [No Added Sugar Salted Caramel Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vsiAqzxcliA)

Silky salted caramel gelato using caramelized allulose and stevia — no added sugar, diabetic-friendly, Ninja Creami Deluxe.

**Ingredients** (750g batch for Ninja Creami Deluxe):

- **632.6g** light cream (18% fat — UK: single cream)
- **80.1g** allulose (powdered)
- **32.3g** skim milk powder
- **3.0g** salt
- **2.0g** stevia drops

**Steps:**

1. Weigh 80g allulose into a small saucepan. Heat on medium-high, swirling the pan manually. The allulose will turn translucent at the edges, then bubble up intensely — bubbles subside in about 10 seconds. Watch closely: allulose caramelizes much faster than sucrose. Pull from heat when you reach the desired amber-brown color (a color swatch is shown on screen in the video). Do not burn — over-caramelization makes it bitter.
2. In a separate pan, combine the light cream and skim milk powder. Heat to around 60–70°C — it needs to be hot before the caramel is added.
3. Pour the hot caramelized allulose into the warm cream mixture. Scrape out every bit from the pan (any left behind throws off the recipe). Whisk or stir together until all allulose crystals are fully dissolved. Return to heat briefly if needed.
4. Add the salt (3g) and stevia drops (2g). Stir to combine.
5. Pour into the Ninja Creami pint container. Bubbles will form on top from agitation — let the mix settle a few minutes, then skim off excess bubbles so the fill level is accurate. Do not freeze with a thick bubble layer on top (it will freeze into an unpleasant layer and overfill the container).
6. Freeze overnight (minimum).
7. Spin on **Ice Cream** mode (Nick notes Ice Cream and Gelato settings perform identically on this machine).

**Notes:**

- Allulose caramelizes significantly faster than sucrose — stay at the pan and don't walk away once it starts to color.
- Powdered allulose caramelizes less evenly than granulated; both work but require close attention.
- Stevia drops serve purely as a sweetness booster (no effect on texture/freezing point). Omit if you prefer less sweet.
- Salt strongly affects freezing point — if increasing salt beyond 3g, reduce allulose to compensate and maintain freeze.
- Caramel flavor from allulose will be more subtle than sucrose caramel; taking it too dark results in bitterness rather than stronger caramel.
- Bubbles in the poured mix are from agitation and the hot liquid addition — they settle on their own within a few minutes.
