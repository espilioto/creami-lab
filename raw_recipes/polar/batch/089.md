### [Jaffa Cake Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j68G6ZitR2g)

Chocolate ice cream base with a chocolate orange fudge swirl, chopped Jaffa Cakes, and a milk chocolate ganache top.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream:
- **450g** milk
- **300g** cream (49% fat)
- **70g** sugar
- **35g** dextrose
- **40g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **20g** cocoa powder 21% fat (Callebaut)
- **30g** milk chocolate
- **2g** stabilizer

Chocolate Orange Fudge Swirl:
- **50g** sugar
- **50g** milk chocolate
- **15g** cocoa powder
- **50g** milk
- **15g** corn syrup
- **7g** coconut oil
- **5g** orange extract (Nielsen-Massey recommended)

Chocolate Ganache Top:
- **50g** milk chocolate
- **5g** coconut oil

Mix-In:
- Jaffa Cakes (several, chopped — quantity to taste)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, sugar, dextrose, and SMP in a pan. If using a stabilizer, mix it into the sugar first before adding to the pan.
2. Add cocoa powder (Callebaut 22–24% fat used here) and whisk well. The smaller-than-usual amount gives a milk chocolate profile rather than dark.
3. Heat until up to temperature, then turn off the heat and stir in the milk chocolate until fully melted and emulsified.
4. Transfer to a container, add the cream, stir to combine, and chill in the fridge.
5. Once chilled, churn in an ice cream machine.
6. While the base chills, make the fudge swirl: combine corn syrup, milk, and coconut oil in a pan. Add sugar. Warm until fully melted.
7. Add cocoa powder, mix well, and bring to a rolling boil for about 30 seconds.
8. Turn off the heat, add the milk chocolate, and stir until glossy and smooth.
9. Once the fudge has cooled slightly, stir in the orange extract. Cool to room temperature before refrigerating (it will stiffen slightly in the fridge but won't go hard).
10. Optional: chop the orange jelly pieces from actual Jaffa Cakes into small cubes and stir into the fudge just before assembly for extra texture.
11. Chop whole Jaffa Cakes into roughly 1cm pieces.
12. Layer churned ice cream and fudge swirl alternately into the tub, then top with chopped Jaffa Cake pieces. Put the lid on and freeze for 20–30 minutes.
13. While the tub sets, melt ganache: combine milk chocolate and coconut oil and melt in the microwave. Let cool slightly.
14. Remove tub from freezer, pour the milk chocolate ganache over the top (over the Jaffa Cake pieces), replace the lid, and freeze for several hours until firm.

**Notes:**

- Nielsen-Massey orange extract is specifically recommended — the fudge tastes identical to actual Jaffa Cake flavor when using it.
- The ganache top sets hard over the Jaffa Cake layer, creating a theatrical chocolate shell that cracks when scooped — inspired by Ben & Jerry's style chocolate tops.
- The small amount of cocoa powder is intentional: it produces a milk chocolate flavor in the base, not dark chocolate.
- If adding orange jelly pieces from real Jaffa Cakes into the fudge, do it just before loading the tub for added bite and texture in the swirl.
- This is a churned recipe (conventional ice cream machine), not a Ninja Creami recipe.
