### [Making Ben & Jerry's Thick Mint Ice Cream at Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6XgSZ0yY1SM)
Ben & Jerry's Topped "Thick Mint" dupe — mint ice cream with cookie swirl, mint chocolate cookie balls, mini cookies on top, and chocolate ganache lid.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **350ml** milk (1.8% fat)
- **300ml** cream (49% fat)
- **90g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **30g** golden syrup / corn syrup
- **35g** skimmed milk powder
- **2 tsp** mint extract (good quality)

Cookies (adjusted recipe — BBC Good Food base):
- **150g** butter, softened
- **200g** sugar
- **1** egg
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **200g** flour
- **50g** dutched cocoa powder
- **1 tsp** baking soda

Ganache Top:
- **50g** dark chocolate (50%)
- **1 tbsp** water

**Steps:**

1. **Cookies:** Cream butter and sugar, beat in egg and vanilla, mix in flour, cocoa powder, and baking soda. Bake at 175°C for 10–12 minutes (adjust to your oven). Cool on a wire rack.
2. **Mint cookie balls:** While a couple of cookies are still slightly warm, break them into a small bowl and mix with a little mint extract and a splash of heavy cream. Take approximately quarter-teaspoon portions, semi-shape on a plate, and freeze. Once frozen, roll smooth and return to freezer until needed.
3. **Mini cookies for top:** Use the smallest cookie cutter you have (~10–12mm diameter). Press down gently into cooled cookies to cut out mini rounds. Store in a small container in the freezer.
4. **Cookie swirl:** Dry 1–2 cookies in the fridge overnight. Break them into a fine cookie dust/powder to layer into the tub.
5. **Ice cream base:** Add all base ingredients except cream and mint extract to a pan. Gently heat to dissolve the sugars. Remove from heat, stir in cream, then mint extract. Chill in fridge for a couple of hours.
6. **Churn** the chilled base as normal in an ice cream machine.
7. **Layer up:** When churn is complete, load the tub — layer in cookie swirl dust and mint cookie balls. Do not overfill. Freeze for about an hour until the top is just starting to firm.
8. **Top:** Place mini cookies on the surface, then pour over the chocolate ganache. Return to freezer for a few more hours until fully set.
9. **Ganache:** Melt 50g of 50% dark chocolate with 1 tbsp water in the microwave, checking every 10 seconds. If it splits, recover using the warm water technique. Nick recommends sticking to 50% chocolate — higher % (e.g. 70%) is more likely to split as a water ganache.

**Notes:**
- This is a **batch recipe** — uses a traditional ice cream churn, not a Ninja Creami.
- The original cookie recipe (from a generic blog) went wrong; Nick switched to a BBC Good Food-based recipe, which worked well.
- Cookie swirl is just dry cookie powder layered into the churned ice cream, mimicking the real B&J method.
- Keep mint cookie balls in the freezer until the very last minute before assembly — they soften quickly.
- The 50% dark chocolate ganache works well as a water ganache; 70% is liable to split (overpowers the cocoa butter ratio).
- If ganache splits, recover by adding a little warm water gradually while stirring.
- Nick notes the mint balls are the star of the recipe — strong mint flavour; skip entirely if you dislike mint.
