### [Tempting Treat: Chocolate Ice Cream with Valrhona Cocoa Powder](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0C_vgeKGd1A)

Episode 4 of the Chocolate Series — a pure Valrhona cocoa powder base with no added chocolate, churned in a traditional ice cream machine.

**Ingredients:**
- **583g** milk (1.8% fat)
- **344g** double cream (48% fat)
- **80g** sugar (sucrose)
- **80g** dextrose
- **57g** Valrhona cocoa powder
- **60g** SMP (skimmed milk powder)
- **2g** commercial stabilizer mix with emulsifiers (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, SMP, sugars, and stabilizer in a pan. Warm over heat, dissolving the sugars fully.
2. Add Valrhona cocoa powder and let it bloom in the warm base for 5–6 minutes, whisking to disperse.
3. Remove from heat. Transfer to a container, stir in the cream, then refrigerate until fully chilled (at least 4 hours).
4. Give the chilled base a thorough mix to ensure it is well incorporated.
5. Churn in an ice cream machine until done.
6. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze until firm.

**Notes:**
- The stabilizer mix contains emulsifiers to help disperse cocoa fat. Egg yolks can substitute if no commercial stabilizer is available.
- Valrhona is a Dutch-processed cocoa (contains potassium carbonate as acidity regulator) — produces a very dark, intensely flavoured, fudgy result that Nick rates above cheaper cocoa powders.
- Optional add-in variation shown: hard fudge pieces + liquid chocolate fudge swirl folded in after churning ("chocolate double fudge").
- Valrhona 1 kg bags sourced from a specialist supplier at roughly £6/bag at time of filming.
- Part of a broader Chocolate Series comparing cocoa powders and chocolate additions — see linked EP1 for the comparison episode.
