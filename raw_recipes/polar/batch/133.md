### [Must-Try Ninja Creami Lemon Gelato Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3aWgxIEZTMA)
Simple 2-ingredient gelato base (half & half + condensed milk) with alcohol-based extract for flavour and freezing-point control.

**Ingredients:**
- **550g** half & half (10% fat — Dairyland brand used)
- **350g** sweetened condensed milk (approx. one 300ml can)
- **10g** alcohol-based lemon extract (or hazelnut, vanilla, mint, coffee — must be alcohol-based)
- **30g** glucose syrup / invert sugar / corn syrup *(optional — improves texture and smoothness)*

**Mix-in (lemon version):**
- White chocolate (Valrhona Ivory), chopped small — added via Mix-In program

**Steps:**
1. Combine half & half and condensed milk in the pint container. No heating required.
2. Add 10g lemon extract (alcohol-based). Whisk briefly to distribute.
3. If using glucose/invert sugar, add 30g and stir in.
4. Freeze for at least 24 hours (video uses overnight).
5. Spin on Gelato program straight from freezer.
6. Add chopped white chocolate on top and run Mix-In program.
7. Serve immediately or store — the alcohol in the extract keeps it from going rock solid on refreezing.

**Notes:**
- The recipe makes two pints if dividing into two flavours (lemon + hazelnut) — base fills two Creami containers.
- Alcohol-based extract is essential for freezing-point depression; glycerin-based extracts also work but require ~50% more by weight.
- Without glucose/invert sugar: serving temp ~-10°C, fat ~9.4%, sugar ~16.7%, solids ~43.6%, water ~65%, POD 170.
- With glucose/invert sugar: serving temp ~-12°C, fat ~8.5%, sugar ~18.5%, solids ~35.9%, water ~63.8%, POD 196.
- Condensed milk already provides very high sugar — no additional sweetener needed.
- Lemon zest can substitute for lemon extract but won't contribute alcohol for FPD (freezing-point depression).
- Result is low-overrun, dense, rich gelato-style ice cream.
