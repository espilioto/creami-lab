### [Ben & Jerry's Rain-Dough Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-b6eL6aNaU)

A churned vanilla ice cream base inspired by Ben & Jerry's Rain-Dough Cookie Dough Twist, with a sprinkle-loaded no-bake cookie dough core and multi-coloured Deco Melt chocolate drops. Batch recipe (not single-pint).

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **500ml** double (heavy) cream
- **250ml** whole milk
- **200g** granulated sugar
- **1–2 tsp** vanilla bean paste
- **2 tbsp** non-fat powdered milk / skimmed milk powder

Cookie Dough Core:
- **136g** all-purpose flour, heat-treated (microwave or oven at 180°C) to kill bacteria
- **113g** unsalted butter, softened
- **136g** granulated sugar
- **1/4 tsp** salt
- **1.5 tbsp** milk, plus more as needed
- **1/2 tsp** vanilla extract
- **36g** rainbow sprinkles

Coloured Chocolate Drops:
- Deco Melts (baking candy melts), assorted colours — make plenty, more than you think you need

**Steps:**

Ice Cream Base:
1. Combine cream, milk, and sugar in a pan. Heat gently until sugar dissolves.
2. Add vanilla bean paste before the mix gets too hot.
3. Remove from heat and refrigerate for 3–4 hours until fully chilled.
4. Before churning, stir in the skimmed milk powder (2 tbsp).
5. Return to fridge for 20 minutes, then give a final stir.
6. Pre-cool your ice cream machine for 10 minutes (optional but recommended).
7. Churn for approximately 20 minutes until done.

Cookie Dough Core:
1. Heat-treat the flour in a microwave or oven (180°C for ~10 min) and allow to cool completely before use.
2. Beat softened butter and sugar together until well combined. Butter must be genuinely soft — hard butter will spray sugar everywhere.
3. Add salt, vanilla extract, and milk. Mix until combined.
4. Fold in the heat-treated flour and rainbow sprinkles.
5. Load the dough into a piping tube or roll into a cylinder shape wrapped in cling film.
6. Refrigerate until firm.

Coloured Chocolate Drops:
1. Pre-chill a flat baking pan in the freezer, lined or unlined.
2. Melt Deco Melts in the microwave until loose and fluid.
3. Dot or pipe the melted chocolate onto the frozen pan — a piping bag is much easier than a spoon.
4. Return the pan to the freezer for 5–10 minutes until fully set.
5. Scrape off the hardened drops and set aside in a container.

Assembly:
1. Fill the tub with churned ice cream, scattering in the coloured chocolate drops as you layer it up.
2. Press the chilled cookie dough cylinder straight down through the centre of the tub.
3. Top up any gaps with remaining ice cream.
4. Freeze for at least 7 hours (or overnight) until fully hardened.
5. Optional: line the tub with cling film before filling if you want to unmould for a cross-section reveal.

**Notes:**
- This is Nick's first attempt at the core and chocolate-drop technique — he calls it the "good, bad and ugly" version, so expect some trial and error.
- Use butter that is genuinely at room temperature for the cookie dough; cold butter will not incorporate well.
- Make more Deco Melt drops than you think you need — Nick made too few and regretted it.
- Deco Melts (baking candy melts) are the correct product to use; the B&J original labels them "chocolate flavour" not "chocolate", hence the substitute.
- The cookie dough core can also be formed without a tube: roll into a log, wrap in cling film, freeze solid, then press into the tub.
- Cookie dough recipe sourced from cookingclassy.com (no almond extract used).
