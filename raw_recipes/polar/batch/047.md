### [Create Ben & Jerry's Tiramisu Ice Cream at Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KaosgB5kA70)

B&J "Tiramisu Topped" recreation — mascarpone ice cream layered with fudge swirl, shortbread pieces, espresso fudge, and a chocolate ganache top. Batch recipe (uses a traditional ice cream machine, not Ninja Creami).

---

#### Ice Cream Base

**Ingredients:**
- **500ml** whole milk *(note: description says 400ml, VTT says 500ml — VTT used here)*
- **120g** sugar (pre-mixed with stabilizer)
- **400g** mascarpone cheese (split: 200g hot, 200g cold)
- **40g** corn syrup / golden syrup
- **40g** dextrose
- **15g** skimmed milk powder
- **10g** butter
- **1.1g** stabilizer blend
- **0.5 tsp** silk gel (or another emulsifier)
- **1/4 tsp** vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

**Steps:**
1. Add milk to a pan. Add sugar (with stabilizer already mixed in), dextrose, and corn syrup.
2. Start heating. Add skimmed milk powder, butter, and silk gel emulsifier.
3. Add 200g of mascarpone to the warm pan and stir in well.
4. Turn off the heat, then stir in the remaining 200g of cold mascarpone.
5. Transfer to a container and chill in the fridge overnight.
6. Once chilled, churn in your ice cream machine until done.

---

#### Fudge Swirl

**Ingredients:**
- **110g** sugar
- **25g** cocoa powder
- **57g** butter
- **65ml** milk
- **1.5 tbsp** corn syrup / golden syrup
- **0.5 tbsp** vanilla extract

**Steps:**
1. Add all ingredients to a pan and heat while stirring.
2. Bring to a rolling boil and let it bubble for at least 1 minute.
3. Transfer to a container and store in the fridge.

---

#### Espresso Fudge

**Ingredients:**
- **340g** 50% chocolate
- **400g** sweetened condensed milk (1 can)
- **2 tbsp** espresso powder

**Steps:**
1. Add condensed milk and chocolate to a pan. Heat until chocolate is fully melted and combined.
2. Stir in espresso powder.
3. Press into a lined 8x8 baking pan. Cool to room temperature then freeze overnight.
4. When needed, chop into small pieces and keep frozen until assembly.

---

#### Ganache Top

**Ingredients:**
- **50g** 50% chocolate
- **1 tsp** deodorized coconut oil

**Steps:**
1. Melt chocolate with coconut oil together until smooth.

---

#### Shortbread

- Shop-bought shortbread used in the video. Nick links BBC Good Food recipe (https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/shortbread-biscuits) as a homemade alternative.

---

#### Assembly

1. When ice cream is freshly churned, layer shortbread pieces and fudge swirl into the container alternating with ice cream.
2. Place chopped espresso fudge pieces on top of the ice cream.
3. Pour over the chocolate ganache.
4. Freeze for at least 4 hours before serving.

---

**Notes:**
- Adding the second half of the mascarpone cold (off the heat) helps keep the texture light and creamy.
- Silk gel is an emulsifier — helps blend the butter and fat properly. Any emulsifier works as a substitute.
- Golden syrup is a direct substitute for corn syrup (UK / no corn syrup available).
- The stabilizer is pre-mixed into the sugar before adding to the pan.
- The espresso fudge makes a large batch (8x8 pan) — only a portion goes into one tub.
- Nick notes the shortbread crunch combined with the fudge swirl is a standout element.
