### [How to make perfect Ben & Jerry's Karamel Sutra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDFSO3ZTOUQ)

Ben & Jerry's Karamel Sutra dupe — two churned ice cream halves (chocolate and caramel) with a sticky caramel core piped down the centre. Batch recipe (multiple tubs).

**Ingredients — Chocolate Base:**
- **550g** milk @ 2% fat
- **300g** cream @ 50% fat
- **160g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose or glucose
- **60g** skimmed milk powder
- **75g** cocoa powder @ 10–12% fat (Nick used a lower-fat cocoa from Design)
- **2g** stabilizer (optional)

**Ingredients — Caramel Base:**
- **500g** milk @ 2% fat
- **400g** cream @ 50% fat
- **140g** sugar
- **30g** dextrose
- **75g** Sosa Dulce de Leche Powder (or 50g canned dulce de leche as substitute)
- **2g** stabilizer

**Ingredients — Caramel Core:**
- **250g** sugar
- **100g** water
- **25g** butter
- **250g** cream (heavy or double)
- **50g** glucose (liquid — important for freeze stability)

**Steps:**

1. **Caramel base:** Add milk to pan. Mix sugar, stabilizer, and Sosa Dulce de Leche Powder together and whisk into the milk. Heat until fully dissolved. Remove from heat, pour into container, add cream, stir to combine. Chill in fridge 4–5 hours.
2. **Chocolate base:** Add milk to pan. Pre-mix sugar, stabilizer, skimmed milk powder, and cocoa powder together dry, then whisk into the milk — combining dry ingredients first helps the cocoa dissolve. Heat until completely dissolved. Remove from heat, pour into container, add cream, stir to combine. Chill in fridge 4–5 hours.
3. **Caramel core (wet caramel):** Add sugar and water to pan and bring to a deep amber colour — watch colour, not a thermometer. Turn off heat. Add butter carefully (will spit). Once butter is fully melted in, add liquid glucose and stir until melted. Finally add cream and stir until fully emulsified. Transfer to container and chill to fridge temperature.
4. **Churn chocolate base** first. When nearly done, take a few spoonfuls and put into one half of each tub (tub held on its side). Cover with cling film/plastic wrap, lid on, freeze on its side for ~45 minutes.
5. **Churn caramel base** while chocolate half is setting in freezer. Once churned, take tubs out of freezer and fill the other half of each tub with caramel ice cream. Tap down to fill voids, cover with cling film, lid on, return to freezer upright.
6. **Core and fill:** After a few hours (monitor — don't let it go rock solid), use a coring tool to bore a tunnel down the centre of each tub. Pipe or pour the chilled caramel core into the tunnel. Seal and return to freezer until fully set.

**Notes:**
- Sosa Dulce de Leche Powder is Nick's preferred caramel flavour ingredient — canned dulce de leche (50g) is the accessible substitute.
- Glucose in the caramel core is important — it lowers the freezing point so the core stays sticky and scoopable rather than setting hard.
- Nick accidentally let the ice cream freeze overnight before coring, making the step much harder; do the coring within a few hours while the ice cream is still workable.
- Semi-sweet 50–56% chocolate (chopped) is added as a mix-in to the chocolate half just before it finishes churning — amounts to taste.
- This is a batch recipe: the ingredient quantities produce multiple tubs. Scale down proportionally for a single tub.
