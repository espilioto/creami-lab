### [Mango Ice Cream: Version 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsyaO9-If6s)
Creamy mango ice cream made with canned mango flesh — great texture, subtle colour and flavour (Version 2 to follow with lower fat for stronger mango character).

**Ingredients:**
- **300g** canned mango flesh (from a 398ml can — use entire contents)
- **350g** milk @ 2%
- **300g** cream @ 33%
- **97g** sugar (sucrose)
- **55g** dextrose (51g added with dry ingredients + 4g pre-mixed with xanthan gum)
- **95g** skim milk powder
- **1.5g** xanthan gum

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk and cream in a saucepan. Add sugar, dextrose (51g), and skim milk powder.
2. Warm gently — no more than ~50°C — whisking until all sugars and milk powder are fully dissolved. Remove from heat.
3. Pour the dairy/sugar mixture into a blender. Add the entire contents of the canned mango (flesh + juice).
4. Blend for 5–10 seconds to start incorporating.
5. Mix the xanthan gum with the remaining 4g of dextrose, then add to the blender. Replace lid.
6. Blend for 2 full minutes until the xanthan gum is completely incorporated and the base is smooth.
7. Strain through a sieve into a clean bowl to remove any lumps.
8. Chill in the fridge until cold (base was noted as being straight out of the fridge before straining).
9. Pour into pint container and freeze for at least 24 hours.
10. Process in ice cream machine on the Ice Cream program.

**Notes:**
- Nick notes the result has a lovely smooth, creamy texture and scoops well, but the mango colour is a subtle yellow rather than bold orange — no artificial colouring used.
- Mango flavour is present but considered a little subtle; Version 2 will use less fat to intensify colour and flavour.
- Pre-mixing xanthan gum with a small amount of dextrose before adding to the blender prevents clumping and helps dispersion.
- Canned mango is used here as fresh mango has a short season; the 398ml can yielded exactly 300g of flesh.
- Nick describes this as technically closer to a Gelato due to the recipe ratios.
