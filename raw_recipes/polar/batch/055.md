### [Delicious Rice Gelato — Gelato di Riso](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9naNqC0I6g0) — enough to submerge the rice for cooking (approx. 200–250ml)
- Whole milk — for the base (quantity not precisely stated, approx. 400–500ml)
- **180g** sugar
- **60g** glucose syrup (40 DE)
- **5g** stabilizer blend (proprietary; Nick notes: if using guar or xanthan gum, reduce to 2.5–3g)
- Skim milk powder (optional — increases smoothness and creaminess; quantity not stated, small amount)
- **15g** vanilla bean paste (or high-quality vanilla extract)

**Steps:**

1. Wash the Arborio rice thoroughly in a fine mesh strainer to remove surface starch.
2. Combine the washed rice with the cream in a pan. Stir to ensure no rice sticks to the bottom. Simmer on a gentle heat for approximately 20–30 minutes, stirring periodically, until the rice is fully cooked and soft (not al dente). Turn off the heat and leave to rest for 10 minutes.
3. In a separate pan, warm the milk. Whisk in the skim milk powder (if using).
4. In a small bowl, combine the sugar and stabilizer blend. Whisk into the milk mixture.
5. Add the glucose syrup to the milk pan.
6. Heat the milk mixture to 85°C, stirring throughout.
7. Once the milk base reaches temperature, add the cooked rice and cream mixture. Stir well to combine.
8. Leave to settle off the heat for 15 minutes.
9. Transfer the entire mixture (including all rice) to a bowl. Chill down quickly in an ice bath or refrigerate overnight until completely cold.
10. Once chilled, stir in the vanilla bean paste.
11. Churn in an ice cream machine until done.
12. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for several hours until hardened.

**Notes:**

- This is a traditional batch recipe for a conventional ice cream machine — not a Ninja Creami recipe.
- Arborio rice (Italian short-grain) is essential; long-grain rice behaves very differently.
- The rice must be fully cooked through — al dente rice will be too hard once frozen.
- As the rice simmers in cream, water evaporates and the cream reduces, concentrating fat — this is intentional.
- Glucose syrup (40 DE) can be substituted with plain corn syrup (not identical but close), or replaced weight-for-weight with sugar as a last resort.
- Vanilla bean paste is preferred over extract for the subtle flavor; use a good quality one.
- Nick deliberately omits eggs, using stabilizers instead for the same structural effect.
- Nick's stabilizer blend is proprietary — if using individual gums (guar, xanthan), halve the quantity to ~2.5g to avoid over-stabilizing.
- The finished gelato has whole soft rice pieces throughout and a clean, subtle vanilla flavor — described as "a Frankenstein between ice cream and rice pudding."
