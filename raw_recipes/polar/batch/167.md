### [Warning: Addictive Australian Milo Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j-8ZS-sbufk)
Cooked malt-chocolate base using Milo powder — rich, creamy, and deeply flavoured through extended heating to concentrate and evaporate excess moisture.

**Ingredients:**
- **450g** milk (2% fat)
- **550g** whipping cream (33% fat)
- **170g** sugar
- **90g** Milo powder
- **30g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **2g** stabiliser

**Steps:**
1. Combine sugar and stabiliser in one container; mix together. Combine Milo and SMP in a second container; mix together.
2. Add milk and cream to a pan and begin warming.
3. Add the sugar/stabiliser mix to the dairy, then add the Milo/SMP mix. Whisk everything together.
4. Heat to 75–80°C and hold at that temperature for 20–30 minutes, stirring. This evaporates excess water (Milo runs thin) and fully hydrates the stabiliser. The extended hold also produces a slightly creamier result.
5. Transfer base to freezer containers. A skin will form on top as it chills — this mixes back in.
6. Chill for at least 4 hours until cold throughout, then freeze overnight (24h minimum).
7. Churn in ice cream machine. Note: the high sugar content slows churning — allow extra time.

**Notes:**
- Milo is a malted drink powder (similar to Horlicks/Ovaltine) originating in Australia, widely available across Asia and North America. Its flavour is mild — 90g is needed to achieve a pronounced taste in ice cream.
- Milo is lower in milk powder than expected, hence the added SMP to boost MSNF and body.
- The 20–30 min hold at 80°C is key: it evaporates excess moisture and fully hydrates locust bean gum (if used in the stabiliser blend), which requires ~30 min at 80–85°C to hydrate completely.
- High sugar content (170g) means the base will take longer to churn than usual.
- Optional add-in shown in video: graham cracker/biscuit crumbs layered into the tub before freezing (leftover from a strawberry cheesecake batch).
- Suggested add-in variation: brownie chunks + caramel sauce (inspired by Gelato Messina's commercial Milo flavour).
