### [AMAZING Haagen Dazs Chocolate Hazelnut ice cream but BETTER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLtsd8IvPas).

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **700g** milk (2%)
- **327g** cream (33%)
- **257g** sugar
- **65g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **45g** cocoa powder (20–24% fat; Nick uses 7 Hills — milk chocolate profile)
- **35g** dark chocolate (70%; 75% also fine)
- **60g** egg yolks (3 large)
- **5g** vanilla extract (add after cooling, just before churning)

**Ingredients — Hazelnut Ganache Swirl:**
- **150g** milk chocolate (40–45%)
- **15g** cocoa powder
- **60g** whole milk (any kind)
- **20g** refined coconut oil (refined = no coconut flavour)
- **10g** glucose syrup or honey (optional)
- **7g** hazelnut extract

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cream, sugar, SMP, cocoa powder, and chopped dark chocolate in a saucepan.
2. Heat on the hob, stirring, until all ingredients are dissolved and combined.
3. Add egg yolks and continue heating to 75°C (safe range: up to 85°C max — above 90°C they scramble). Stir constantly. This cooks the yolks and activates the lecithin for emulsification.
4. Remove from heat. Transfer to a container and refrigerate to chill completely.
5. While the base chills, pre-chill your ice cream machine.
6. Make the ganache: combine milk chocolate, cocoa powder, coconut oil, milk, and glucose syrup/honey in a small saucepan. Heat on the hob, stirring, until fully melted and smooth — it will look dry at first.
7. Once the ganache is hot and combined, stir in the hazelnut extract. Pour into a container and cool in the fridge for 5–10 minutes until the ice cream base is churned.
8. Remove the chilled base from the fridge, stir well, and add vanilla extract. Churn in your ice cream machine.
9. Layer churned ice cream and ganache into your container. Give one gentle mix to distribute — do not over-stir; you want large chunks of ganache throughout.
10. Freeze until solid.

**Notes:**
- This is a 1.5L batch. For a Ninja Creami Deluxe (24oz), use the full batch. For a standard Ninja Creami (16oz), divide by three.
- Stabilizer is optional here because egg yolk lecithin acts as the emulsifier/stabilizer. If adding one, use 1.2–1.5g per kilo of mix and choose a type with a lower activation temperature. Nick used locust bean gum.
- Nick uses 7 Hills cocoa powder (milk chocolate profile, 20–24% fat). If using a stronger dark-profile cocoa, reduce the quantity.
- Refined coconut oil is essential — unrefined will add coconut flavour.
- The ganache will look very dry and thick at first on the heat; keep stirring and it comes together once the milk and chocolate melt together.
