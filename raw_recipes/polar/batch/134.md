### [Ninja Creami - How To Make Mint Ice Cream At Home!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8jKOYuN8LSA)

Condensed milk mint choc chip ice cream using fresh peppermint leaves steeped in the dairy base — makes two pints (one plain, one green-colored).

**Ingredients:**

- **300g** 2% milk
- **300g** 33% whipping cream
- **300g** condensed milk (standard supermarket can)
- Fresh peppermint leaves (handful, stalks removed)
- Semi-sweet chocolate chips (high quality, to taste — added as mix-in)
- Green food coloring (optional, for one pint)

**Steps:**

1. Add milk and cream to a saucepan. Strip peppermint leaves from stalks and add to the dairy.
2. Warm to ~50°C to steep — the heat encourages the essential oils to leach out. Leave for at least 1 hour (2 hours for stronger flavor); heat can be off after initial warming.
3. Add the condensed milk and stir to combine.
4. Strain out the mint leaves. Divide base between two Ninja Creami pints. Add green food coloring to one if desired.
5. Freeze overnight (minimum).
6. Spin on Ice Cream mode.
7. After spinning, make a divot down the center of the pint and add chocolate chips. Run Mix-In function.
8. Serve immediately — texture softens quickly.

**Notes:**

- Real peppermint leaves give a distinctly different flavor vs. mint extract — stronger and more natural.
- Do not chop the leaves into the base; remove them entirely before freezing.
- Condensed milk provides both sweetness and MSNF (milk solids-non-fat), so no additional sugar is needed.
- The high sugar/fat content means this re-freezes well and can be eaten straight from the tub without respinning — a deliberate design choice from Nick.
- Batch: recipe yields 2 pints (base split equally).
- The green-colored pint was reserved for a separate Starbucks peppermint mocha frappuccino recipe.
