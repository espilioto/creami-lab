### [Perfect Sugar-Free Hibiscus Sorbet in the Ninja Creami](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mjseQKvbe4)

A simple 4-ingredient sugar-free hibiscus sorbet steeped for 1 hour, strained, and spun in the Ninja Creami.

**Batch:** 1.5L — fills 2x Ninja Creami Deluxe pints, 3x standard Ninja Creami pints, or 1x 1.5L ice cream machine.

**Ingredients:**

- **1,114g** water
- **240g** xylitol (allulose also works)
- **144g** inulin
- **3 tbsp** dried hibiscus flowers
- **5g** xylitol (reserved for stabilizer mix)
- **2g** guar gum

**Steps:**

1. Add 1,114g water, 240g xylitol, and 144g inulin to a pan. Warm gently over low heat, whisking until fully dissolved (around 5 minutes). Do not boil.
2. Turn off heat. Add 3 tablespoons of dried hibiscus flowers and stir in. Leave to steep for 1 hour at room temperature.
3. After steeping, combine 5g xylitol and 2g guar gum in a small bowl, mix together, then stir into the warm hibiscus mixture. The residual heat is sufficient to dissolve both.
4. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into Ninja Creami pints, discarding the spent hibiscus flowers.
5. Freeze overnight (minimum 24 hours).
6. Spin on Sorbet mode. No re-spin needed — texture should be smooth with a flat top.

**Notes:**

- Sorbet requires 25-35% sugar-substitute content by weight for proper texture; without this, the result is dry and powdery — do not reduce xylitol significantly.
- Nick's calculated stats for this recipe: serving temperature -15C, zero sugar, 25.4% solids (slightly low), 74% water (slightly high), POD 169, PAC 368.
- Guar gum is used instead of CMC/xanthan because there is no need to heat the base to 85C.
- The steeped hibiscus produces a vivid deep purple-red color.
- Nick notes that a well-balanced recipe should produce a flat top after freezing; a domed/bumped top indicates the mix needs adjustment.
