### [How to make the most delicious dulce de leche gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D0-aAwonX7U)
Argentinian-style Dulce de Leche gelato adapted from Gellö Helados (Vera Cruz, Mexico), using two types of Argentine DDL for an authentic, less-sweet flavor profile.

**Ingredients:**
- **471g** water (no milk in this recipe)
- **60g** skimmed milk powder
- **28g** whole milk powder
- **60g** dextrose (powdered glucose if dextrose unavailable)
- **150g** whipping cream (33% fat)
- **126g** Havana dulce de leche (repostero/pastry-grade DDL)
- **90g** Vacalin dulce de leche
- Stabilizer blend: carrageenan + locust bean gum + guar gum (amounts not specified on screen)
- Emulsifier: sunflower lecithin (amount not specified on screen)

**Steps:**
1. Combine water, skimmed milk powder, whole milk powder, dextrose, and the stabilizer/emulsifier blend in a pot. Stir to combine.
2. Add whipping cream and both dulce de leche varieties.
3. Heat on the hob, bringing to 85°C and holding for a couple of minutes to fully hydrate the stabilizers and homogenize the mix.
4. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, and refrigerate overnight (minimum 4 hours).
5. Next day, stir the chilled base well, then churn in an ice cream machine until it reaches approximately -10 to -12°C.
6. Transfer to ice cream containers and freeze for 24 hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- Recipe is a slightly modified version of Gellö Helados' original; adapted for home ice cream machines.
- Two DDL types are used: a repostero/pastry-grade DDL (Havana) designed for ice cream use, and a standard table-grade DDL (Vacalin) — each has a different flavor strength.
- Argentine DDL is notably less sweet and more complex than North American/UK canned DDL (e.g. Eagle Brand); sourcing authentic Argentine DDL is strongly recommended. Nick sourced his from www.theargentino.com (ships worldwide from Patagonia).
- The invert sugars naturally present in dulce de leche give good scoopability straight from the freezer — no temper wait needed.
- Base is designed as a mix-in-friendly recipe; primed for nuts and chocolate add-ins.
- Must heat to 85°C due to carrageenan and locust bean gum requiring high-temperature hydration.
- Chill overnight for best results (4 hours minimum).
