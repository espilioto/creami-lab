### [Malted Mini Egg Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AdISavTeLbU)

A malted ice cream base churned with micro mini eggs — inspired by a Calgary ice cream shop (Marble Slab / Made by Marcus style seasonal flavour).

**Batch:** 1500g — makes 2x Ninja Creami Deluxe pints or 3x standard Ninja Creami pints.

**Ingredients:**
- **564g** whole milk
- **66g** whipping cream
- **170g** sugar
- **60g** dextrose
- **67g** skim milk powder
- **2g** tara gum
- **4g** sunflower lecithin (emulsifier)
- **50g** dry malt extract (medium roast — powder or liquid)
- **3 packs** micro mini eggs (Cadbury-style; broken up slightly)

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk and whipping cream in a saucepan.
2. Mix together sugar, dextrose, skim milk powder, tara gum, and lecithin in a small bowl; whisk to combine and break up clumps.
3. Add the dry ingredient mix to the milk/cream and stir to incorporate.
4. Add the dry malt extract slowly (it's a fine powder — add carefully to avoid dust).
5. Heat the base to 85°C (185°F) and hold for 5 minutes, stirring throughout.
6. Strain through a fine mesh sieve to remove any undissolved lecithin lumps.
7. Cool, then refrigerate for 24 hours to let the malt flavour develop.
8. Whisk the chilled base to re-homogenise, then churn in your ice cream machine.
9. In the final stages of churning, add the micro mini eggs (a mix of whole and lightly crushed pieces).
10. Transfer to tubs and freeze overnight to harden.

**Notes:**
- Nick suggests doubling the malt extract (100g) for a stronger malt flavour — 50g gives a medium intensity.
- If using liquid malt extract, use the same weight (50g or 100g); it adds a negligible amount of extra water.
- Micro mini eggs are strongly preferred over standard size — they don't freeze rock-solid and won't break your teeth; their candy shell colour also leeches into the base for a decorative effect.
- Use medium or dark roast dry malt extract for best flavour depth; light roast lacks intensity.
- Tara gum requires reaching 85°C to hydrate and activate properly.
- Strain the base before chilling — lecithin can leave visible lumps.
- Malt extract is available at homebrew/beer-making shops or online (Amazon).
