### [Tiramisu Ice Cream in the Ninja Creami?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVqzrxzi0A4)

A double-layered tiramisu pint: coffee-soaked ladyfinger base, thin dark chocolate divider, and a cooked cream cheese gelato top — spun on Lite Ice Cream (Deluxe TOP mode).

**Batch:** Recipe makes 2 pints (quantities below are for both tubs combined).

---

**Ingredients — Coffee-Soaked Ladyfinger Base (per 2 pints):**
- ~430–450ml espresso (4 lungo shots; Nick ended up needing 4 after starting with 3)
- 5 tbsp Allulose (erythritol-and-monk-fruit blend; used to depress freezing point of coffee liquid — approx. 20% by weight)
- 12 ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi; Safeway / Amazon / most European supermarkets)

**Ingredients — Chocolate Divider Layer:**
- 50g dark chocolate (60% cocoa)
- 1 tsp deodorized (refined) coconut oil

**Ingredients — Cream Cheese Gelato Top (per 2 pints):**
- 244g full-fat cream cheese (8g fat per 30g; mascarpone is ideal but expensive — standard cream cheese used here)
- 540g milk (2% used; any milk works)
- 79g xylitol (rounded to 80g)
- 35g skimmed milk powder
- 3–4g hot-process stabilizer (proprietary blend; if using xanthan gum, reduce significantly — xanthan goes gummy fast)
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste

---

**Steps:**

1. **Coffee soak:** Brew 4 lungo espresso shots (~430–450ml total). Stir in 5 tbsp allulose until dissolved. Dip ladyfingers one at a time, ensuring they are thoroughly soaked. Layer them in the bottom of each pint tub. Pour a little extra coffee liquid over the top to fill gaps. Freeze for a few hours until cold and set (not fully solid).

2. **Chocolate layer:** Melt 50g dark chocolate with 1 tsp refined coconut oil in the microwave. Remove frozen ladyfinger base from freezer and pour a very thin layer of melted chocolate over the top. Return to freezer — chocolate sets almost instantly on the cold base.

3. **Cream cheese gelato base (cooked):** Add milk to a saucepan. Cut cream cheese into smaller pieces and add. Heat on the hob, stirring, until the mixture reaches 85°C (185°F) — required for the hot-process stabilizer to activate. It will be thick and lumpy at first; keep stirring. Once at temperature, add xylitol, skimmed milk powder, stabilizer, and vanilla bean paste. Stir until smooth. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours to chill completely.

4. **Assemble:** Pour the chilled cream cheese base on top of the set chocolate layer in each tub. Freeze for 24h minimum (36h recommended given the cooked base).

5. **Spin:** Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**, **full tub** mode. The top ice cream layer processes well; the bottom coffee layer may remain harder depending on how much the allulose depressed the freezing point.

6. **Optional chocolate top:** After the ice cream base has frozen hard in the tub (before spinning), add another very thin layer of melted chocolate on top — it shatters into flecks through the ice cream during spinning.

---

**Notes:**
- This is a Ninja Creami **Deluxe** recipe by design — the TOP/FULL/BOTTOM partial-spin feature is used to process only the ice cream layer while leaving the coffee base untouched. Can be made in a standard machine but without that layering control.
- The allulose at ~20% by weight was not quite enough to keep the ladyfinger layer soft at -18°C freezer temp — Nick notes you may need more, or use a higher-PAC sweetener, to keep the bottom layer scoopable rather than frozen solid.
- Nick used a branded Allulose/monk fruit blend ("Vop" / "Arial" — brand name unclear from audio); any allulose or erythritol-based sweetener works for FPD (freezing point depression) in the coffee soak.
- Mascarpone is the traditional choice for the cream layer; cream cheese is a direct, cheaper substitute.
- Xanthan gum can sub for the hot-process stabilizer but use much less — it goes very gummy at higher amounts.
- Result: top ice cream layer was delicious (creamy, cheesecake-like, chocolate flecks); bottom coffee/ladyfinger scoop was good but froze harder than intended. Nick rates concept a success with the noted freezing point adjustment needed.
- Spin mode used: **Lite Ice Cream, full tub**.
