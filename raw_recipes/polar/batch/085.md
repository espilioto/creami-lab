### [Ben & Jerry's Dirt Cake Topped](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZPno_X1pLc)

Vanilla pudding ice cream with Oreo cookie chunks and crumb swirl, topped with a milk chocolate ganache and crushed cookie crumb — a homemade recreation of Ben & Jerry's Dirt Cake Topped.

**Ingredients:**

Base:
- **370g** milk (2%)
- **370g** cream (33% whipping cream)
- **26g** sugar
- **90g** invert sugar / corn syrup
- **10g** vanilla extract (optional — pudding mix provides enough vanilla flavour)
- **56g** skim milk powder
- **84g** Jell-O vanilla pudding mix

Oreo layer & swirl:
- **270g** pack Original Oreos (1 full pack)

Chocolate top:
- **100g** milk chocolate (Cacao Barry, ~41-45% cacao)
- **10g** refined coconut oil (deodorized — no coconut flavour)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, sugar, invert sugar, vanilla extract, skim milk powder, and Jell-O vanilla pudding mix in a saucepan. Whisk until fully combined.
2. Heat to 80°C, then remove from heat. Transfer to a container and refrigerate until completely chilled.
3. Set aside 9 Oreo cookies. Roughly chop the remaining Oreos into large cookie chunks and store separately.
4. Split the 9 reserved Oreos, scrape out the cream filling, then crush the cookie wafers with a pestle and mortar into a coarse crumb (not fine powder). Store separately.
5. Pre-chill your ice cream machine bowl for 5–10 minutes. Pour in the chilled base and churn.
6. A couple of minutes before churning is done, add the large cookie chunks and let them incorporate.
7. Layer into tubs: one scoop of ice cream, a layer of cookie crumb powder, repeat until ice cream is used up. Reserve some cookie crumb for the top. Leave ~1 cm of headspace at the top.
8. Freeze tubs for approximately 3 hours until the surface is firm and smooth.
9. Melt 100g chocolate with 10g coconut oil in the microwave (~30 seconds). Stir until fully melted and emulsified.
10. Gently pour the melted chocolate over the top of each frozen tub. Immediately sprinkle the reserved cookie crumb on top.
11. Return to freezer to harden completely before serving.

**Notes:**

- This recipe makes multiple tubs (batch yield — description states 270g pack of Oreos across the batch).
- Jell-O vanilla pudding mix is the key flavour driver; vanilla extract is optional and can be omitted.
- Use deodorized (refined) coconut oil for the chocolate top — it has zero coconut flavour. Extra virgin olive oil also works and adds a subtle flavour note.
- For the cookie swirl, remove the cream filling from the reserved 9 Oreos before crushing — only the wafer crumb is used.
- Leave ~1 cm headspace in the tub before freezing so the chocolate top fits within the lid line.
- Nick uses Cacao Barry milk chocolate (~41% cacao) for the topping; any quality milk chocolate around 40–45% cacao works.
- Recipe metrics (base only): ~13% fat, ~17% sugar, ~10.9% MSNF (milk solids-non-fat), ~41.5% total solids, ~58.3% water.
