### [Ben & Jerry's Cookie Dough Twist: A Decadent Delight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VJBAtzcF8f4)

A churned chocolate ice cream base inspired by Ben & Jerry's Dough-ble Chocolate Cookie Dough Twist, paired with a no-bake chocolate cookie dough core.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **250g** double/heavy cream
- **300g** water
- **40g** sugar
- **65g** dextrose (or use 110g total sugar if no dextrose)
- **50g** cocoa powder, 12% fat (De Zaan)
- **100g** condensed milk
- **2** egg yolks
- **50g** chocolate chips, 56% cocoa (Barima), added at end of churn
- **0.7g** locust bean gum (optional)
- **0.45g** guar gum (optional — blended with the sugars as a 70/30 guar/carrageenan mix)

Cookie Dough Core:
- **60g** unsalted butter, room temperature
- **15g** sugar (white)
- **55g** brown sugar
- **5g** salt
- **30g** milk
- **15g** golden syrup (or regular corn syrup — not high-fructose)
- **5g** vanilla extract
- **70–90g** all-purpose/plain flour (heat-treated: bake at 180°C for 10 min, cool completely)
- **50g** cocoa powder, 12% fat
- **60g** semi-sweet chocolate chips

**Steps:**

Ice Cream Base:
1. Add water to a pan. Add dextrose, sugar, and stabilizers (mixed dry into the sugars). Heat and dissolve.
2. Add condensed milk and egg yolks. Stir continuously.
3. Add cocoa powder as the mix heats up. Cook to 65–70°C to pasteurise the egg yolks.
4. Add cream and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
5. Transfer to a container, refrigerate until fully chilled (3–4 hours).
6. Churn in ice cream machine as normal. Add chocolate chips just before the end of the churn.

Cookie Dough Core:
1. Beat room-temperature butter with white sugar and brown sugar until well combined.
2. Mix in vanilla extract and golden syrup.
3. Add cocoa powder and heat-treated flour. Mix well.
4. Fold in chocolate chips.
5. Transfer mixture to a bag and refrigerate until cooled and firm.
6. Roll into a cylinder shape. Freeze until solid.

Assembly:
1. Fill the tub with churned ice cream, leaving about 2.5cm (1 inch) of space at the top.
2. Press the frozen cookie dough cylinder straight down into the centre of the tub.
3. Top up any gaps with remaining ice cream mix.
4. Freeze for several hours until fully hardened.

**Notes:**
- The base uses no milk — condensed milk and water replace it, following Ben & Jerry's actual ingredient list.
- The base will be noticeably thick and viscous before churning; this is expected and typically yields a creamier, less icy result.
- Heat-treat flour at 180°C for 10 minutes and cool completely before use — do not sieve it while still hot.
- Golden syrup can be substituted with light corn syrup; avoid high-fructose corn syrup.
- Barima 56% chocolate chips and De Zaan cocoa powder were used in the original and praised for flavour.
- Nick rated this significantly better than the actual Ben & Jerry's product.
