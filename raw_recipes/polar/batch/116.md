### [Making Häagen Dazs Midnight Cookies & Cream / Triple Chocolate Fudge Cookie / Chocolate Midnight Cookies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sc-1dZwI5zs)

Haagen-Dazs recreation of a discontinued custard-based chocolate cookies & cream with a homemade chocolate fudge swirl and cream-removed Oreo pieces, churned in a Musso 4080.

**Ingredients — Base:**
- **300ml** milk
- **300ml** double (heavy) cream (49% fat)
- **100g** white sugar
- **30g** dark muscovado sugar
- **2 Tbsp** skimmed milk powder
- **2** egg yolks
- **20g** cocoa powder
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract

**Ingredients — Chocolate Fudge Swirl:**
- **50g** sugar
- **50g** 70% dark chocolate
- **15g** cocoa powder (reduce to 10g — see Notes)
- **50ml** milk
- **3 Tbsp** corn syrup
- **1/2 Tbsp** coconut oil (deodorised)
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- Pinch of kosher salt

**Ingredients — Mix-In:**
- Oreo cookies (cream filling removed, cookies chopped)

**Steps:**
1. Combine all base ingredients except the cocoa in a pan. Add egg yolks directly — no tempering needed.
2. Slowly bring to 80–85°C, stirring continuously. This pasteurises the eggs in-pan without traditional tempering.
3. Add the cocoa powder and combine well.
4. Transfer to a container, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
5. For the fudge: combine milk and cocoa in a pan over gentle heat, then add corn syrup and coconut oil. Mixture may look slightly separated — keep going.
6. Bring fudge to a rolling boil, then remove from heat. Add the chopped chocolate and stir well to combine.
7. Transfer fudge to a container and cool to room temperature.
8. While the base is churning in the machine, split Oreos, scrape out and discard the cream, and chop the cookie wafers.
9. Once churned, layer ice cream into a tub, folding in cookie pieces and fudge swirl as you go.
10. Freeze for a few hours until set, then serve.

**Notes:**
- Fudge cocoa: 15g was slightly too intense compared to the original Haagen-Dazs. Drop to 10g for a closer match.
- The fudge recipe is a reverse-engineered approximation — no official Haagen-Dazs numbers exist. Results are very good but not identical.
- Oreo cream is removed because the original Haagen-Dazs uses plain chocolate cookie wafers with no cream filling.
- Machine used: Musso 4080.
