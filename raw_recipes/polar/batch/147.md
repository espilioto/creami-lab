### [Super EASY Ninja Creami Coffee Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-LM4HRpu-k)
American-style coffee ice cream made with 18% coffee cream (UK single cream), condensed milk, golden syrup, and alcohol-based coffee extract — no stabilizers, no churning.

**Ingredients:**
- **635g** 18% fat coffee cream (UK: single cream; North America: coffee cream)
- **350g** sweetened condensed milk (1 standard 300ml can)
- **10g** golden syrup (or corn syrup / glucose syrup)
- **4g** Nielsen-Massey coffee extract (alcohol-based — essential)

**Steps:**
1. Combine cream, condensed milk, golden syrup, and coffee extract in a bowl. Whisk briefly to combine — no need to worry about air bubbles; this cream won't whip.
2. Split the mixture evenly between two Ninja Creami pints.
3. Lid on, freeze overnight (minimum — full freeze required).
4. Spin on Ice Cream.
5. Serve as-is or stir in a spoonful of dulce de leche and top with grated Valrhona Dulcey (blonde caramel chocolate).

**Notes:**
- Makes 2 pints (batch recipe — each pint gets half the mix).
- Extract must be alcohol/ethanol-based — this is what balances freezing point and texture. Water-based flavourings will not work the same way.
- Golden/corn/glucose syrup is optional but improves mouthfeel and freezing point balance; omit if unavailable.
- If adding dulce de leche or any liquid sauce, stir it in after spinning rather than using the Mix-In program — the program distributes sauces too thinly.
- A well-balanced recipe: once spun, it can be refrozen and eaten later without respinning.
- Nick uses Nielsen-Massey coffee extract; any quality alcohol-based coffee extract works.
