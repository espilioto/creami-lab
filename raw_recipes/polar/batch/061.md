### [EASY Coconut Frozen Yogurt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4qe6zR9JkA)

Simple 4-ingredient coconut frozen yogurt — creamy, tangy, and strong coconut flavour with no stabilizers.

**Ingredients:**
- **296g** coconut Greek yogurt (2% fat or less; coconut flakes inside are a bonus)
- **327g** coconut cream (~just under a full standard can)
- **100g** allulose (can substitute regular sugar or xylitol — use less xylitol)
- **37g** coconut milk powder (used in place of skim milk powder for full coconut theme)

**Steps:**
1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan. Warm gently to 40–50°C to fully dissolve the allulose and disperse the coconut milk powder — do not overheat.
2. Stir until smooth, then pour into the Ninja Creami Deluxe pint container.
3. Freeze for a minimum of 24 hours.
4. Spin on Ice Cream setting. The first spin may leave the centre unprocessed (expected given the thick, high-solids base) — run a second spin immediately after.

**Notes:**
- Recipe is sized for the Ninja Creami Deluxe (750ml pint). For the standard Creami, reduce all amounts by roughly 1/3.
- Nick explicitly flags this as an off-balance/"experimental" recipe — high fat and high solids from coconut cream + coconut milk powder together. It works well despite being outside normal calculator ranges.
- The base is very thick before freezing; not suitable for a traditional churn machine.
- A double spin was required to get smooth texture — factor this in.
- Texture and flavour result described as excellent: strong but not overpowering coconut, tangy yogurt note, great mouthfeel.
- Optional: fold in toasted coconut flakes as a mix-in after spinning to further elevate the coconut experience.
- The description notes any Greek yogurt flavour works as a 1-for-1 swap (replace coconut yogurt with any flavour + matching powder/cream) for a simple frozen yogurt template.
