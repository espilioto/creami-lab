### [White Chocolate Gelato (Gelato Messina) with Fudge Pretzels & Fudge Swirl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFR1lMHRaEQ)

Gelato Messina-sourced white chocolate gelato churned in an ice cream machine, layered with white chocolate fudge swirl and fudge-covered pretzels.

**Ingredients — Gelato Base:**
- **670g** milk
- **100g** sugar
- **15g** skimmed milk powder
- **30g** dextrose (or glucose)
- **5g** stabilizer
- **180g** white chocolate, chopped

**Ingredients — White Chocolate Fudge Swirl:**
- egg (quantity not specified)
- sugar (quantity not specified)
- white chocolate (quantity not specified)
- butter (quantity not specified)

**Ingredients — Mix-In:**
- fudge-covered pretzels (quantity to taste)

**Steps:**
1. Pour milk into a pan and slowly heat to 40°C.
2. Add the dry ingredients (sugar, dextrose, skimmed milk powder, stabilizer) and stir to combine.
3. Continue heating to approximately 65°C (mix will start to steam lightly). Hold at 65°C for 30 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes to prevent sticking.
4. Remove from heat and place pan in an ice bath. While cooling, add the chopped white chocolate and stir until melted and fully incorporated.
5. Continue cooling in the ice bath, stirring every 10–15 minutes, until the base reaches 4°C.
6. Transfer to the fridge until ready to churn.
7. Before churning, give the base a thorough whisk (immersion blender or hand whisk).
8. Churn in an ice cream machine.
9. Make the fudge: in one bowl whisk egg and sugar together; in a second bowl gently melt white chocolate and butter in the microwave (carefully — white chocolate burns easily). Combine both bowls and whisk smooth. Set aside.
10. Once churning is done, layer into the tub: spoon fudge swirl into the bottom, add broken fudge-covered pretzels, alternate gelato and mix-ins layer by layer.
11. Freeze until firm.

**Notes:**
- Recipe is adapted from the Gelato Messina recipe book (available at gelatomessina.com or Google Books).
- This is a traditional churned gelato — not a Ninja Creami recipe.
- Gelato style means less fat than ice cream, which lets the white chocolate flavour come through more intensely.
- Best eaten the same day; will keep a couple of days in the freezer before texture degrades.
- White chocolate is very sensitive to heat — melt gently in short microwave bursts.
- Fudge swirl uses white chocolate rather than dark for consistency with the base flavour.
