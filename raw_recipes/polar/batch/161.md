### [Ultimate Nutella Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_HPWVlTdEKQ)

Super-premium churned Nutella ice cream with brownie bites and a marshmallow (Swiss meringue) swirl — inspired by Ben & Jerry's Phish Food style.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **300ml** double cream
- **250ml** whole milk
- **65g** Nutella
- **75g** granulated sugar
- **50g** high-quality cocoa powder
- **1/2 tsp** vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- **75g** milk powder (added after chilling, before churning)

Brownie Bites:
- **100g** frozen brownies (crushed/powdered)
- **2 tbsp** Nutella
- Chocolate mould

Marshmallow Swirl (Swiss meringue):
- **2** large egg whites
- **100g** granulated sugar
- **1/4 tsp** cream of tartar
- **1/2 tsp** vanilla extract (optional)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, and sugar in a saucepan over gentle heat. Add Nutella and stir until fully dissolved and smooth.
2. Add cocoa powder and stir to incorporate. Turn off heat and cool for 5–6 minutes.
3. Add vanilla extract and a pinch of salt; stir well.
4. Transfer to a fridge-safe container, cover with cling film, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
5. After chilling, stir in 75g milk powder. Return to fridge for 20 minutes to allow the powder to dissolve fully.
6. Pour base into ice cream maker (pre-cool bowl if required) and churn for 20–25 minutes until thick and creamy.
7. **Brownie bites:** Crush/powder 100g frozen brownies. Mix with 2 tbsp Nutella to form a thick paste. Press into a chocolate mould and freeze solid.
8. **Marshmallow swirl:** Combine egg whites, sugar, and cream of tartar in a heatproof bowl/pan. Heat to 71°C, stirring. Remove from heat and whisk vigorously for 5–6 minutes until thick and glossy. Add vanilla if using.
9. **Assembly:** Layer churned ice cream, marshmallow swirl, and brownie bites in a container, repeating layers until full.
10. Freeze overnight before serving.

**Notes:**

- Sugar is reduced in the base because Nutella already contributes significant sugar.
- Milk powder is added cold (not heated with the base) to avoid any scorching; the 20-minute fridge rest lets it dissolve.
- Brownie bites must be frozen solid before mixing with Nutella so they can be powdered; store-bought brownies can substitute homemade.
- The marshmallow swirl is a Swiss meringue — a thermometer is recommended to hit 71°C accurately. Whisking off the heat for 5–6 minutes develops the thick, stable swirl texture (same technique Ben & Jerry's uses for marshmallow ribbons).
- Freeze result overnight; let sit out a couple of minutes before scooping to avoid melting too fast.
- This is a **churned** recipe using a traditional ice cream maker, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
