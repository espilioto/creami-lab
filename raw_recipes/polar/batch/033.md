### [Burnt Basque Cheesecake Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DU_V3sqmiM)

A lemon cheesecake ice cream base loaded with large cubes of homemade burnt basque cheesecake. Batch recipe (traditional ice cream machine, not Ninja Creami).

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **550g** milk, 1.8% fat
- **150g** cream, 50% fat
- **250g** mascarpone cheese, 40–45% fat
- **150g** sugar
- **75g** dextrose
- **65g** skimmed milk powder
- **5g** lemon extract (Nelson Massey brand used)
- **2g** stabiliser (optional)

Burnt Basque Cheesecake:
- **600g** cream cheese (e.g. Philadelphia), room temperature
- **4** eggs
- **225g** sugar
- **40g** plain flour
- **300g** double cream
- **5ml** vanilla extract (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Steps:**

Burnt Basque Cheesecake:
1. Bring cream cheese to room temperature. Place in a bowl, add sugar, and mix until smooth and well combined — do not whip.
2. Add eggs one at a time, mixing just until combined after each — no whipping.
3. Add flour and mix in thoroughly.
4. Add double cream (and vanilla extract if using) and mix until completely combined.
5. Line a springform pan with baking parchment — fold it up over the edges since the batter rises.
6. Pour batter into the prepared pan.
7. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C fan for 45–50 minutes. Check through the oven door — do not open it. Adjust temperature down if your oven runs hot; slight overbrowning on top is acceptable.
8. Remove from oven and cool to room temperature, then refrigerate overnight minimum.
9. Once fully chilled, cut into bite-sized cubes. Keep refrigerated until needed.

Ice Cream Base:
1. Add milk to a pan. Add dextrose, sugar, and stabiliser (if using — pre-mix stabiliser into the sugars before adding). Heat and dissolve completely.
2. Add mascarpone — bring to room temperature first if possible to help it incorporate. Whisk into the warm milk until fully combined.
3. Once fully combined, add skimmed milk powder and mix through.
4. Turn off the heat and add lemon extract. Give a final mix.
5. Finally, stir in the cream (not heated — added off the heat).
6. Transfer to a container and refrigerate until fully chilled, approximately 3–4 hours.
7. Once chilled, give the mix a final stir and churn in an ice cream machine until done.

Assembly:
1. While the ice cream is churning, take the cheesecake cubes out of the fridge.
2. When churning is complete, load the tub by alternating scoops of ice cream with cubes of cheesecake.
3. Top with 2–3 cubes of burnt basque cheesecake to finish.
4. Freeze for 4–5 hours until hardened.

**Notes:**
- Nick over-baked his cheesecake but considered it still excellent — don't be discouraged by imperfect browning.
- Mascarpone is ~40–45% fat, so only a small amount of extra cream is needed; the mascarpone carries the richness.
- Bring mascarpone to room temperature before mixing — cold mascarpone is harder to incorporate into warm milk but will combine eventually with sustained whisking.
- Stabiliser is optional but recommended if you won't eat the whole pint in one sitting.
- Nick's afterthought: make a biscuit base (digestive/graham cracker + butter), freeze it, then break it up and scatter over the top at serving for extra texture.
- The original Crumbs and Doilies recipe called for 200°C fan for 50–60 min; Nick recommends checking from 45 min and adjusting for your oven.
