### [Ben & Jerry's Chocolate Fudge Brownie - make at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cd9Pvt5btPQ)

A full recreation of B&J's Chocolate Fudge Brownie — homemade gooey malt brownies folded into a medium-dark chocolate ice cream base.

---

**Gooey Brownies:**

- **120g** all-purpose flour
- **60g** diastatic malt powder
- **50g** dark chocolate cocoa powder
- **200g** semisweet chocolate
- **57g** dark chocolate
- **250g** granulated sugar
- **50g** brown sugar
- **4** eggs
- **260g** butter

**Ice Cream Base:**

- **60g** dark cocoa powder @ 24% fat (Calibo brand used; 55g primary + 5g dark/black cocoa — Van Gaal 100, 12% fat)
- **300g** double cream @ 49% fat (UK; US/Canada substitute: 500g whipping cream + reduce milk to 450g)
- **165g** sugar
- **550g** whole milk @ 1.8% fat (UK; US/Canada: 450g)
- **50g** skimmed milk powder
- **50g** glucose syrup
- **2** egg yolks
- **1g** stabilizer (optional)

---

**Steps:**

**Brownies:**
1. Melt the semisweet chocolate, dark chocolate, and butter together in a microwave (or bain-marie) until fully melted.
2. Add in the eggs and sugar; combine well.
3. Add all dry ingredients (flour, malt powder, cocoa powder) and mix until fully incorporated.
4. Pour into a lined 8×8 inch (20×20 cm) baking pan.
5. Bake at 200°C (385°F) for 25 minutes. Do not exceed this temperature.
6. Remove from oven, cool slightly, then cut into ~1–1.5 cm cubes. Cool completely before use.

**Ice Cream Base:**
1. Add glucose syrup to a saucepan, then pour milk in on top.
2. Whisk egg yolks until smooth and add them to the pan.
3. Add the cocoa powder (both types if using the two-cocoa approach) and whisk to combine.
4. Add skimmed milk powder and mix thoroughly.
5. Heat gently, stirring, until the mixture reaches 65°C.
6. Remove from heat and pour into a container. Add the double cream and stir to combine.
7. Add sugar and stabilizer (if using); mix well.
8. Chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
9. Churn in an ice cream machine until soft-serve consistency.
10. Layer churned ice cream and brownie cubes into a tub and freeze for several hours before serving.

---

**Notes:**

- This is a batch recipe — makes more brownies than needed for one batch of ice cream. Leftovers keep well.
- The diastatic malt powder is key to replicating the subtle maltiness in the original B&J brownie. Nick discovered this note in the real product and reverse-engineered it from the ingredients list.
- B&J's brownies are made by Greyston Bakery in Yonkers, NY — Nick adapted a Greyston-style recipe with the malt addition.
- Two cocoa powders used for the ice cream: a high-fat primary cocoa (Calibo 24%) for bulk flavor, plus a small amount of intense black cocoa (Van Gaal 100, 12% fat) to deepen color and achieve a medium-dark chocolate profile.
- B&J's chocolate ice creams are formulated to ~13–15% fat, ~16–18% sugar. The recipe is built to approximate these ratios.
- For US/Canada: swap 300g double cream + 550g milk for 500g whipping cream + 450g milk — approximate fat equivalence.
- Do not exceed 200°C when baking the brownies — they should be gooey, not fully set.
- Pan size matters: recipe is calibrated for an 8×8 pan. Larger pans will need reduced bake time.
