### [Honey Pistachio Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LR6ZCUFZQI0)

Italian-style gelato made with real roasted pistachios blended into a paste, honey as the primary sweetener, and optional shaved dark chocolate mixed in.

**Note: This is a large batch recipe (multiple pints). Scale down as needed.**

**Ingredients:**

- **226g** heavy cream (33–35% fat)
- **128g** milk (2% or 3.25% / semi-skimmed or full fat)
- **205g** sugar
- **154g** honey (local/raw recommended; reduce by 20g for firmer result)
- **50g** skim milk powder
- **150g** roasted salted pistachios
- **50g** dark chocolate (60–80%), finely chopped/shaved (optional but recommended)
- **4g** almond extract (optional)
- **4g** stabilizer — Nick uses his "442 blend" (guar gum + xanthan gum based; substitute your own stabilizer blend or omit)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, cream, honey, sugar, and skim milk powder in a saucepan. Whisk in the stabilizer (pre-mixed with a small amount of the sugar to prevent clumping).
2. Heat on the hob, stirring constantly to prevent honey from burning on the bottom. Bring to 85°C (185°F) to activate the stabilizer, then remove from heat.
3. While the base heats, add the roasted salted pistachios to a small blender. Blend until as fine as possible — it will be chunky at this stage.
4. Once the base is hot, add a couple of spoonfuls of the hot milk mixture into the blender with the pistachios. Blend again until a smooth pistachio paste forms. Add more hot liquid if needed to loosen.
5. Add the pistachio paste into the hot base and stir to combine. If using almond extract, stir it in now.
6. Chill the base in the refrigerator for 4–5 hours to let the pistachio and honey flavors develop. (Ice bath optional for faster chilling.)
7. Before churning, whisk the chilled base well to re-incorporate any pistachio solids that have settled.
8. **Churned version:** Transfer to a pre-chilled ice cream machine and churn. In the last few minutes of churning, add the finely chopped/shaved dark chocolate. Dish into containers and freeze for at least 12 hours before serving.
9. **Ninja Creami version:** Pour base into a Ninja Creami Deluxe tub, freeze for at least 24 hours. Spin on Ice Cream setting. After the first spin, make a divot in the center, add the chopped chocolate, and run the Mix-In setting.

**Notes:**

- This is a large batch — feel free to halve the recipe or fill multiple Ninja Creami pints.
- Skim milk powder is important for milk solids and proper texture; technically optional but Nick recommends including it.
- Almond extract boosts pistachio flavor (pistachios and almonds share flavor compounds). Don't overdo it or it dominates. Nick uses 4g for this large batch.
- The natural color of real pistachio is a pale/muted green — no artificial coloring needed, but green food coloring can be added for a more vibrant look.
- Honey functions like corn syrup (fructose/glucose mix), depressing the freezing point and keeping the gelato soft/scoopable. If the result is too soft, reduce honey by ~20g.
- Nick uses Callebaut or Belcolade 80% cacao chocolate for the mix-in. The shards stay intact in the Creami Deluxe on Mix-In setting.
- Use a honey variety you enjoy — stronger-flavored honeys will be more pronounced in the final gelato.
