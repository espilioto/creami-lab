### [Ultra Low Sugar Strawberry Sorbet in Ninja Creami](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ydlvz5OS3s)

Strawberry sorbet with xylitol instead of sugar, corn syrup or invert sugar for texture, taragum as stabilizer, and an optional reduced balsamic glaze.

**Ingredients — Sorbet (1 pint):**
- **135g** fresh strawberries
- **264g** water
- **68g** xylitol
- **30g** corn syrup (or invert sugar)
- **0.5g** taragum (stabilizer)
- **0.5g** sea salt

**Ingredients — Balsamic Glaze (optional topping):**
- **~100ml** balsamic vinegar (reduce by half on hob)

**Steps:**
1. Quarter the strawberries and add to a pan with the water, xylitol, corn syrup/invert sugar, sea salt, and taragum.
2. Heat on the hob over steady medium heat, stirring to dissolve the xylitol. Cook the strawberries down for 10–15 minutes until soft enough to break apart with a whisk or fork.
3. Remove from heat and let cool for 5–10 minutes. Do not blend while still hot.
4. Once cooled enough, blend (pulse first, then run on low for 20–30 seconds). Be careful with residual heat — lid can burst from pressure if mixture is still hot.
5. Pour into Ninja Creami pint tub. Freeze minimum 24 hours (36h recommended for cooked bases).
6. For the balsamic glaze: simmer ~100ml balsamic vinegar on the hob and reduce by half until syrupy. Store separately.
7. Spin on Sorbet. Dish up and drizzle reduced balsamic glaze over the top. If eating from the tub, poke holes with a chopstick and pour glaze in — use sparingly, it is very concentrated.

**Notes:**
- The mix may visibly layer/separate in the freezer as solids settle. Stir back together before re-freezing — this is normal with high-fruit sorbets.
- Xylitol has a very strong freezing point depression effect; 68g is near the upper limit for a pint — do not increase beyond this or it will not freeze. A gram scale is essential.
- Corn syrup or invert sugar significantly improves texture; the sorbet will still work without it but will be noticeably inferior.
- Taragum requires heat to hydrate — do not skip the cooking step.
- Strawberries contain ~5% natural sugar, so zero-sugar is not achievable; xylitol keeps added sugar minimal.
- The balsamic glaze is very acidic and concentrated after reduction — it cuts through the sweetness of the sorbet.
- Nick advises against using erythritol ("arrol") in sorbets due to its freezing behaviour (he planned a dedicated video on this).
