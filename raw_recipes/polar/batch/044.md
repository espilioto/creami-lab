### [Cookies n Cream No Churn Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tCtGv4qJ0-4)
- **600ml** double/heavy cream
- vanilla extract (to taste)
- **1 pack** cream-filled cookies (e.g. Oreos), chopped/crushed

**Steps:**

1. Combine condensed milk and double cream in a large bowl.
2. Whisk together until the mixture is thick.
3. Add vanilla extract and mix in.
4. Fold in the chopped/crushed cookies.
5. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze until solid.

**Notes:**

- No ice cream machine required — technique relies on whipping the cream and condensed milk together for structure.
- Use any cream-filled cookie of your choice.
- Nick plans to upload more no-churn flavour variations in future videos.
