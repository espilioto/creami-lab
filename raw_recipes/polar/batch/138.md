### [High Protein Keto Vanilla Cookies & Cream Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HVFqfie3XQ)

High-protein, no-added-sugar keto vanilla base with chocolate mint keto cookies mixed in.

**Ingredients:**
- **290g** high-protein milk (Nutrell 2%, ~7.2% protein by weight — higher than Fairlife)
- **290g** whipping cream (33–36% fat)
- **80g** xylitol
- **85g** vanilla protein powder (Pure Protein Vanilla brand used; 25g protein/serving — check for added sugars)
- **8g** vanilla extract (alcohol-based or glycerin-based both work)
- Keto-friendly cookies for mix-in (high-protein, ultra-low-sugar — chocolate mint variety used; ~24g per 2-cookie serving, 6g fat, 2g sugar, 4g allulose, 4g protein)

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cream, and xylitol in a saucepan. Warm gently on the hob for ~5 minutes until xylitol dissolves — do not overheat.
2. Remove from heat. Whisk in vanilla protein powder until fully combined. Do not cook the protein (overheating causes texture issues).
3. Add vanilla extract and stir through.
4. Pour base into Ninja Creami Deluxe pint. Freeze until solid (base was not heated hot, so freezes faster than a cooked base — still allow at least 24h).
5. Spin on Ice Cream.
6. Make a divot in the centre of the spun pint. Add crushed keto cookies. Run Mix-In.

**Notes:**
- Recipe makes one Ninja Creami Deluxe-sized batch.
- Nick prefers xylitol over erythritol — he finds erythritol always recrystallises over time regardless of quantity. If using erythritol, keep quantities low and stabilise.
- Allulose is a viable xylitol substitute but has a weaker freezing-point-depression effect — use slightly more.
- Vanilla extract (alcohol or glycerin-based) serves dual duty: boosts flavour and lowers freezing point. Vanilla bean powder adds flavour only, no freezing-point effect.
- Protein powders can cause high viscosity in churn machines due to thickeners/gums in the powder — not an issue in the Ninja Creami since the base is frozen solid then processed, not churned.
- Use any keto-friendly cookie for the mix-in; keep them low-sugar to stay on theme.
- Taste result: dense, scoops nicely, strong vanilla protein powder flavour with natural vanilla finish from the extract. Chocolate mint cookies complement the vanilla base well.
