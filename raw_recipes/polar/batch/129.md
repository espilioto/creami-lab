### [Making Vanilla Ice Cream at home - Only 4 Ingredients](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41K6_jrhXzI)

A no-fuss 4-ingredient churned vanilla ice cream using condensed milk, cream, and your choice of vanilla.

**Ingredients:**
- **350g** milk 2%
- **350g** cream 33%
- **400g** sweetened condensed milk (1 can / 300ml / 10 oz)
- **15g** vanilla bean paste (or 15g vanilla extract, or 1 vanilla bean)

**Steps:**
1. If using a vanilla bean, split it and heat gently with the milk on the lowest heat for 30–60 minutes to infuse. Skip this step for extract or paste.
2. Combine milk, cream, condensed milk, and vanilla in a bowl. Whisk for a couple of minutes until fully combined.
3. Refrigerate the mix while the ice cream machine comes down to temperature (~10 minutes).
4. Churn in an ice cream machine for ~20 minutes until soft-serve consistency.
5. Transfer to a container and freeze until firm.

**Notes:**
- Yields approximately 1.4L from ~1.1L of ingredients — about 20–25% overrun, which Nick considers ideal for quality ice cream.
- Vanilla bean paste keeps the caviar (seeds) visible in the final product; all three vanilla forms work equally well.
- The recipe is a base template — swap vanilla for lemon, orange, coffee, chocolate, or hazelnut extract to create variations.
- No heating required when using extract or paste — just mix and churn.
