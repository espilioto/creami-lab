### [AMAZING Black Forest Ice Cream — My TOP 5 Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8gz8cvzkuQ)

Peruvian 100% cacao base churned with Amarena cherry syrup and 70% South African chocolate pieces — a bitter-sweet Black Forest ice cream. Batch/churn recipe (~1,388g base, not single-pint).

**Ingredients:**

*Ice cream base:*
- **705g** milk
- **400g** heavy cream (36%)
- **160g** sugar
- **120g** dextrose
- **2g** stabilizer blend (mixed into sugars before adding to dairy)
- **1g** pink Himalayan salt
- 100% cacao block (Peruvian — Willie's Cacao brand), amount not stated explicitly; used to flavour the full base

*Cherry syrup (thickened — layered in):*
- **100g** Fabbri Amarena wild cherry syrup
- **1 tsp** sugar
- **½ tsp** guar gum

*Mix-in (added in last minutes of churn):*
- 70% South African dark chocolate, chopped

**Steps:**

1. Weigh out sugars (160g sugar + 120g dextrose), salt, and 2g stabilizer blend into a bowl. Whisk the stabilizer through the dry sugars to break up any lumps.
2. Pour milk and cream into a pan. Add the sugar/stabilizer mixture and begin warming on the hob.
3. Chop the 100% cacao block into pieces and add to the warming dairy. Stir continuously — the mix will look bitty and grainy at first; once it passes ~60°C the cocoa fat melts and it comes together.
4. Bring to 85°C (185°F), then remove from heat.
5. Chill completely in the refrigerator — at least 4 hours (let the flavour develop fully).
6. To make the cherry syrup: mix 1 tsp sugar and ½ tsp guar gum together, then stir into 100g Fabbri Amarena wild cherry syrup with a small whisk. Allow to sit in the fridge for a couple of hours if possible (it will thicken further). The syrup works without refrigerating, but thickens better chilled.
7. Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine. In the last couple of minutes of churning, add the chopped 70% South African chocolate so it gets fully incorporated.
8. When churning is complete, layer into tubs: one scoop of ice cream, then a generous spoonful of thickened cherry syrup; repeat until the tub is full.
9. Freeze to harden off completely — approximately 24 hours.
10. Serve directly from the freezer (no need to temper ahead of time).

**Notes:**
- The chocolate base must use a strong 100% cacao — the slight bitterness is intentional and essential to balance the ultra-sweet cherry syrup.
- Nick uses Willie's Cacao (Peruvian 100% cacao block, 185g, two tablets per pack) — excellent quality, UK-based, also available online. Any high-quality 100% cacao works.
- The 70% South African chocolate adds balanced chocolate chip texture throughout.
- Fabbri Amarena wild cherry syrup is a professional-grade culinary syrup (~80% sugar, 1.3kg bottle). Available on Amazon. Any cherry syrup works for the flavour, but it must be cherry.
- Thickening the cherry syrup with guar gum is key — it holds distinct layers in the tub rather than bleeding into the ice cream.
- Nick rates this one of his favourite chocolate ice creams ever — the contrast of bitter base, sweet cherry, and chocolate pieces is the whole point.
- This is a traditional churn recipe (not Ninja Creami).
