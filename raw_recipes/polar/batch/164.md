### [Valrhona Chocolate Ice Cream 2.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oug3TRwgBBE)

Luxury, rich, decadent all-Valrhona chocolate ice cream with a caramel and hazelnut praline swirl — made using Valrhona cocoa powder, Caramelia chocolate, and Valrhona praline.

**Ingredients:**

- **375g** milk
- **300g** cream
- **110g** sugar (sucrose)
- **50g** dextrose
- **45g** Valrhona cocoa powder
- **45g** skimmed milk powder
- **1.5g** stabilizer blend (optional)
- **50g** Valrhona Caramelia chocolate (chopped, mix-in)
- **100g** Valrhona praline (swirl)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, sugar, and dextrose in a saucepan over heat. Stir until completely dissolved.
2. Once the mixture reaches 50–60°C, add the cocoa powder and skimmed milk powder. Whisk until fully homogenized. Add stabilizer blend if using.
3. Remove from heat, transfer to a container, and stir in the cream. Chill in the fridge until completely cold.
4. Give the base a final stir, then churn in an ice cream machine.
5. In the last minute of churning, add the chopped Caramelia chocolate pieces.
6. Layer churned ice cream into containers, swirling the Valrhona praline throughout as you load each layer.
7. Freeze for several hours (or overnight) until fully set.

**Notes:**

- This is a traditional churned recipe, not a Ninja Creami no-churn recipe.
- Valrhona praline is a hazelnut-almond spread — described as "better than Nutella." The recipe was inspired by a Valrhona Instagram post.
- Valrhona Caramelia is a caramel milk chocolate; chopping it small ensures even distribution during the final churn stage.
- Nick notes the ice cream held well in the freezer for close to two months before serving — the stabilizer blend helps with long-term storage.
- Stabilizer blend is listed as optional but recommended if storing for more than a few days.
- Ingredients were funded via Ko-Fi supporter contributions (luxury ingredient batch).
