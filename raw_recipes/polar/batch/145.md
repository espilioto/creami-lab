### [Stunning Hazelnut Gelato in the Ninja Creami - ULTRA smooth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRJ7khMUhdk)

Rich, nutty hazelnut gelato using extract instead of nut butter, with homemade hazelnut praline served alongside.

**Ingredients:**

Gelato base (Ninja Creami Deluxe — 750g batch):
- **567g** cream, 10% fat (half and half in Canada/US)
- **46g** skim milk powder
- **124g** granulated sugar
- **5g** Lauran super strength creamy hazelnut extract
- **4g** guar gum (3g if using cup measurements / less confident in weighing)
- **5g** sunflower lecithin (optional but recommended — improves perceived creaminess)

Hazelnut praline:
- **100g** hazelnuts, roasted
- **100g** granulated sugar (75g also works)

**Steps:**

1. Weigh all gelato base ingredients into a bowl. Add cream first, then skim milk powder, sugar, guar gum, and lecithin. Break up any lecithin lumps with the back of a spoon.
2. Add 5g hazelnut extract. Whisk everything together thoroughly.
3. Pour into Ninja Creami Deluxe container. Freeze overnight (minimum) until frozen solid.
4. For the praline: spread sugar in a flat layer in a pan. Heat on medium until caramelized — aim for a reasonably dark amber, not so dark it turns bitter.
5. Spread roasted hazelnuts on a tray/pan. Pour the hot caramel over the hazelnuts, capturing all the nuts. Let harden completely at room temperature.
6. Once hardened, blend the praline in a blender for at least 5 minutes until semi-smooth paste. Work in stages if using a small blender — let it cool between runs to avoid overheating the motor.
7. Transfer praline paste to a container and refrigerate overnight. Reheat gently when ready to serve.
8. Spin frozen gelato base in the Ninja Creami on Gelato mode.
9. Serve gelato with praline paste drizzled or spooned alongside.

**Notes:**

- Sized for the Ninja Creami Deluxe (750g / ~24oz). For the standard Ninja Creami, remove ~30% of the mix.
- Hazelnut extract brand used: **Lauran super strength creamy hazelnut** — available on Amazon UK, Amazon France, and in the US/Canada. Other brands also available on Amazon UK.
- Real hazelnut butter is extremely difficult to make smooth at home (requires commercial equipment) — extract is the practical solution for home use.
- Guar gum target is 4g for authentic slightly-chewy gelato texture; drop to 3g if unsure or doing cup measurements.
- Lecithin is optional — Nick notes his recipes are balanced without it, but it increases perceived creaminess.
- If praline splits during blending (fat separates from solids): dissolve 3g lecithin in a small amount of warm water (~half to 3/4 tsp), add the emulsion to the blender, and re-blend. Result will be smooth.
- Praline works with any flavorful nut — hazelnuts and almonds are most common.
- Single spin result described as: "perfect texture, ultra smooth, slightly chewy — how gelato is meant to be."
