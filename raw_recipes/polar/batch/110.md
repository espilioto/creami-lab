### [Pumpkin Spice Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=liA8HlIivC4)
Low-fat pumpkin spice gelato using allulose and a homemade spice blend, spun on Lite Ice Cream.

**Ingredients:**

Pumpkin Spice Blend (make ahead):
- **3 tbsp** ground cinnamon
- **2 tsp** ground ginger
- **2 tsp** nutmeg
- **1.5 tsp** ground allspice
- **1.5 tsp** ground cloves

Gelato Base:
- **521g** 2% milk
- **100g** whipping cream (33–36% fat)
- **95g** allulose
- **31g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **5–10g** pumpkin spice blend (to taste; Nick used ~5g / 1 tsp)
- **2g** cold-process stabilizer (guar gum or xanthan gum)
- **1.5g** lecithin (added as emulsifier to help cohesion of fats; ideally pre-mixed with allulose)

**Steps:**
1. Combine all spice blend ingredients in a jar, shake well to break up any lumps of ginger. Set aside.
2. Pre-mix stabilizer into the skim milk powder to prevent clumping when added to liquid.
3. Add milk, cream, allulose, SMP+stabilizer mix, and pumpkin spice blend to a saucepan.
4. Add lecithin (ideally pre-mixed with the allulose/sugar). Heat on the hob for ~5 minutes, stirring to dissolve all powders.
5. Pour into Ninja Creami pint, stir to distribute pumpkin spice (dry spices are hydrophobic and will settle).
6. Freeze for minimum 24 hours.
7. Spin on **Lite Ice Cream** (fat is very low, ~5.5g total; water content ~70.6%, higher than Nick's preferred 68% max).
8. Serve plain (Scoop A) or topped with pumpkin spice caramel (Scoop B — Patreon recipe).

**Notes:**
- This is a ~750g batch, slightly over a standard pint.
- Pumpkin spice can be bought pre-made (common in the US) or mixed from scratch as above.
- Stir the pint periodically while freezing (e.g., once an hour) to keep spices evenly distributed, as dry spices settle in liquid.
- Pre-mix lecithin with the allulose/sugar before adding to the base to avoid clumping.
- Nick chose Lite Ice Cream over Gelato mode due to the very low fat content.
- Nick notes: remove the cream and calorie savings come at the cost of a poor mouthfeel/texture.
