### [Ruby Chocolate Ice Cream with Fudge Pieces and Strawberry Swirl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gs4oOrHYbA)

Valentine's Day ruby chocolate ice cream base with Chambord, homemade dark chocolate fudge pieces, and a sieved strawberry-Chambord swirl, churned in an ICE100 Cuisinart machine.

**Ingredients:**

Fudge:
- **400ml** condensed milk
- **340g** dark chocolate

Strawberry Swirl:
- **100–150g** seedless strawberry jam
- **1 Tbsp** Chambord

Ice Cream Base:
- **250ml** milk
- **500ml** double (heavy) cream
- **140g** sugar
- **110g** ruby chocolate
- **2–3 Tbsp** skimmed milk powder
- **1 Tbsp** Chambord

**Steps:**

Fudge:
1. Melt condensed milk and dark chocolate together.
2. Pour into a mould and refrigerate until set.
3. Keep a piece in the freezer when ready to grate (grating frozen makes it easier).

Strawberry Swirl:
1. Sieve the strawberry jam to remove seeds.
2. Stir in the Chambord.
3. Refrigerate until ready to use.

Ice Cream Base:
1. Combine milk, sugar, and ruby chocolate in a pan; gently heat to just steaming.
2. Remove from heat, cover, and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours.
3. When ready to churn, add the skimmed milk powder and leave for 20 minutes.
4. Whisk thoroughly, then add the cream and Chambord.
5. Churn in Cuisinart ICE100 for 20–25 minutes until done.
6. Layer into the container with grated fudge pieces and the strawberry-Chambord swirl.

**Notes:**

- Ruby chocolate gives a naturally fruity, slightly tart flavour — pairs well with strawberry and raspberry notes from the Chambord.
- Chambord is used in both the swirl and the base to amplify berry intensity.
- Grating the fudge from frozen produces fine pieces that distribute evenly.
- Skimmed milk powder is added after chilling and rested 20 minutes before churning — improves body without cooking the powder in.
- Machine used: Cuisinart ICE100 (traditional churn-freeze machine, not Ninja Creami).
