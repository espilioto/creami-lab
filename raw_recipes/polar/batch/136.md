### [No Added Sugar Luxury Triple Chocolate Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=322XErJ4F6I)

Rich, high-solids triple chocolate ice cream — cocoa powder, dark chocolate in the base, plus fudge brownie and fudge swirl add-ins — no added sugar, designed for a traditional ice cream machine (also works in Ninja Creami).

**Ingredients:**

- **600g** whipping cream (33–36% fat)
- **600g** whole milk (3.5% fat)
- **150g** allulose
- **100g** cocoa powder, 20–24% fat (Fry's brand used; "premium cocoa/cacao")
- **50g** dark chocolate, no added sugar (65% cacao, e.g. Lindt), chopped
- **45g** skim milk powder
- **5g** liquid stevia (approx. 1 tsp — to boost perceived sweetness, as allulose is less sweet than sugar)
- **~0.75g** stabilizer blend (Nick's own blend; he suggests asking in comments — not disclosed)

**Add-ins:**

- Fudge brownies (Costco Two-Bite Brownies used — dense, chewy; supermarket brownies work but are lighter)
- Condensed milk fudge pieces (chopped; Nick's own recipe — link in description)
- Soft fudge swirl (Nick's own recipe — link in description)

**Steps:**

1. Add milk to a saucepan. Add allulose and stir to combine.
2. In a separate container, mix skim milk powder with the stabilizer blend; whisk into the cold milk mixture to avoid clumps.
3. Add cocoa powder and liquid stevia directly into the cold liquid. Stir together.
4. Heat the mixture, stirring, to 85°C. Hold at 85°C for 5 minutes (achieves ~2–3% evaporation; don't stress about exact evaporation).
5. Add the chopped dark chocolate to the hot base; stir until fully melted (takes ~30–60 seconds).
6. Add the cold cream (straight from fridge) to the hot base and stir together — this rapidly drops the temperature (to ~41°C immediately).
7. Transfer to a container. Cool in fridge overnight (or use an ice bath first if preferred).
8. Churn in a pre-chilled ice cream machine per machine instructions.
9. Layer base and add-ins (brownie pieces, fudge chunks, fudge swirl) into tubs as you pack them. Freeze until completely hard.

**Notes:**

- This is a high-solids recipe — it scoops firmer than usual even when PAC is balanced to -4°C. Let it temper for 5 minutes before scooping.
- The added dark chocolate increases total solids/density, which improves melt resistance in the bowl.
- Cocoa powder goes directly into cold liquid — Nick does this intentionally despite viewer complaints.
- Stabilizer is Nick's undisclosed proprietary blend (~0.75g total — very low because the base is already high fat).
- Add-in recipes (hard fudge and soft fudge) are linked separately in the video description.
- Also works in the Ninja Creami (note: this was made in a traditional churning machine).
