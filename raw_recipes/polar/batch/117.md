### [Making INTENSE Chocolate Extract at Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LctwRr6RKkg)
DIY chocolate extract by steeping cacao nibs in alcohol — a sugar-free, intensely flavored ingredient for ice cream, baking, and more.

**Ingredients:**
- Organic cacao nibs — enough to fill ~3/5 of a 118ml (4oz) jar
- Vodka, rye whiskey, or rum — 40% ABV minimum, to fill the jar (approx. 70–80ml)

**Steps:**
1. Fill a small jar (118ml / 4oz) roughly 3/5 full with organic cacao nibs.
2. Top off with 40% ABV alcohol (vodka or rye whiskey recommended; rum must be at least 40% ABV).
3. Seal the jar and give it a quick shake.
4. Leave on the counter or in a cupboard for 3–4 weeks minimum; 2 months yields a seriously intense extract.
5. Strain out the cacao nibs and decant the liquid. Keep a fresh batch going continuously.

**Notes:**
- Use organic cacao nibs — non-organic/ultra-processed nibs can produce an artificial, bitter aftertaste.
- Minimum steep: 3–4 weeks. Optimal: 2 months for full intensity.
- Shake the jar daily during steeping for better flavor extraction.
- Cacao nibs will eventually go off if left in the liquid long-term; strain and decant once steeped.
- Yields approx. 50–75ml of finished extract per 118ml jar.
- Use in ice cream bases, hot chocolate, baking, cocktails, or savory dishes.
- This is a **component/technique video** — no specific gram weights given; fill ratios are visual (3/5 nibs, remainder alcohol).
