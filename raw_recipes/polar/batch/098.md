### [MUST TRY Ninja Creami Gingerbread Cookie Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2IsiNj2cpoA)

Chocolate-ginger ice cream base (1 kilo / 2 pints) with homemade chewy gingerbread cookies mixed in.

> **BATCH RECIPE** — makes 2 standard Ninja Creami tubs (1 kg total base).

---

#### Gingerbread Cookies

**Ingredients:**
- **375g** all-purpose flour
- **165g** dark brown sugar, packed
- **4.5g** baking soda
- **8g** ground cinnamon
- **5g** ground ginger
- **1g** ground cloves
- **3g** pink Himalayan salt
- **170g** unsalted butter, room temperature, cut into pieces
- **252g** dark molasses (fancy molasses / dark molasses)
- **30g** milk

**Steps:**
1. In a stand mixer bowl add flour, spices (cinnamon, ginger, cloves), baking soda, salt, and brown sugar. Fit the balloon whisk attachment and mix on low to combine dry ingredients and break up any lumps.
2. Add butter pieces and mix on low until mixture resembles fine sand or a mealy crumb texture.
3. With the mixer running, slowly pour in molasses and milk until a dough forms. Dough will be very sticky.
4. Divide dough into two portions. Press each flat (do not roll into a ball — that generates unnecessary heat), wrap in plastic wrap/cling film, and refrigerate for at least 24 hours (or freeze for 30–45 minutes if short on time).
5. Preheat oven to 180°C / 350°F. Prepare baking sheets lined with parchment paper.
6. Flour the work surface and rolling pin generously (dough is very sticky). Roll out one portion of chilled dough and cut into shapes. Use an offset spatula to gently slide shapes onto the parchment-lined tray rather than picking them up by hand.
7. Bake for exactly 10 minutes. Underbaking = too soft; overbaking = too hard and burnt. Target: chewy with enough structure to hold shape in ice cream.
8. Cool on the tray. Reserve the second half of dough for another use (freezes well).

---

#### Chocolate Ginger Ice Cream Base

**Ingredients (makes 2 pints / ~1 kg):**
- **370g** whole milk
- **40g** cocoa powder (Ghirardelli or Valrhona recommended)
- **165g** dark brown sugar
- **390g** 33% whipping cream
- **35g** skim milk powder
- **~2g** ground ginger (approx. 3/4 tsp)
- **1g** guar gum

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cocoa powder, and brown sugar in a bowl or jug. Stir to begin incorporating cocoa (it resists absorbing liquid cold — submerge it under the surface and stir continuously). Microwave for approximately 1 minute until warmed to ~50–55°C, then stir until fully dissolved and smooth.
2. In a separate container, combine skim milk powder, ground ginger, and guar gum. Stir them together (guar gum disperses easily in liquid but pre-mixing with the powder helps even distribution).
3. Add the dry powder mix to the warm chocolate base and stir to combine.
4. Pour in the cold whipping cream and stir well. The cold cream immediately cools the base.
5. Divide between two Ninja Creami tubs and freeze for at least 6–7 hours (or longer — Nick filmed 3 days later).

---

#### Spinning & Mix-In

**Steps:**
1. Spin on Ice Cream setting (or Gelato — Nick notes these settings perform identically and the distinction is marketing).
2. Break one gingerbread cookie into pieces and press into the centre of the spun pint.
3. Run Mix-In stage.
4. Optionally fold in additional broken cookie pieces after the Mix-In cycle.

---

**Notes:**
- The cookie recipe makes more than needed for 2 pints — freeze the remaining dough half for later use.
- More brown sugar in the cookie dough = softer cookies; less = crispier. Aim for chewy so pieces stay pleasant in the frozen base rather than turning rock-hard.
- Do not roll dough into a ball before chilling — pressing flat avoids generating extra warmth from your hands/rolling.
- Cocoa powder fully absorbs liquid at ~50–55°C; microwaving the milk-cocoa-sugar mix briefly shortcuts this process versus a long cold stir.
- Ice Cream and Gelato settings on the Ninja Creami are functionally the same — use either.
- Gingerbread premix packets from the supermarket can substitute for the homemade cookie dough if desired.
