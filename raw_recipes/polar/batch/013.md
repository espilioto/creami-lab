### [Amazing Häagen-Dazs Belgian Chocolate Using Condensed Milk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_unavailable_see_info_json)

Simplified third take on the Häagen-Dazs Belgian Chocolate dupe — uses sweetened condensed milk for ease, churned in a traditional ice cream machine with two types of Callebaut milk chocolate folded in at the end.

**Note: Batch recipe — traditional ice cream machine, not Ninja Creami.**

**Ingredients:**

- **325g** milk (2% fat)
- **400g** cream (33% fat)
- **380g** sweetened condensed milk
- **40g** dextrose (or corn syrup — same amount)
- **40g** Seven Hills organic cocoa powder
- **~50–80g** Callebaut milk chocolate block (~45% cocoa solids) — for mix-ins (grated shavings + chopped chunks)

**Steps:**

1. Combine cream and milk in a pan over low heat. Add dextrose and cocoa powder. Whisk to combine.
2. Open the condensed milk and pour the entire can in. Whisk continuously for 4–5 minutes until everything is fully emulsified — make sure the condensed milk has no streaks and is completely incorporated.
3. Remove from heat. Pour into a container and chill overnight in the fridge.
4. Prepare chocolate mix-ins: using a Callebaut milk chocolate block, grate half into fine shavings and chop the other half into slightly larger chunks. Place both in separate containers in the freezer.
5. Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine.
6. About 2 minutes before churning is complete, add all the frozen chocolate shavings and chunks. Let the machine combine for the remaining time.
7. Load into tubs. Freeze 2–3 hours to harden completely before serving.

**Notes:**

- Seven Hills organic cocoa powder is essential — Nick has tried many cocoa powders and none replicate the Häagen-Dazs flavor profile as closely. Worth tracking down specifically for this recipe.
- Dextrose and corn syrup are interchangeable at the same weight — use whichever is easier to find.
- No cooking to a specific temperature needed — just warm enough to fully dissolve and emulsify the condensed milk with the other ingredients.
- Two chocolate textures (fine shavings + larger chunks) give contrast in every bite. Both must be frozen before adding to the churner.
