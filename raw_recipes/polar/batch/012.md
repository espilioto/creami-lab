### [Amazing Häagen-Dazs Coffee Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGqM09J_Jdc)

A cold-process (no-cook) high-fat coffee ice cream made to match — and beat — Häagen-Dazs' iconic coffee flavour. Batch recipe (~1000g, traditional ice cream machine).

**Ingredients:**
- **135g** whole milk
- **450g** double cream
- **175g** espresso (approx. 4 espresso pods, strong)
- **130g** sugar
- **40g** dextrose
- **65g** skim milk powder
- **1g** guar gum
- **1g** pink himalayan salt
- Chocolate-covered coffee beans (espresso beans) — for topping

**Steps:**
1. Combine guar gum with 1 tablespoon of the skim milk powder in a small container and mix together — this prevents the gum from clumping when added to the liquid.
2. Add milk, cream, sugar, dextrose, remaining skim milk powder, and the guar gum/milk powder mix into your main container. Stir to begin dissolving the sugars.
3. Add the brewed espresso and stir well to combine everything.
4. Check sugars are dissolved by running a spoon along the bottom — if you feel crystals, keep stirring.
5. Lid on, place in the fridge for 1 hour to allow the skim milk powder to fully hydrate.
6. After 1 hour, remove from the fridge and give a final thorough stir to fully homogenise — reincorporate any skin or layer on top before pouring.
7. Churn in ice cream machine for approximately 15–20 minutes until ready.
8. Scoop into container and top with chocolate-covered coffee beans.
9. Freeze for 4–5 hours to harden.

**Notes:**
- Cold process — only the espresso is hot. Guar gum works without heat, which is why it's chosen here.
- Use strong espresso for pronounced coffee flavour. Filter coffee works too — just make it strong.
- Always stir your base thoroughly before pouring into the machine; lumps from undissolved skin ruin the final texture.
- Guar gum is optional — if skipping it, still include the salt.
- Erythritol ≤ 8% of mix weight keeps crystallisation at bay; xylitol or glycerin help prevent it.
- Decoration: chocolate-covered espresso beans add visual contrast and a flavour hit.
