### [Lemon Meringue Pie Ice Cream (Philly Style, No Eggs)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zV9tAIwk6WU)

Eggless philly-style lemon ice cream base layered with crushed meringue and lemon curd.

**Ingredients:**

- **500g** double/heavy cream
- **250g** full-fat whole milk
- **170g** granulated sugar
- **30g** non-fat milk powder (2 tablespoons — acts as stabilizer)
- **2.5g** vanilla extract (½ tsp — reduced from full 1 tsp to not overpower lemon)
- **1** whole large lemon, zest/rind only

**Mix-ins / assembly:**
- Crushed French meringue (shop-bought used in video)
- Lemon curd (shop-bought used in video; homemade = lemon juice + eggs + butter + sugar)

**Steps:**

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine cream, milk, sugar, milk powder, vanilla extract, and lemon zest.
2. Whisk well until fully combined.
3. Pre-chill ice cream machine (condenser type) for ~10 minutes.
4. Pour mixture into ice cream maker and churn ~30 minutes until soft-serve consistency.
5. In a pre-chilled container, layer: lemon curd on the bottom, then a layer of ice cream, then crushed meringue, then more lemon curd — repeat until container is full.
6. Freeze to harden.

**Notes:**

- Philly style = no eggs; produces a creamy, luxurious texture without a custard base.
- Milk powder is used as a stabilizer to prevent the ice cream going rock solid in the freezer.
- Vanilla is intentionally halved to avoid competing with lemon flavor.
- Lemon zest in the base gives subtle, fresh lemon flavor; lemon curd in the layers provides the punch.
- Meringue must be very dry or it will dissolve into the ice cream. Shop-bought works well.
- Lemon curd can be made at home (lemon juice, eggs, butter, sugar) but shop-bought is fine.
