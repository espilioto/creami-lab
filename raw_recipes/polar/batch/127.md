### [Making Serendipity Friends Ice Cream - Central Perk Coffee Almond Fudge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BsLh_8bE27o)

Custard-based coffee ice cream with Dairymilk fudge swirl and dark chocolate covered almonds, inspired by Serendipity's Friends Reunion tie-in flavour.

**Ingredients — Coffee Ice Cream:**
- **300ml** whole milk
- **300ml** double (heavy) cream
- **150g** granulated sugar
- **3** egg yolks
- **3–4 tsp** instant coffee
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **1/4 tsp** salt

**Ingredients — Dairymilk Fudge:**
- **110g** Cadbury Dairymilk chocolate
- **110g** butter
- **1** egg
- **25g** sugar

**Mix-ins:**
- **50–75g** dark chocolate covered almonds

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cream, sugar, egg yolks, and salt in a saucepan. Heat gently to 82–85°C, stirring continuously — no traditional tempering needed, just heat everything together.
2. Add instant coffee powder and stir to dissolve fully.
3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla extract.
4. Pour into a container and chill in the fridge for 4 hours.
5. Churn the chilled base in your ice cream machine.
6. Make the fudge: melt Dairymilk chocolate and butter together in the microwave. Whisk egg and sugar together, then combine with the chocolate-butter mixture. Refrigerate until needed. Warm slightly before using — it sets firm from the fridge and won't pour cold.
7. Chop the dark chocolate covered almonds to your preference (mix of large and small pieces works well).
8. Layer the churned ice cream into a container, swirling in the fudge and almonds generously. Don't skimp.
9. Freeze for at least a few hours (24 hours recommended) before scooping.

**Notes:**
- The fudge recipe is specifically developed for Cadbury Dairymilk — other chocolate brands may require adjustments to get the right consistency.
- Fudge sets firm in the fridge; microwave briefly to loosen it to a pourable consistency before layering.
- Instant coffee can be swapped for decaf if preferred.
- Scoops well after a full freeze; strong coffee aroma and flavour. Creamy texture despite the egg-based custard method.
- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe (not a no-churn or Ninja Creami recipe).
