### [The Best Ben & Jerry's Caramel Brownie Recipe You Need](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBvbcW-55TY)

Homemade dupe of Ben & Jerry's France-only "Caramel Brownie Party" — caramel ice cream base churned with homemade brownies, caramel swirl, and Oreo cookie pieces.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **335ml** whole milk
- **340ml** cream
- **100g** sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **1–2** egg yolks
- **60g** caramel sauce (from batch below)
- **42g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **1.5g** stabiliser

**Ingredients — Caramel Sauce (makes a batch; 60g used in base):**
- **300g** sugar
- **110g** butter
- **110ml** cream
- **3/4–1 tsp** salt

**Ingredients — Brownies:**
- **110g** butter
- **150g** plain flour (all-purpose)
- **35g** cocoa powder
- **1/2 tsp** baking powder
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **275g** Bournville dark chocolate
- **275g** sugar
- **3** eggs

**Ingredients — Mix-Ins:**
- Oreo cookies (broken into pieces), quantity to taste

**Steps:**
1. **Brownies:** Melt chocolate and butter together in a microwave. Add eggs and sugar; beat well. Fold in dry ingredients (flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt). Pour into a pan and bake at 160°C for 22 minutes. Cool completely, then cut into chunks.
2. **Caramel sauce:** Heat sugar in a pan until fully melted (stir or swirl as preferred). Once caramelised, add butter and combine well. Add cream and stir until smooth. Transfer to a container and reserve.
3. **Ice cream base:** Combine milk, SMP, sugar, dextrose, and egg yolks in a saucepan. Heat while dissolving sugars, then add stabiliser. Continue heating to 80°C. Add 60g of the caramel sauce and mix in. Remove from heat, add cream (this drops the temperature quickly). Transfer to a container and refrigerate until fully cold (up to 4 hours).
4. **Churn** the cold base in an ice cream machine for 15–30 minutes depending on machine.
5. **Layer/fold in** brownie chunks, broken Oreo pieces, and extra caramel sauce swirl during layering into the storage container.
6. Freeze until firm before serving.

**Notes:**
- Ice cream base is adapted from Patrik Svensson's Häagen Dazs salted caramel recipe (icecreamcalc.com), adjusted to Nick's preference.
- Caramel sauce makes more than needed for the base — the remainder is used as a swirl mixed in during layering.
- Any favourite brownie recipe can be substituted for the Bournville-based one above.
- Oreos used for the cookie pieces; any similar sandwich cookie works.
- This recipe is subscriber-suggested (IDKFPV). The flavour was a France-only B&J release at time of filming.
