### [Why Blueberry Ice Cream should be everyone's favorite](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z1T75sEi0Ys)
Cooked blueberry ice cream with a marshmallow fluff swirl, made with frozen blueberries reduced on the hob for concentrated flavor.

**Ingredients:**
- **100g** frozen blueberries
- **300g** 2% milk
- **420g** 33% cream
- **55g** skim milk powder
- **35g** corn syrup
- **130g** sugar
- **1.5g** ice cream stabilizer (Special Ingredients brand, off-the-shelf)
- Store-bought marshmallow fluff (quantity to taste, layered as mix-in)

**Steps:**
1. Simmer the frozen blueberries on the hob, crushing them down with a silicone spoon, until the excess water boils off and the mixture reduces to a reasonably viscous syrup.
2. Combine milk, cream, skim milk powder, corn syrup, and sugar in a pot. Pre-mix the stabilizer into the sugar before adding.
3. Heat the base on the hob, stirring continuously (the corn syrup will clump at the bottom initially — keep stirring).
4. Once the base is coming up to temperature, add the blueberry reduction and stir to combine.
5. Continue heating to 85°C, then remove from heat.
6. Chill the base completely in the fridge, then whisk to homogenize.
7. Churn in a pre-chilled ice cream machine for 15–20 minutes until ready.
8. Layer into the container: scoop of ice cream, spoonful of marshmallow fluff, repeat until full. Freeze overnight (minimum).

**Notes:**
- Frozen blueberries work perfectly — the reduction step boils off the excess freeze-water and concentrates flavor.
- The stabilizer is pre-mixed into the sugar to ensure even dispersion before adding to the liquid base.
- Store-bought marshmallow fluff is very thick and forms more of a core than a true swirl — warm it slightly if you want to swirl it through properly.
- The blueberries produce a fully natural purple color — no artificial colors needed.
- Result: smooth, easily scoopable straight from the freezer, no discernible ice crystals.
