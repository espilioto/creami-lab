### [Amazing Biscoff Ice Cream in Ninja Creami](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sn76CZ2Xks4)

Simple no-stabilizer Biscoff ice cream using condensed milk, cream, and Biscoff spread — balanced with extra milk to bring fat% down from an initial 24%.

**Ingredients:**
- **286g** whipping cream (30–35% fat)
- **218g** milk (any fat %)
- **350g** condensed milk (1 × 300ml can)
- **156g** Biscoff spread (Lotus)

**Steps:**
1. Weigh cream, milk, and condensed milk directly into the Ninja Creami pint.
2. Add 156g Biscoff spread.
3. Stir thoroughly until the spread is fully incorporated — it will resist at first when hit by cold liquid, keep stirring until smooth.
4. Lid on, freeze until solid (Nick notes this one took a while due to high fat content and warm ambient temp — allow extra time in summer).
5. Spin on Ice Cream program.

**Notes:**
- No stabilizer needed — high fat content provides structure.
- The caramel notes from condensed milk complement Biscoff well; Nick chose condensed milk specifically for this.
- Recipe started at ~24% fat; the extra milk brings it down to a workable range.
- Spinning removes all crunchiness from the spread — result is ultra-smooth. Add Biscoff cookie crumble as mix-in after spinning if you want texture back.
- Biscoff contains cinnamon — expect a subtle cinnamony undertone in the finished ice cream.
- Works in any ice cream machine as a standard churn recipe too.
