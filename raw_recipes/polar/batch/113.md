### [Making Häagen Dazs Caramel Cone Explosion ice cream at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24oLvxorIEY)

Caramel ice cream with almond praline (brittle) and milk-chocolate-covered waffle cone pieces — homemade dupe of Häagen Dazs Caramel Cone Explosion (Canada/US).

**Ingredients:**

_Ice cream base:_
- **500g** whole milk
- **400g** cream
- **30g** sugar
- **5g** light brown sugar
- **50g** dextrose
- **55g** SMP (skim milk powder)
- **1.1g** stabilizer (optional)

_Caramel:_
- **100g** sugar
- **30g** water (approx. 2 tbsp)
- **50g** cream
- **50g** butter

_Almond praline / brittle:_
- **100g** sugar
- **30g** water
- **200g** blanched almonds (toasted)

_Chocolate-covered waffle cone pieces:_
- **100g** milk chocolate
- **10g** neutral / refined coconut oil (deodorized — no coconut flavor)
- Waffle cone pieces (quantity to taste)

**Steps:**

1. **Caramel:** Combine 100g sugar and 30g water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil — no stirring, only swirling. Once it turns a light amber and starts darkening, remove from heat. Carefully add 50g cream and 50g butter (mixture will bubble vigorously — cold butter causes more splatter). Stir until fully combined and smooth. Set aside.
2. **Ice cream base:** Warm 500g milk in a pan. Add sugars (with stabilizer mixed in) and dextrose; heat to 30–50°C. Add SMP and stir until fully dissolved. Pour in the prepared caramel; stir well and keep at 50–60°C until caramel is fully incorporated. Remove from heat, add 400g cream, stir, then refrigerate until fully chilled.
3. **Almond praline:** Spread blanched almonds on a baking tray; toast at 175°C for 15–20 minutes. Meanwhile, cook 100g sugar and 30g water in a pan until golden amber. Remove almonds from oven; roughly break them into smaller pieces (blender pulse, rolling pin, or chopping). Stir almond pieces into the hot caramel; cook briefly if deeper color is desired. Pour onto a silicone baking mat, spread flat, and cool completely. Once set, smash into small chunks.
4. **Chocolate-covered waffle cone pieces:** Melt 100g milk chocolate in the microwave. Stir in 10g deodorized coconut oil until smooth. Break waffle cones into pieces and either dip individually or toss all pieces in the chocolate, then spread on a tray/pan. Refrigerate until set, then transfer to freezer until needed.
5. **Churn:** Take chilled base from fridge, give it a final stir, and churn in ice cream machine. In the final minutes of churning, add almond praline chunks (if machine is strong enough; otherwise fold in by hand after churning). Add chocolate-covered waffle cone pieces for the last few seconds. Turn off machine, transfer to tub, freeze until firm.

**Notes:**

- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe (not Ninja Creami). Yield is larger than a single pint.
- The stabilizer (1.1g) is optional — Nick lists it but does not specify the blend.
- Use deodorized / refined coconut oil for the chocolate coating — virgin coconut oil will impart a coconut flavor.
- Nick notes the caramel separates more easily with warm butter, though cold butter causes less violent bubbling — trade-off to consider.
- Caramel cone pieces are messy to coat individually; tossing all pieces in the chocolate at once and setting as a slab is the cleaner method.
- Keep finished waffle cone pieces in the freezer right up until mix-in time to prevent sogginess.
- If using a lower-power ice cream machine, fold in praline and cone pieces by hand after churning rather than adding during the last spin.
