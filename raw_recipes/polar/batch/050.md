### [Gingerbread Cookie Dough Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WKFcEm3BQ3E)
A 12% fat custard base flavored with molasses and warm spices, folded with soft edible gingerbread cookie dough.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **458g** whole milk (3.25%)
- **264g** heavy cream (33–35%)
- **65g** egg yolks
- **115g** sugar
- **17g** glucose syrup or corn syrup (golden syrup also works)
- **45g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **26g** molasses (not blackstrap — too bitter)
- **6g** ground ginger
- **4g** cinnamon
- **1g** nutmeg
- **0.5g** ground cloves
- **1g** salt
- **10g** vanilla extract

**Ingredients — Gingerbread Cookie Dough:**
- **100g** heat-treated all-purpose flour (plain flour)
- **40g** soft butter
- **50g** brown sugar
- **25g** molasses
- **10g** milk (or gingerbread syrup, e.g. Monin/Fabri, to boost flavor)
- **2g** ground ginger
- **1g** cinnamon
- **0.3g** nutmeg
- **0.1g** ground cloves (optional)
- **0.5g** salt
- **2g** vanilla extract

**Steps:**
1. Heat-treat the flour: spread on a baking sheet and bake at 70°C (175°F) for about 10 minutes until the internal temperature reaches at least 70°C. Stir occasionally. Let it cool slightly.
2. Make the cookie dough: cream together butter and brown sugar by hand until combined (sieve the brown sugar first if lumpy). Add molasses, salt, and vanilla extract. Mix in the spices. Add the heat-treated flour and mix. Add milk (or gingerbread syrup) a little at a time until the dough comes together with a soft, chewable texture — not too dry, not too wet.
3. Turn the dough out onto a silicone mat, flatten/roll to even thickness, and shape as desired. Chill in the freezer or refrigerator until firm enough to handle, then cut into chunks.
4. Make the custard base: combine milk, corn syrup, egg yolks, sugar, skim milk powder, molasses, spices, salt, and vanilla extract in a saucepan. Heat over medium, stirring constantly to prevent the egg yolks from sticking and scrambling on the bottom.
5. Cook the custard until it thickens (nappe consistency). Add cream. Transfer to a container and refrigerate for about 1 hour to chill.
6. Churn the base in an ice cream maker until frozen.
7. Fold in gingerbread cookie dough chunks during or after churning. Pack into a container and freeze.

**Notes:**
- Nick films a double batch in the video — all amounts above are the single (standard) batch from the description.
- Do not use blackstrap molasses in the base — it is too bitter; save it for savory dishes.
- The cookie dough is formulated to stay soft and chewy straight from the freezer, not rock-hard.
- Spice levels are flexible — increase ginger for more heat, increase cinnamon for a pumpkin-spice-adjacent profile.
- Vanilla bean powder (optional) can be added to the base alongside vanilla extract for a deeper vanilla note without affecting the recipe balance.
