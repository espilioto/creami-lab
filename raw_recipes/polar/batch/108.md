### [Making Ben & Jerry's: Salted Caramel Brownie Topped ice cream at home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xaKN8JOv4p0)

Ben & Jerry's Salted Caramel Brownie Topped dupe: vanilla sweet cream base (from the B&J book) with brownie chunks, salted caramel swirl, and a crackle chocolate top.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **500ml** double (heavy) cream or whipping cream
- **250ml** whole milk
- **2** large eggs
- **150g** sugar
- Vanilla extract (Nielsen-Massey brand used)

**Ingredients — Brownies:**
- **110g** butter
- **275g** dark chocolate
- **275g** sugar
- **3** large eggs
- **150g** plain flour
- Baking powder (pinch)
- Salt (pinch)
- **3 tbsp** high-quality cocoa powder

**Ingredients — Salted Caramel Sauce:**
- Jude's salted caramel sauce (store-bought, UK brand) or homemade (see: https://www.livewellbakeoften.com/homemade-salted-caramel-sauce/)

**Ingredients — Chocolate Crackle Top:**
- **100g** dark chocolate (~70%)
- **1 tsp** coconut oil

**Steps:**

1. **Chocolate shapes (optional mix-in):** Melt chocolate in a mold for decorative shapes. Use 50–70% dark chocolate. Set in freezer.
2. **Brownies:** Melt butter and dark chocolate together in the microwave. Add sugar and 3 eggs; stir well until fully incorporated. In a separate (large) bowl combine flour, baking powder, salt, and cocoa powder. Add the chocolate batter and stir thoroughly — avoid flour explosion. Pour into a lined 8x8 baking pan (or whatever size you have). Bake at 170°C for 30 minutes. Cool completely, then cut into chunks.
3. **Ice cream base:** Whisk 2 eggs in a bowl, then slowly add sugar while whisking until fully incorporated. Add milk, cream, and vanilla extract; whisk until combined. Churn in an ice cream maker for 20–30 minutes until soft-serve consistency.
4. **Load the tub:** Layer in brownie chunks and chocolate shapes, then swirl in salted caramel sauce. Freeze for ~30 minutes to set the layers.
5. **Crackle top:** Melt 100g dark chocolate with 1 tsp coconut oil in the microwave (~30 seconds, stir well). Working quickly, pour over the surface of the ice cream. Return immediately to freezer. Freeze for at least a couple of hours (ideally overnight — Nick froze for ~24 hours before serving).
6. **Serve:** Break through the crackle top layer with a spoon.

**Notes:**
- Base is Ben & Jerry's "Sweet Cream Base #1" from their official book — described as light in texture; Nick notes he personally prefers a richer, higher-body base.
- Recipe uses raw eggs — use pasteurized eggs if preferred (can be pasteurized at home).
- Caramel sauce: Jude's is a UK brand. The linked homemade recipe is recommended as an easy alternative.
- The crackle top works because coconut oil lowers the melting point of chocolate, causing it to set hard on cold ice cream.
- Nick's wife did the tub decoration.
