### [Beautiful Costco Salted Caramel Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ZVcWs7Xsh0)

Rich, creamy gelato built around Costco's Salted Butter Caramel sauce — just four ingredients, no extra stabilizers needed.

**Ingredients:**

- **225g** Costco Salted Butter Caramel sauce (French, from Costco — contains sugar, glucose syrup, condensed milk, cream, carrageenan, butter, salt)
- **600g** milk (2% semi-skimmed or whole)
- **250g** cream, 33–36% fat (double cream also works — slightly higher fat)
- **30g** skim milk powder (SMP)

**Mix-in / garnish (optional):**

- Valrhona Dulcey 35% blonde chocolate (caramelised white/milk hybrid), melted and drizzled — or substitute Caramac or any caramel-flavoured chocolate

**Steps:**

1. Combine the milk, cream, and skim milk powder in a bowl. Whisk together until the powder is dissolved.
2. Warm the Costco caramel sauce briefly in the microwave (~1 minute on high, repeat if needed) until it is fluid enough to blend into the dairy.
3. Add the warmed caramel to the dairy mixture and stir/whisk until fully incorporated and uniform caramel colour throughout.
4. Pour the base into Ninja Creami pints and freeze overnight (minimum).
5. Spin on Gelato or Ice Cream setting (Nick notes the speed is identical — either works).
6. Optional mix-in: use a spoon to hollow a well in the centre of the spun pint, pour in melted Dulcey/caramel chocolate, then re-spin or fold in.
7. Dish up and garnish with extra chocolate drizzle or flakes if desired.

**Notes:**

- No additional stabilizer required — the caramel sauce already contains carrageenan, and the cream also has carrageenan. Adding more is unnecessary.
- Serving temperature: -14°C / 6.8°F (warmer than typical ice cream — allow a couple of minutes out of the freezer).
- Nick's reported metrics: 7.5% fat, 20% sugars, 9.1% MSNF, 37% solids, 64% water.
- If Costco caramel is unavailable, a Caramac bar or any caramel-flavoured chocolate melted into the base would be a good substitute.
- Nick used the Ninja Creami OG (not Deluxe) in this video — behaviour is the same on Deluxe.
