### [Beautiful Custard Apple Gelato - Chirimoya, Anone, Sitafal, Srikaya](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6-7oHRYljU8)

Cold-process custard apple (atamoya) gelato — all ingredients blended raw and spun on the Ice Cream setting.

**Ingredients:**

- **375g** custard apple flesh (atamoya/cherimoya), strained and deseeded (~3 ripe fruit)
- **321g** whole milk
- **254g** whipping cream / heavy cream (33%+ fat)
- **128g** sugar
- **45g** glucose syrup (corn syrup or golden syrup work)
- **55g** skim milk powder
- **2g** guar gum

**Steps:**

1. Allow fruit to ripen at room temperature until soft to the touch (like a ripe avocado) — 3–4 days from firm.
2. Halve the fruit, remove all seeds, and scoop out only the soft creamy white flesh. Discard the outer skin/shell.
3. Pre-mix the guar gum with the sugar to break up any clumps before blending.
4. Add all ingredients (fruit flesh, milk, cream, sugar mixture, glucose syrup, skim milk powder) into a blender.
5. Blend on low for 1 minute, then on high for 1–2 minutes until completely smooth.
6. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve to remove any remaining seed fragments or fibrous particles.
7. Pour into the Ninja Creami pint container and freeze for at least 24 hours.
8. Process on the Ice Cream setting.

**Notes:**

- Atamoya is a hybrid of cherimoya and sugar apple — sweeter and creamier than either parent. Cherimoya, sitaphal (sitafal), anon, and srikaya are all related varieties and can be used interchangeably.
- Sugar content of the fruit is ~14g per 100g (similar to mango) — factor this in if adjusting sweetener levels.
- Always wait for full ripeness before using — underripe fruit will lack sweetness and the characteristic custard texture.
- Glucose syrup prevents ice crystal formation and improves scoopability.
- Mixing guar gum into the sugar before adding to the blender prevents clumping.
- Flavor profile: tropical, creamy, with notes of pineapple, green apple, banana, and a hint of papaya.
- Result was described as incredibly smooth texture with a very clean, delicate flavor.
