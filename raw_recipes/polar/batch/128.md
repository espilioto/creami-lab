### [Stark Raving Hazelnuts Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C7--9U5Inz4)

Marvel-inspired luxury hazelnut ice cream with whiskey caramel sauce and 24ct gold leaf — a custard base churned in a traditional ice cream machine, not a Ninja Creami. Batch size: ~1.5L.

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base (22.3% fat, MSNF 8%, Sugar 16.1%, Total Solids 46%):

- **500ml** milk (1.8% fat)
- **600ml** double cream (heavy cream, 49% fat)
- **6** egg yolks
- **220g** dark muscovado sugar
- **40g** SMP (skimmed milk powder, added after chilling)
- **1** Tahitian vanilla bean + seeds (large)
- **1.5 tbsp** Frangelico (hazelnut liqueur — optional)
- **200g** chopped Piedmont hazelnuts

Whisky Caramel Sauce:

- **200g** granulated sugar
- **60ml** water
- **120ml** double cream (heavy cream)
- **2 tbsp** Laphroaig whisky (10-year)
- pinch of salt

Decoration:

- **1 sheet** 24ct gold leaf per serving (80x80mm sheets)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk, double cream, muscovado sugar, and egg yolks in a suitable pan. Gently heat to 77°C — do not boil.
2. Add vanilla seeds and the pod. Add Frangelico. Cover and refrigerate to chill the base.
3. Remove vanilla pod from chilled base, then stir in skimmed milk powder. Let hydrate for 20 minutes.
4. Pour base into ice cream machine and churn until done.
5. Layer churned ice cream with chopped Piedmont hazelnuts and whiskey caramel swirl, then freeze for at least 4 hours before serving.
6. For the whisky caramel: heat water and sugar together without stirring until desired amber colour is reached. Remove from heat, add double cream mixture, then add a pinch of salt and the Laphroaig whisky. Transfer to a heatproof container.
7. To serve: scoop, drape whisky caramel over the top, and finish with one sheet of gold leaf per portion.

**Notes:**

- This is a traditional churned custard recipe (ice cream machine required), not a no-churn or Ninja Creami recipe.
- Piedmont (Piemonte) hazelnuts are considered the premium choice; the description notes these specifically over generic hazelnuts.
- Tahitian vanilla is preferred here — noted as widely considered the best vanilla variety.
- Frangelico can be omitted without significantly affecting the base texture.
- Laphroaig 10-year Scotch whisky is the specified spirit for the caramel — its smoky/peaty character is intentional.
- Gold leaf options mentioned: gold spray, gold glitter, gold powder, or leaf — gold leaf recommended for best visual effect.
- Total cost estimate: ~£92.81 per 1.5L batch (assuming no ingredients on hand, including full bottles of booze).
- Batch is large enough to justify buying full bottles of alcohol.
