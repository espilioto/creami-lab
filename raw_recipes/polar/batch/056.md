### [Delicious Strawberries n Cream Gelato](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKJCBh83LS0)

Fresh strawberry gelato cooked down with sugar and corn syrup into a rich invert syrup base, churned in a traditional ice cream machine or Ninja Creami.

**Ingredients:**
- **200g** fresh strawberries
- **115g** sugar
- **55g** corn syrup (or glucose syrup)
- **195g** 2% milk
- **386g** 33% whipping cream
- Skim milk powder (amount adjusted — original had too much; see Notes)
- **1.2g** stabilizer (optional — off-the-shelf generic blend; guar gum, xanthan gum, or a combination blend works)

**Steps:**
1. Hull and quarter the strawberries (smaller pieces cook faster).
2. Combine strawberries, sugar, and corn syrup in a pan over high heat. Stir and cook 10–15 minutes until strawberries fully break down into a thick, foaming syrup.
3. Remove from heat and allow to cool somewhat.
4. In a separate bowl, combine milk, cream, and milk powder (with stabilizer mixed into the powder if using a hot-process stabilizer).
5. Whisk the dairy mixture, then slowly pour in the warm strawberry syrup, scraping out all the syrup from the pan.
6. If using a hot-process stabilizer, heat the combined mixture back up to 85°C, stirring continuously. Remove from heat.
7. Transfer to a bowl and chill in the fridge for 3–4 hours until fully cold.
8. Blend briefly (30–60 seconds) to break up any remaining fruit chunks (optional but recommended — large fruit pieces go icy).
9. Churn in a pre-chilled ice cream machine for approximately 12–15 minutes.
10. Pack into tubs and freeze.

**Notes:**
- Corn syrup / glucose creates invert sugar during cooking, which inhibits recrystallization and gives a smooth, creamy texture — this largely replaces the need for a stabilizer in this recipe.
- Strawberries are ~95% water, so milk quantity is kept low to compensate.
- Stabilizer is optional here; the invert sugar does significant stabilizing work on its own.
- Nick adjusted the milk powder quantity down from the original (original video had too much) — use a conservative amount.
- Take out of the freezer a few minutes before serving to soften enough to scoop — sugar level was intentionally reduced slightly to let strawberry flavor shine, so the gelato sets firmer.
- Works in both a traditional ice cream machine and Ninja Creami.
