### [Keto Chocolate Caramel Ice Cream (Rolo-inspired)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFnXQi_WndE)

Keto/sugar-free chocolate ice cream with a homemade allulose caramel swirl — churned in a traditional ice cream machine, layered into tubs.

**Ingredients — Ice Cream Base:**
- **390g** heavy cream (33% fat)
- **197g** water
- **107g** allulose
- **36g** inulin
- **25g** cocoa powder (Nick uses deZaan True Gold)
- **2g** tara gum

**Ingredients — Allulose Caramel:**
- **80g** allulose
- **28g** butter, room temperature
- **45g** heavy/whipping cream, room temperature
- **1/4 tsp** vanilla extract
- **1/2 tsp** flaky sea salt

**Steps:**
1. Pre-mix tara gum with the allulose to prevent clumping.
2. Add cream, water, and allulose/tara gum mixture to a pan. Heat, stirring, until ~50°C.
3. Add cocoa powder and stir until fully dissolved and homogenized.
4. Bring base to 80–85°C, then remove from heat.
5. Transfer to a container and chill in the fridge.
6. Make the caramel: add allulose and butter together into a pan over medium heat. Do not stir — let melt and start to brown on its own, swirling the pan as needed.
7. When a rich caramel color is reached, turn off heat. Carefully add room-temperature cream (mixture may spit). Stir until combined.
8. Let caramel cool off-heat until no longer bubbling, then stir in vanilla extract and a pinch of flaky sea salt. Refrigerate alongside the ice cream base.
9. Once base is fully chilled, stir to remove lumps and pour into a pre-chilled ice cream machine (pre-run for 10 minutes). Churn until soft-serve consistency.
10. Layer ice cream and caramel into tubs: one scoop of ice cream, one drizzle of caramel, repeat until tubs are full.
11. Freeze for at least 24–48 hours before serving.

**Notes:**
- This is a **traditional churned recipe** (not a Ninja Creami recipe) — yields multiple tubs.
- Allulose is critical for both the base and caramel. It has strong freezing-point depression — measure precisely; 20–30g too much will prevent the base from freezing.
- Avoid erythritol for caramel — it recrystallizes almost every time. Allulose or monk fruit work best.
- Flaky/high-quality sea salt is recommended over table salt — different character and flavor impact.
- Nick uses deZaan True Gold cocoa powder — milky chocolate profile (~30–35% cocoa solid equivalent), dissolves easily. Any quality cocoa works.
- Nick reports 9.5g net carbs per tub (500ml), 4.5g net carbs per serving (half tub).
- Base macros per description: 18.8% fat, 0 sugar, 3% MSNF, 43.2% solids.
