### [DIY Toffifee Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pix2I3t9FoA)
Hazelnut milk chocolate and dulce de leche ice cream inspired by Toffifee sweets — simple off-the-shelf ingredients, churned base with swirl layering.

**Ingredients:**
- **300ml** milk
- **400ml** double (heavy) cream
- **180g** sugar
- **1-2 tbsp** dulce de leche (plus more reserved for the swirl)
- **100g** hazelnut milk chocolate (brand used: Lindt; any supermarket variety works)
- **2 tbsp** milk powder

**Steps:**
1. Combine milk, cream, and sugar in a saucepan. Heat gently and stir in the dulce de leche until fully incorporated.
2. Transfer the base to a larger vessel to avoid overflow during churning. Chill in an ice bath or the fridge.
3. Once chilled, add the milk powder, leave for 20 minutes, then mix again before churning.
4. Chop the hazelnut milk chocolate and keep it chilled in the fridge or freezer until needed.
5. Churn the base. As this is a high-sugar recipe, allow extra churn time and avoid overfilling the machine.
6. Once churned, layer the ice cream with the remaining dulce de leche and the chopped hazelnut chocolate.
7. Freeze overnight before serving.

**Notes:**
- High sugar content means the mix takes longer to freeze and churn — don't overfill the machine.
- Use a larger pan than you think you need when heating the base; the mixture is prone to spilling.
- Lindt hazelnut milk chocolate is recommended but any hazelnut milk chocolate works.
- Reserve most of the dulce de leche for the swirl layer — only 1-2 tbsp goes into the base.
- This recipe was originally created for a community member (Munira @ I Scream SG) who popularised it in Singapore.
