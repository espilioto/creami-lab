### [Kinder Bueno Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MQgLlh7SQu4)
Hazelnut ice cream base layered with homemade hazelnut butter, a white chocolate hazelnut cream topping, and a milk chocolate stracciatella swirl — a full from-scratch Kinder Bueno recreation. Batch recipe (makes ~2 litres).

**Ingredients:**

Ice Cream Base:
- **830g** milk
- **740g** whipping cream (33% fat)
- **220g** sugar
- **60g** corn syrup (or glucose syrup — not high-fructose corn syrup)
- **120g** skim milk powder (SMP)
- **3.5g** stabilizer (optional)
- **5–10g** hazelnut extract (5g if strong, 10g if weak — add after heating)

Hazelnut Butter:
- **100g** hazelnuts (roasted, skins removed)
- **175g** white chocolate (e.g. Callebaut), melted

Hazelnut Cream:
- **130g** white chocolate (melted)
- **5g** hazelnut extract
- **5g** corn syrup (or dextrose/corn sugar)
- **~10g** refined (deodorized) coconut oil

Milk Chocolate Stracciatella:
- **100g** milk chocolate
- **10g** refined coconut oil (or any unflavored oil)

**Steps:**

1. Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C). Spread hazelnuts on a small pan and roast 10–15 minutes until skins crack and crack. Transfer hot nuts to a lidded container, shake vigorously to remove skins. Allow to cool slightly; don't stress over a few stubborn skins.
2. Combine milk, cream, sugar, corn syrup, and skim milk powder in a saucepan. Mix stabilizer with a little sugar first (prevents clumping), then add to the pan. Heat over medium, stirring, until it reaches 75–80°C (stabilizer activation temperature).
3. Remove from heat. Transfer base to a container and refrigerate 3–4 hours. Add hazelnut extract only after the base has chilled (not during heating).
4. While the base chills, make the hazelnut butter: blend the warm skinned hazelnuts into a paste. Add the melted white chocolate (175g) and blend until fully smooth. Refrigerate.
5. Make the hazelnut cream: melt 130g white chocolate with ~10g refined coconut oil in the microwave in 10-second bursts, stirring between each, until smooth. Add 5g hazelnut extract and 5g corn syrup. Stir to combine. Refrigerate. (Coconut oil prevents the chocolate from seizing and helps it stay pourable.)
6. Make the milk chocolate stracciatella: melt 100g milk chocolate with 10g refined coconut oil in the microwave until smooth. Keep warm/fluid for churning.
7. Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine. In the final 1–2 minutes of churning, pour in half (~50g) of the milk chocolate mixture — it will shatter into flakes throughout the ice cream.
8. Layer the churned ice cream into containers: alternate scoops of ice cream with spoonfuls of hazelnut butter throughout the tub until full.
9. Pour the hazelnut cream gently over the top in a thin layer. Drizzle the remaining milk chocolate stracciatella over that. Freeze 4–5 hours to harden.

**Notes:**
- This is a large batch recipe — divide everything by two for a smaller yield.
- Stabilizer is optional but recommended for storage longevity; it keeps texture quality for longer.
- Hazelnut extracts vary greatly in strength — start at 5g, go up to 10g if needed.
- Use refined (deodorized) coconut oil — not virgin — to avoid coconut flavor in the chocolate elements.
- The hazelnut cream and hazelnut butter need to come up to room temperature before layering so they're pourable.
- Nick notes the white chocolate + hazelnut extract combination closely replicates the Kinder Bueno core filling.
- If you only want to make part of this, even just the ice cream base with the hazelnut cream swirled on top is described as "ice cream heaven."
