### [Low-Sugar Homemade Ice Pops for Kids (Peppa Pig Strawberry Popsicles)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11fvbXJKAUA)

Ultra low-sugar strawberry ice pops made with real fruit, a small amount of corn syrup for texture, and a milk/cream base — shaped in Peppa Pig popsicle moulds for kids.

**Ingredients:**
- **65g** fresh strawberries, quartered
- **60ml** water (1/4 cup — to prevent burning during cooking)
- **6g** corn syrup
- Milk and cream mixture (exact amounts visual-only in video; described as "not a lot of cream")
- Milk powder (amount visual-only in video)
- Allulose (amount visual-only in video)

**Steps:**
1. Add strawberries and water to a pan over medium-high heat.
2. Cook until strawberries soften and break down, stirring with a silicone utensil to help break them up.
3. Turn off the heat and add the corn syrup, then a portion of the milk/cream mixture; stir to homogenize the strawberry flavor into the dairy.
4. Add the remaining milk/cream mixture and stir well.
5. Add the milk powder and allulose; stir for 1–2 minutes until fully dissolved (residual heat is enough).
6. If large strawberry chunks remain, break them up with a blender or potato masher.
7. Pour mixture into popsicle moulds, filling to the top. Put lids on (or cover with cling wrap).
8. Freeze until fully set.

**Notes:**
- Makes 7 popsicles: some moulds are 30g, others 50g (Peppa Pig moulds from Amazon/TEMU).
- Nutrition per 100g: 126 kcal, 7g fat, 5g sat fat, 9g carbs, 8.75g sugar, 4.3g protein. Per 30g pop: ~38 kcal, under 3g sugar, under 3g fat.
- Corn syrup is used specifically for texture and chew in the popsicle; can be omitted for zero sugar, but texture will suffer.
- Exact amounts for milk, cream, milk powder, and allulose are shown visually in the video and not narrated — recipe reconstruction requires watching the video directly.
