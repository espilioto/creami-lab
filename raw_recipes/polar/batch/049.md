### [DELICIOUS S'mores Ice Cream for 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nd2yAk8eWnw)

Toasted-marshmallow ice cream base with buttery graham cracker crumb and milk chocolate stracciatella. Batch recipe (2L / ~4 Ninja Creami Deluxe pints) — halve for 2 pints, quarter for 1 pint.

**Ingredients:**

*Ice cream base:*
- **562g** whole milk
- **857g** 33% whipping cream
- **327g** marshmallows (standard, ~60% sugar — do not substitute low/zero-sugar marshmallows)
- **110g** sugar
- **38g** dextrose (sub: glucose syrup or corn syrup)
- **105g** skimmed milk powder
- No stabilizer (gelatin in marshmallows stabilizes the base)

*Graham cracker crumb:*
- **150g** graham crackers, crushed
- **60g** butter
- **30–40g** golden syrup (sub: honey, corn syrup, or invert sugar)

*Stracciatella:*
- Milk chocolate (Nick used Valrhona + Cacao Barry milk chocolate), quantity to taste
- Small amount of coconut oil (to thin chocolate for drizzling)

**Steps:**

1. Preheat oven to 420°F / 215°C.
2. Spread marshmallows on a baking sheet. Place on the middle shelf and bake until expanded and softened (~8–10 min). Watch closely — every oven differs. Then torch or broil the tops until deeply toasted/charred (this burnt-sugar char is the key s'mores flavor).
3. Meanwhile, combine milk, cream, sugar, dextrose, and skimmed milk powder in a pan over medium heat. Warm to ~50–60°C (until steam rises and all dry ingredients are dissolved — no need to reach 85°C since there is no stabilizer).
4. Working in batches if needed, transfer some warm base into a blender, add the toasted marshmallows (in batches), and blend for ~30 seconds each time until fully emulsified. Combine all blended base together.
5. Chill base completely in the fridge.
6. **Graham cracker crumb:** Melt butter in the microwave. Stir in crushed graham crackers and golden syrup until a buttery, cohesive mix forms. Press into a baking pan and bake at the same oven temp for ~10–12 minutes until the butter and syrup just start to bubble. Do not over-bake. Cool, then chop into small (~1 cm) cubes.
7. Once base is fully chilled, stir well and churn in an ice cream machine.
8. Near the end of churning, drizzle in melted milk chocolate (thinned with a little coconut oil) to form stracciatella flakes.
9. Layer into tubs: ice cream → graham cracker crumb → chocolate drizzle → ice cream → graham cracker crumb → chocolate drizzle. Freeze.

**Notes:**

- Marshmallow sugar content matters: standard marshmallows are ~60% sugar (28g marshmallow = 17g sugar). Low-sugar or zero-sugar marshmallows will result in a rock-hard base — account for this if substituting.
- The char on the marshmallows is intentional and essential. Do not skip toasting to darkness — that is what delivers the s'mores flavor.
- The golden syrup in the crumb creates a chewy (not crumbly) texture in the frozen ice cream. Honey or corn syrup work equally well.
- For Ninja Creami use, make a half or quarter batch. The full marshmallow blend can be done in one blender pass at smaller volumes.
- Nick notes this recipe works equally well in a traditional churn machine.
