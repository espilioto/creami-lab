### [Discovering the Perfect Bounty Ice Cream Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xc7RcXgh89s)

Two failed attempts at a Bounty (Almond Joy) ice cream — iterating toward the right coconut flavor and texture using available UK supermarket ingredients.

---

#### Attempt 1 — Infusion Method (failed)

Original recipe called for frozen grated coconut (unavailable), so creamed coconut was substituted. The high fat content (~65%) of creamed coconut flooded the base with fat, leaving only ~15g of coconut solids after straining — resulting in a powdery, crumbly texture. Flavor was good; texture was not.

**Ingredients:**
- Coconut cream (infused, then strained)
- Creamed coconut (substituted for frozen grated coconut — 100% coconut, ~65% fat)
- Whole milk
- Whipping cream
- Sugar
- Corn syrup
- Proprietary stabilizer and emulsifier mix
- Pinch of salt
- Shredded coconut (added after heating)

**Mix-in / Finish:**
- Milk chocolate stracciatella (milk chocolate + small amount of coconut oil, drizzled at end of churn)

**Steps:**
1. Combine coconut cream, creamed coconut, whole milk, and whipping cream in a saucepan. Heat on the stove until combined.
2. Chill overnight in the fridge.
3. Strain the mix. (Note: most solids were lost — creamed coconut fat fully dissolved into the base.)
4. Add sugar, corn syrup, stabilizer/emulsifier mix, and a pinch of salt. Heat to dissolve sugars.
5. Stir in shredded coconut. Chill the base again until cold.
6. Churn in an ice cream machine.
7. Near the end of churning, drizzle in melted milk chocolate thinned with a small amount of coconut oil (stracciatella technique).

**Result:** Flavor spot-on (Bounty-like), but texture powdery and crumbly due to excessive fat from creamed coconut. Not what was intended.

---

#### Attempt 2 — Revised Base (closer, but flavor off)

Abandoned the infusion approach. Used coconut milk and coconut cream directly in the base without straining.

**Ingredients:**
- **400g** coconut milk @ 18% fat
- **400g** coconut cream @ 22% fat
- **110g** sugar
- **100g** dextrose
- **100g** double cream
- **75g** SMP (skimmed milk powder)

**Steps:**
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well until dissolved.
2. Chill the base until cold.
3. Churn in an ice cream machine.

**Result:** Much better texture — scoops traditionally, smooth. Flavor tasted like "fake coconut" — suspected cause is the specific coconut milk brand used (18% fat). Still not achieving the true Bounty bar flavor. Nick noted more desiccated coconut is also needed for the right Bounty texture/chew.

---

**Notes:**
- Target flavor profile: Bounty bar (UK) / Almond Joy (US) — dense, chewy, desiccated coconut filling covered in milk chocolate.
- Fat content of coconut products varies widely (11%, 15%, 18%, 21%, 22%) and they are not interchangeable — fat level has a major impact on texture.
- Creamed coconut (~65% fat) is unsuitable as a flavor infusion ingredient; it overwhelms the fat balance of the base.
- Frozen grated coconut (the ideal ingredient) was unavailable locally — this whole video is a workaround exploration.
- The stracciatella finish (milk chocolate + coconut oil) in Attempt 1 looked and tasted good — worth keeping in a future version.
- Nick plans to continue testing different coconut milk/cream products until the right Bounty flavor is achieved. More desiccated coconut is also needed for texture.
- This video is explicitly a "discovery in progress" — neither recipe is considered a finished result.
