### [Hazelnut Affogato with SKOR Bar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVNlfrSj6wQ)
Hazelnut half-and-half gelato with chopped SKOR pieces, topped with a hazelnut espresso shot and caramel sauce — served affogato style.

**Ingredients:**

*Hazelnut Gelato Base (made separately — see linked half-and-half gelato video):*
- Half-and-half (equal parts)
- Sweetened condensed milk
- Hazelnut extract (alcohol-based)
- Invert sugar / corn syrup (small amount)

*Mix-In:*
- **SKOR mini bars** — a few, chopped small (Heath Bar is a valid substitute)

*Affogato Assembly:*
- **1 shot** hazelnut espresso (brand: Rosso Cafe)
- **Caramel syrup/sauce** — drizzle to taste

**Steps:**

1. Prepare the hazelnut half-and-half gelato base in advance and freeze for at least 24 hours (see linked video for full base recipe).
2. Chop SKOR mini bars into small pieces.
3. Spin the frozen gelato pint in the Ninja Creami on Gelato.
4. Use a spoon to cut a channel down the middle of the processed pint all the way to the bottom.
5. Fill the channel with chopped SKOR pieces and run Mix-In.
6. Pull a shot of hazelnut espresso.
7. Scoop gelato into a serving bowl or glass.
8. Pour the hazelnut espresso shot directly over the top of the gelato.
9. Finish with a drizzle of caramel syrup.

**Notes:**

- This is an assembly recipe — the gelato base is made in a prior video (hazelnut half-and-half gelato). The base is half-and-half + condensed milk + hazelnut alcohol extract + invert sugar/corn syrup.
- SKOR bar is similar to a Dime bar; it contains toffee with almond pieces and a chocolate coating. Heath Bar is the direct North American equivalent.
- The hazelnut espresso amplifies the hazelnut flavor in the gelato without overpowering it — the coffee bitterness comes through the cream.
- Caramel syrup on top highlights the SKOR bar's toffee-caramel notes.
- A "balanced" base means leftovers can go back in the freezer successfully.
- Nick's tasting note: "I could just eat this for hours" — one of his highest-rated recipes.
