### [How to make Cadbury Ice Cream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-sjbNIYTPWM)

Custard-based chocolate ice cream loaded with Cadbury Dairy Milk, cocoa powder, Fingers, and a Flake — with an optional Mozart chocolate liqueur swirl.

**Ingredients:**

- **500ml** milk
- **600ml** double (heavy) cream
- **6** egg yolks
- **300g** sugar
- **120g** Cadbury Dairy Milk chocolate
- **2 tsp** Cadbury Cocoa Powder (heaped)
- **1** Cadbury Flake
- **1/2 pack** Cadbury Fingers
- **1–2 tbsp** Mozart Chocolate Cream Liqueur (optional)
- Dark chocolate sauce (for layering)

**Steps:**

1. Combine milk and sugar in a pan. Gently heat until sugar dissolves and milk just begins to steam — do not boil.
2. Add 6 egg yolks to a separate bowl. Temper by gradually adding ~5 tablespoons of the hot milk mixture while whisking vigorously, then pour the tempered eggs back into the pan.
3. Keep over low heat, stirring continuously. Add 120g Cadbury Dairy Milk chocolate (broken up) and 2 heaped tsp cocoa powder. Stir until chocolate melts and custard coats the back of a spoon. Pour into a large jug.
4. Stir in 600ml double cream and whisk to combine. This is your chocolate ice cream base.
5. Pour the base into a pre-chilled ice cream maker (pre-chill ~20 minutes if required) and churn until soft-serve consistency — approximately 20 minutes.
6. While churning, slice Cadbury Fingers and Flake into chunks.
7. Once churned, add half the chocolate chunks and the Mozart liqueur (if using) into the machine for a final quick churn to distribute evenly.
8. Spoon half the ice cream into your container, add the remaining chocolate chunks, then top with the rest of the ice cream.
9. Add a generous squirt of dark chocolate sauce and gently swirl with a spoon to create ribbons throughout. Freeze until firm.

**Notes:**

- Tempering tip: if unsure, keep adding an extra spoonful of hot milk to the eggs before returning them to the pan — this prevents scrambling.
- The Mozart Chocolate Cream Liqueur is entirely optional but adds a boozy depth; Cadbury originally made their own chocolate liqueur but discontinued it.
- Cadbury add-ins are flexible — use any favourite Cadbury chocolate (Roses, Buttons, Heroes, etc.) cut into chunks.
- This is a traditional churned custard recipe, not a Ninja Creami recipe.
