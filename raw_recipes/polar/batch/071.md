### [Ben & Jerry's Phish Food (Homemade)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYWdY5TKZtI)
Chocolate ice cream base with marshmallow cream swirl, salted caramel sauce, and molded chocolate fish pieces — a full recreation of the B&J classic.

**Ingredients:**

Ice cream base:
- **80g** sugar
- **2.5g** vanilla extract
- **30g** high-quality cocoa powder
- **30g** non-fat powdered milk

Chocolate fish (mix-ins):
- **100g** 80% dark chocolate
- **1 tbsp** water

Marshmallow cream:
- **2** large egg whites
- **100g** granulated sugar
- **1/4 tsp** cream of tartar
- **1/2 tsp** pure vanilla extract (optional)

Caramel sauce:
- Shop-bought salted caramel sauce (used: Jude's Salted Caramel)

**Steps:**

1. **Chocolate fish:** Combine chocolate and water in a bowl. Microwave in 30–45 second bursts, stirring until melted and smooth. Pour into small silicone molds (fish-shaped if available). Refrigerate until set, then pop out and store in fridge until assembly.
2. **Marshmallow cream (Swiss meringue):** Combine egg whites, sugar, and cream of tartar in a heatproof bowl over a pan of simmering water (double boiler). Stir constantly, heating gently until sugar fully dissolves and mixture reaches 71°C. Remove from heat and whisk with a hand or stand mixer until glossy, smooth, and stiff peaks form. Cover and refrigerate.
3. **Ice cream base:** Combine all base ingredients (sugar, vanilla, cocoa powder, milk) in a saucepan. Gently heat, stirring to dissolve and combine. Allow to cool, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours (24 hours ideal). Before churning, whisk in 2 tbsp non-fat milk powder to boost MSNF (milk solids-non-fat) and improve creaminess.
4. **Churn:** Pour chilled base into ice cream maker and churn 25–35 minutes until soft-serve consistency.
5. **Assembly:** Layer churned ice cream into tub. Dot in chocolate fish pieces, drizzle caramel sauce, and add spoonfuls of marshmallow cream. Gently swirl with a spoon to incorporate without fully mixing. Freeze until firm.

**Notes:**
- The water added to the chocolate prevents it from setting rock-hard in the freezer — this keeps the fish pieces pleasantly soft/fudgy.
- No fish mold? Use any small silicone mold (dinosaurs were used here as a substitute).
- Heating the base breaks down fat molecules; cooling encases them in milk protein, producing a creamier texture.
- Jude's Salted Caramel sauce is recommended; dolce de leche works but has a stronger, distinct flavor.
- The non-fat milk powder is added after chilling, whisked in just before churning — it adds MSNF for body and creaminess.
- This is a traditional churned ice cream recipe (ice cream maker required), not a Ninja Creami recipe.
