#### Raspberry Cheesecake Topped Protein Ice Cream
337 cal | 24g protein | 26g C, 13g F, 24g P | Tags: cheesecake, raspberry, white chocolate
**Source:** Raspberry Cheesecake Topped Protein Ice Cream

**Base:**
- 400g Fat Free High Protein Milk
- 22g (2 tbsp) whipped (or regular) cream cheese
- 40-45g Monk Fruit sweetener
- 5g (1 tsp) vanilla bean paste (or extract)
- A pinch of salt
- 1g (1/4 tsp) xanthan gum

**Mix-ins/Toppings:**
- 28g (1 oz) frozen raspberries (they have to be frozen so they don't introduce any extra liquid to the mix)
- 14g (1/2 a small package) Teddy Grahams (can sub for graham crackers)
- 15g white chocolate chips (60 cal) mixed with 2g of coconut oil (27 cal)

**Steps:**
1. Blend all ingredients for the base and freeze (at least 16 hours)
2. 1-2 hours before you make your ice cream, put the Teddy Grahams in the freezer. This allows them to firm up and withstand the power of the Mix-in setting (so they don't get completely pulverized)
3. Run your pint under hot water for at least 60 seconds
4. Spin on "Ice Cream" setting
5. Spin on "Respin" setting if very powdery
6. Make a hole down to the bottom with a butter knife and add the Teddy Grahams (save 1 or 2 to crumble on top) and raspberries
7. Run the "Mix-in" cycle
8. Combine the white chocolate and coconut oil in a microwave safe dish
9. Microwave the mixture in 30 second increments until it is smooth enough to pour
10. Pour the chocolate mixture over the ice cream
11. Crumble the extra Teddy Grahams over top

- 1g (1/4 tsp) xanthan gum

#### How to Make Hard Ice Cream in the Ninja Creami
**Source:** For my hard ice cream lovers, this is for you

**Key Info:**
- If you want hard, Ben & Jerry's style ice cream, you need to add mix-ins earlier than most people think -- when the texture is still slightly crumbly
- The ideal texture before adding mix-ins should be slightly crumbly. When you scrape down the sides, the ice cream should be slightly melted and hold together just barely
- Run the Mix-in setting at this crumbly stage and it comes out as perfect hard ice cream
- If you want soft serve or blizzard consistency, you can use the mixing/respin setting before adding mix-ins
- For super soft texture, add milk between spins to loosen it up further

#### The Best Way to Prep a Ninja Creami
**Source:** The Best Way to Prep a Ninja Creami

**Key Info:**
- Start by weighing dry ingredients in a separate cup on the scale: sweetener (40-45g), xanthan gum (~0.8g / 1/4 tsp), salt (~0.5-1g), and any dry spices
- Swirl/mix the dry ingredients together first so xanthan gum gets dispersed -- this prevents clumping when added to milk
- Add milk to the pint (400g Fat Free Fairlife), then vanilla bean paste (5g / 1 tsp), then any flavor extracts
- Pour dry ingredients into wet ingredients
- Use an immersion blender (highly recommended over milk frother or hand mixer) -- keep it low in the pint to prevent overflow
- Blend for at least 30 seconds to get fully incorporated
- Cover and put straight into freezer

#### Ninja Creami Quick Start Guide (5 Tips for Beginners)
**Source:** Ninja Creami Quick Start Guide

**Key Info:**
1. **Mix your base really well.** Powders like sweeteners, binding agents, extracts, and paste all sink and clump at the bottom. Use an immersion blender for 30-60 seconds. Hand frother or regular blender also work.
2. **Use a binding agent.** This is huge for texture. Options: xanthan gum (1/4 tsp per pint), guar gum (1/4 tsp per pint), or Jello pudding mix (1 tbsp). These make it thick and creamy instead of icy and chalky.
3. **Freeze for at least 16 hours, ideally 24.** Anything less and it probably won't freeze all the way through -- your ice cream will be super soft and soupy.
4. **Melt the ice crystals before spinning.** Run your pint under hot water for 30-60 seconds, or leave it out on the counter for about 10 minutes. Makes a massive difference in overall texture.
5. **Use the Lite Ice Cream setting** for protein ice cream. It's one of the longest and most powerful settings. Low sugar and low-fat bases freeze much harder and need more processing power. If your base is softer (banana base, higher fat/sugar), the regular Ice Cream setting can work.
- **Bonus:** If it comes out crumbly, do not panic. Just respin it. You do NOT need to add milk in between spins -- adding milk usually makes it too soft. Just respin normally, even if it takes a couple more respins.

#### How to Fix Your Xanthan Gum Clumping Up
**Source:** How to Fix Your Xanthan Gum Clumping Up

**Key Info:**
- Measure all dry ingredients into a separate cup first: sweetener (40-45g), xanthan gum (1/4 tsp / ~1g), salt (1/8 tsp), and any dry spices
- Swirl the dry ingredients together for 2-3 seconds -- this disperses the xanthan gum throughout the other dry ingredients
- Then dump all dry ingredients into the wet ingredients (milk) in the pint
- The xanthan gum won't clump because it's dispersed among the other dry ingredients
- Use an immersion blender (recommended over hand frother) -- it mixes way better, especially with thicker ingredients like nut butters and fruit
- Blend for 30-60 seconds for a perfectly blended pint

#### Ninja Creami 101: Texture (5 Factors)
**Source:** Ninja Creami 101: Texture

**Key Info:**
1. **Ingredients in the base:** More protein or fat = thicker and creamier. Adding protein powder, cream cheese, or peanut butter helps texture. Watery liquids like almond milk or non-dairy milks can make it very icy. Use binding agents (xanthan gum, guar gum) to combat iciness.
2. **Freeze time:** The #1 cause of "too soupy" results. Freeze at least 16 hours in a cold freezer, ideally 24+. If the pint doesn't look completely frozen solid like a block, it's not ready.
3. **Spin settings:** Each setting spins at a different rate and time. For protein ice cream, use a longer setting like Lite Ice Cream or Sorbet. These bases freeze harder due to lower fat/sugar content, so they need more processing power.
4. **Handling:** Run pint under hot water for 30-60 seconds or leave out on counter 5-10 minutes. Goal is to melt ice crystals off the side. But don't do it too long or it leads to a soupy pint.
5. **Mix-ins:** Can ruin a pint if you add something very watery like fresh fruit. Freeze your mix-ins -- they hold up way better. Chop mix-ins to desired size, freeze them, then add as mix-ins.

#### This is KILLING your Ninja Creami Recipes (Jello Pudding Mix vs Xanthan Gum)
**Source:** This is KILLING your Ninja Creami Recipes

**Key Info:**
- Ran a blind taste test comparing: Jello pudding mix vs xanthan gum vs neither
- **Jello pudding mix:** Gives soft, pasty texture -- more like pudding than ice cream. Adds calories and sometimes unwanted flavor. Ingredient list is "insane" -- packed with sugar, corn syrup, artificial flavors, food dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5)
- **Xanthan gum:** Much more ice cream-like texture. Way more firm, chewier, better mouth feel. No flavor and basically zero calories.
- Also tested guar gum vs xanthan gum in another blind test -- xanthan gum came out on top
- For flavor, use real ingredients: cocoa powder, cream cheese, fruit -- not Jello pudding mix flavors
- Recommendation: Use 1/4 teaspoon of xanthan gum in all your recipes

#### 500+ Pints Later: How to Fix Ice Crystals
**Source:** 500+ pints later... here's how to actually fix ice crystals in the Ninja Creami

**Key Info:**
1. **Melt the icy ring:** Run pint under hot water for 30-60 seconds, or leave out on counter for ~10 minutes. The blade never touches the walls, so the ice on the sides doesn't get scraped.
2. **Scrape down sides between spins** with a butter knife. Warning: non-Ninja brand containers might scrape microplastics into your pint.
3. **Use a stabilizer/binding agent:** Xanthan gum, guar gum, or Jello pudding mix. These bind water so pint stays creamy without adding fat.
4. **Don't be afraid of a little fat.** Even 40-50 calories of fat from 1% milk, 2% milk, nut butter, or an egg yolk can turn an icy pint into creamy ice cream.
5. **Use casein-based protein powder** (if using protein powder). For milk, use ultra-filtered milk like Fairlife or A2 milk -- they have less water, more protein, and freeze much smoother than skim milk or plain whey protein.

- **Chocolate Chip Hack:** Combine 15g chocolate chips with 2g coconut oil, melt in microwave (30 sec intervals, stirring between). Pour into hole in center of base before Mix-In cycle. Creates perfect, creamy chocolate chips.
